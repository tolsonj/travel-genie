# Tech & Connectivity

### Connectivity strategy

| Option | Provider | Cost | Coverage |
|--------|----------|-----:|----------|
| **Recommended** | Airalo / Ubigi Japan eSIM | $15–25 / 7 days | Nationwide 4G/5G |
| Alt | Pocket WiFi (Narita/HND pickup) | ¥500–800/day | Unlimited but extra device |
| Roaming | US carrier int'l day pass | $10–12/day | Expensive for 7 days |

**Recommendation:** 5–10 GB eSIM — activate on landing at HND.

### Offline survival kit (download before Jul 31)

| Item | App / file |
|------|------------|
| Tokyo Metro + JR map | Google Maps offline (Tokyo + Kyoto rectangles) |
| Kyoto bus routes | Japan Transit Planner offline packs |
| Japanese translation | Google Translate offline Japanese pack |
| Bookings | PDF confirmations in Files (hotels, Shibuya Sky, Shinkansen, flights) |
| VJW QR | Screenshot in Photos |
| Emergency | US Embassy Tokyo card image |

### Power & plugs

| Topic | Japan |
|-------|-------|
| Plug type | **Type A** (same as US — 2 flat pins) |
| Voltage | 100V (US 120V devices OK) |
| Strategy | No adapter needed; bring 20,000 mAh power bank for long dawn days |

### Digital security

| Topic | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| VPN | Not required — no major site blocks |
| Public WiFi | Avoid for banking; use eSIM |
| Hotel WiFi | Generally safe; use HTTPS |
| Document backup | iCloud/Google Drive encrypted folder |

### Payment apps

| App | Works in Japan? |
|-----|-----------------|
| Apple Pay (Suica) | **Yes** — essential |
| Google Pay | Yes at chains |
| Venmo/Zelle | No |
| PayPay | Local QR — optional if you get Japanese SIM |
| Credit cards | Visa/MC at hotels, department stores, Shinkansen |
