# Hidden Gems

### Tokyo hidden gems (3+ per hub)

| Gem | Area | Day fit | Why locals love it |
|-----|------|---------|-------------------|
| Yanaka Ginza shotengen | Yanaka | Aug 2 eve backup | Old Tokyo shitamachi — cat town, no high-rises |
| Nezu Shrine azalea garden | Nezu (near Ueno) | Aug 1–2 | Mini-Fushimi torii without crowds |
| Kagurazaka cobblestone lane | Kagurazaka | Aug 3 eve alt | French-Japanese fusion neighborhood; evening lanterns |
| Harmonica Yokocho | Kichijoji | Off-route | Tiny alley bars — skip unless reroute |
| Todoroki Valley | Setagaya | Off-route | Urban ravine — 45 min from Ueno, save for return trip |

### Kyoto hidden gems

| Gem | Area | Day fit | Why locals love it |
|-----|------|---------|-------------------|
| Philosopher's Path (early) | Higashiyama | Aug 6 backup | Canal walk — best before 8 AM |
| Otagi Nenbutsu-ji | Arashiyama west | Aug 6 add-on | 1,200 moss-covered rakan statues — far fewer tourists |
| Nishiki Tenmangu shrine | Nishiki | Aug 6 | Tiny shrine inside market — locals pray before shopping |
| Pontocho alley | Downtown | Aug 5 post-dinner | Atmospheric lane along Kamogawa — no reservation needed to walk |
| Shimogamo Shrine forest | North Kyoto | Off-route | Primeval forest approach — needs extra half-day |
