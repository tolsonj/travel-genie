# Customs & Borders

## Reasoning

**Single country, no land borders:** All "crossings" are airport entry (HND Aug 1) and exit (KIX Aug 7). Domestic Tokyo→Kyoto Shinkansen does not involve customs or immigration.

**US return:** Declare goods over $800; Japan tax-free purchases may still require US duty if aggregate exceeds allowance.

**Purchasing strategy:** Buy tax-free in Tokyo/Kyoto; consumables consumed in Japan; durable goods (watches, electronics) carry home with receipts.

## Output

### Border crossing summary

| Crossing | Mode | Customs | Immigration | Duration |
|----------|------|---------|-------------|----------|
| US → Japan (HND Aug 1) | Air | Japan customs after baggage | Japan immigration | 30–60 min |
| Tokyo → Kyoto (Aug 4) | Shinkansen | None | None | — |
| Japan → US (KIX Aug 7) | Air | US CBP at ATL | US (Global Entry if enrolled) | 20–45 min ATL |

### Japan entry customs (Aug 1)

| Allowance | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Alcohol | 3 bottles (760mL each) spirits |
| Tobacco | 400 cigarettes |
| Perfume | 2 oz |
| Other goods | Total value under ¥200,000 — no duty |
| Currency | Declare if carrying >¥1,000,000 |

### Japan exit / US entry (Aug 7)

| Topic | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Tax-free goods | May be inspected at KIX; keep items unopened in sealed bag if required |
| US duty-free | $800 personal exemption |
| Declare | Gifts, commercial samples, >$10,000 cash equivalent |
| Food import to US | Rice, meat, fresh fruit generally **prohibited** — do not bring Nishiki fresh items home |
| Medication | Prescription in original packaging + doctor note |

### Restricted items — Japan

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Drugs | Strict — no cannabis CBD; pseudoephedrine limited |
| Weapons | Hiking poles OK in checked bag; pocket knives <6cm blade |
| Counterfeit goods | Seized if discovered |
| Cultural artifacts | Export permit needed for antiques >50 years |

### VAT / tax-free purchasing strategy

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| Shop | Look for "Tax Free" sign at Ginza, Don Quijote, department stores |
| Purchase | Show passport; pay tax-excluded price |
| Seal | Consumables sealed until departure |
| Departure KIX | Present goods if customs requests |
| US arrival | Declare if total goods >$800 |

### Best purchases in Japan

| Category | Where | Savings vs US |
|----------|-------|---------------|
| Stationery | Tokyo Station, Itoya | 20–40% |
| Sneakers (JP exclusives) | Harajuku | Variable |
| Grand Seiko / JDM watches | Ginza | 15–25% before duty |
| Kit-Kat flavors / snacks | Don Quijote | Souvenir value |
| Kyoto ceramics | Teramachi | Quality + authenticity |

### Airport logistics

| Airport | Tip |
|---------|-----|
| HND | Immigration often faster than NRT; VJW QR lane |
| KIX | Tax-free inspection counter near security; allow +15 min |
| ATL | CBP agriculture inspection for food/souvenirs |
