# Traveler Profile

## Reasoning

**Country count vs timeframe:** One country (Japan) over 6 hub nights / 7 travel days is ideal for a relaxed solo trip. Two hubs — Tokyo (shopping, food, coastal nature day trip) and Kyoto (history, temples, bamboo) — deliver all four interests without inter-country border friction.

**Entry/exit fit:** Profile leaves start city to the planner. **Open-jaw HND in / KIX out** with Tokyo→Kyoto Shinkansen avoids backtracking to Tokyo and puts the historic finale where temple-and-nature density peaks.

**Late July / early August window:** Peak summer heat and domestic travel season (Obon buildup) — profile deal-breakers managed via dawn starts, AC hotels, indoor midday blocks, and Enoshima for the "beach walk" perfect-day element. Tokyo avg ~31°C, Kyoto ~33°C humid. Crowds spike at Senso-ji, Fushimi Inari, and Arashiyama by 10 AM — schedule accordingly.

**Travel party:** Solo, one king/queen room, economy from **ATL**, 1 passenger — straightforward booking; Japan is exceptionally solo-dining friendly.

**Decision rule:** Vote when traveling with others — solo trip, so planner optimizes without forks unless noted as optional swaps.

**Budget sanity:** $5,000 all-in. MCP-verified flights **$2,114** (~42%) + hotels **$750** (~15%) = **$2,864** fixed ≈ 57%. Remaining **~$2,136** for Shinkansen (~$120), food, IC cards, attractions, shopping — ~$305/day, feasible with mix of street food and 1–2 splurge meals.

**Interest weighting:** Food 25% · Nature 25% · Shopping 25% · History 25% — balanced across Tokyo (shopping/food/nature day trip) and Kyoto (history/nature/food markets).
