# Culture & Museums

## Reasoning

History ranks **#3** after nature and food in [[01-traveler-profile]] — prioritize **temples, shrines, preserved towns** over museum marathons. [[04-master-itinerary]] already caps **one major indoor site per day**; this step maps **culture anchors to specific days** with dawn/AC timing for **late July–August heat and crowds**.

Solo visitor: temples free or low-cost; photography rules vary (no flash in halls). **Museum fatigue check**: max 1 museum + 1 temple complex per day — never stack Tokyo National Museum + Meiji + shrine on same day (itinerary complies).

## Output

### Culture Philosophy

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Outdoor history before 10:00** | Fushimi, Kiyomizu, Shirakawa-go |
| **Indoor AC midday** | Museums, sake museums, depachika culture |
| **Observe, don't perform** | Gion geiko district — no blocking alleys for photos |
| **One wow shrine/temple per day** | Depth over checklist |
