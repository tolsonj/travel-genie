# Culture & Museums

## Reasoning

**One-site-per-day rule:** Major temple/shrine each exploration day — Senso-ji (Aug 2), Kinkakuji + Fushimi (Aug 5 split AM/PM), Arashiyama (Aug 6). Avoid stacking Tokyo National Museum + Senso-ji same morning.

**Crowd strategy:** All MCP-recommended attractions require dawn or late-afternoon timing in peak summer. Shibuya Sky is the modern "culture" counterpoint — urban panorama as living architecture.

**Social filter:** teamLab is creator-famous but timed tickets and AC make it ideal rainy/heat backup, not primary anchor.
