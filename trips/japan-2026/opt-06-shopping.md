# Shopping Strategy

### Japan Shopping Strategy (Solo)

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Buy late, carry light** | Major souvenirs in **Kyoto/Osaka** last week; Tokyo for specialty only |
| **Tax-free** | Passport + same-day purchase ≥ ¥5,000 at registered stores; unopened until customs |
| **Payment** | IC card + contactless Visa; some small shops cash-only |
| **Packing** | Soft foldables in carry-on; ship heavy via **takkyubin** from department store desks |

---

### Tax-Free (免税)

1. Look for **Tax-Free** sign; show passport at counter (not vending machines).
2. Categories: consumables (food/cosmetics) vs general (electronics, clothing) — **separate bags**, sealed until departure.
3. **Customs**: You are a **US visitor** — Japan export OK; US import duty-free exemption ~$800 **aggregate** merchandise; declare over limit.
4. **Receipt**: Keep until KIX; staff may staple passport copy — remove carefully at immigration.

**Best tax-free stops**: Don Quijote (Osaka Namba), Bic Camera (Kyoto Station), department store duty-free desks (Isetan/Shiseido counters).

| **Sake (nigori/ginjo)** | Takayama/Kyoto | Brewery shops; airport sealed | ¥1,500–4,000 |

| **Matcha/snacks** | Osaka | Kuromon + Don Quijote | ¥500–3,000 |
| **Anime/games** | Tokyo/Osaka | Akihabara brief; Nipponbashi Osaka | ¥3,000–15,000 |
| **Stationery** | Tokyo | Loft/Itoya Marunouchi (Day 5 rainy) | ¥1,000–5,000 |

**Skip**: Mass-produced Sensō-ji trinkets unless one sentimental piece.

### Per-Region Shopping Plan

#### Tokyo (Days 1–5)

| Day | Window | Spot | Buy |
|-----|--------|------|-----|
| 2 | After Asakusa | **Kappabashi** | Kitchen tools, fake food samples |
| 2 | Optional | Nakamise | 1 omamori only if meaningful |
| 3 | Skip | Takeshita | Too crowded — photo walk only |
| 5 | AM/PM | **Tokyo Station City** depachika | Premium snacks for gifts; no tax-free on food eaten in Japan |
| 5 | Rainy | **Itoya** Ginza | Pens, notebooks |

**Optional evening**: **Akihabara** (1 hr) — Super Potato retro games; Yodobashi Camera; go before 21:00.

#### Takayama / Alps (Days 6–7)

| Day | Spot | Buy |
|-----|------|-----|
| 6 | Sanmachi Suji | Sake cups, **sarubobo** charm, Hida wood |
| 7 | Shirakawa-go | Local soba kits, woven goods (small) |

**Tip**: Shirakawa-go gift shops repeat Takayama — buy only if unique.

#### Kyoto (Days 8–11)

| Day | Spot | Buy |
|-----|------|-----|
| 8 | **Nishiki** | Tsukemono, yatsuhashi, tea — eat some, gift sealed packs |
| 9 | Fushimi | Sake brewery bottle (check flight liquids) |
| 11 | **Kiyomizu-zaka** early | 1 ceramic piece before shops heat up |

**Avoid**: Gion costume rental shops disguised as culture — not shopping goal.

#### Osaka (Days 12–13)

| Day | Spot | Buy |
|-----|------|-----|
| 12 | **Kuromon** | Food souvenirs same-day eat vs sealed |
| 12–13 | **Don Quijote Namba** | Tax-free snack/clothing bulk |
| 13 | **Nipponbashi** | Games/anime if Tokyo missed |

**Final sweep**: KIX duty-free — whisky, Kit Kat regional, cosmetics; prices OK not always best.

### Traps to Avoid

1. **Multi-floor tax-free maze** — queue at one consolidated counter per store.
2. **Ceramics in carry-on** — bubble wrap at store; else **takkyubin to home** (international shipping rare — carry carefully).
3. **Gachapon binge** — fun but adds ¥500×30 fast.
4. **Fake "outlet" villages** — time sink; skip unless rainy.
5. **Shopping on transit days** — Day 6, 8, 12 are for logistics + food only.
