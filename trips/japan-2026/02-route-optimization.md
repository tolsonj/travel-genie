# Route Optimization

## Reasoning

**Region order**: Tokyo → Japanese Alps (Takayama hub) → Kyoto → Osaka. Rationale aligned with [[01-traveler-profile]]:

1. **Tokyo first** — jet-lag soft landing, urban food/culture acclimation, best international flight access (NRT/HND).
2. **Alps second** — nature priority and **cooler elevation** before Kansai heat; logical Shinkansen + Limited Express vector westward.
3. **Kyoto third** — history and temple density while energy is high; mid-trip cultural anchor.
4. **Osaka last** — food-forward finale, shorter hop to **KIX** for open-jaw exit; avoids backtracking to Tokyo.

**Hub selection** (single-country adaptation of “country” logic):

| Hub | Role | Why this base |
|-----|------|---------------|
| **Tokyo** | Arrival + urban nature (parks, day-trip options) | Flight in; Meiji/Yoyogi, Ueno, optional Nikko or Kamakura day trip |
| **Takayama / Japanese Alps** | Nature relief | Hida mountains, Shirakawa-go day trip, morning markets; cooler than lowland cities |
| **Kyoto** | History + refined food | Temples, Philosopher’s Path, Fushimi early; fewer nights than Tokyo to limit temple fatigue |
| **Osaka** | Street food + departure | Dotonbori, Kuromon Market; 40 min to KIX |

**Late July–early August scoring** (1 = poor, 5 = excellent for this traveler):

| Hub | Weather | Crowds | Nature fit | Food | History | **Composite** |
|-----|---------|--------|------------|------|---------|---------------|
| Tokyo | 2 (hot/humid) | 3 | 3 | 5 | 3 | **3.2** |
| Takayama / Alps | 4 (cooler) | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | **4.2** |
| Kyoto | 2 | 2 (peak tourism) | 3 | 4 | 5 | **3.2** |
| Osaka | 2 | 3 | 2 | 5 | 3 | **3.0** |

**Day allocation tradeoff**: 5 Tokyo / 2 Alps / 4 Kyoto / 2 Osaka (+ arrival/departure buffers) balances nature (Alps block), history (Kyoto depth), and budget (fewer hotel moves). One fewer Tokyo night would under-serve jet lag recovery; one fewer Kyoto night would rush Fushimi + Arashiyama + Higashiyama.

**Transit**: JR Tokaido Shinkansen + Hida Limited Express. **7-day JR Pass** activation timed to cover Tokyo→Takayama→Kyoto→Osaka inter-hub legs (validate exact pass math at booking—Hida may need supplement). Intra-city: IC card (Suica/PASMO).

**Open-jaw**: Fly **into NRT or HND**, **out of KIX** — saves ~2.5 hrs + ¥14,000+ vs returning to Tokyo.

### Logistical Traps to Avoid

1. **Wrong airport pair** — HND is closer to Tokyo but NRT often cheaper; pick one inbound and stick to hotel transfer plan.
2. **JR Pass timing** — Activate to cover longest contiguous rail block; Hida Limited Express may need **seat fee + small supplement** even with pass.
3. **Kyoto in August heat** — Fushimi Inari at **06:00** or fail; midday temple visits = exhaustion + crowds.
4. **Shirakawa-go without reservation** — Morning buses sell out in summer; book 2–4 weeks ahead.
5. **Suitcase hub changes** — Takayama has cobblestone old town; use **takkyubin** (luggage forwarding) Tokyo→Kyoto or Kyoto→Osaka at least once.
6. **Sunday Shinkansen** — Jul 27 or Aug 3 may be busy; reserve seats early.

### Alternative Routes

#### Option B: Tokyo → Kyoto → Alps → Osaka
- **Trade-off**: History earlier while fresh; Alps as cooler breather before Osaka finale
- **Downside**: Tokyo→Kyoto first skips logical geographic sweep; Alps after Kyoto adds backtracking feel (Kyoto→Takayama→Osaka is L-shaped)
- **Best for**: If Tokyo heat at start is unacceptable and you fly in late evening

#### Option C: Drop Osaka — Kyoto base + KIX via Haruka
- **Route**: Tokyo (5) → Takayama (2) → Kyoto (5) → KIX depart
- **Trade-off**: Saves one hotel move; Osaka street food lost
- **Best for**: Strict history/nature focus; still exit KIX from Kyoto in ~75 min

#### Option D: Add Kanazawa (1 night) — cut Tokyo to 4 nights
- **Trade-off**: Kenroku-en garden + seafood; tighter Tokyo
- **Downside**: +1 transit day pushes toward 20% ceiling; budget pressure

### Booking Priority Queue

1. **International flights** (open-jaw **NRT/HND → KIX**) — book 2–4 months out; target $900–1,400
2. **Shirakawa-go bus** (Takayama ↔ Shirakawa-go) — summer seats limited
3. **7-day JR Pass** (or individual tickets if pass math fails) — purchase voucher before departure; activate Day 6
4. **Tokyo hotel** (5 nights) — business hotel near JR line (Shinjuku, Tokyo Station, Ueno)
5. **Takayama ryokan or guesthouse** (2 nights) — one night with **onsen** as budget splurge
6. **Kyoto hotel** (4 nights) — near Nijo or Karasuma for bus/subway access; not Gion (night noise + price)
7. **Osaka hotel** (2 nights) — Namba or Umeda for KIX access
8. **Timed entries** — teamLab (if desired), any Ghibli/Nintendo slots, optional Kamikochi day tour
