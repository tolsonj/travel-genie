# Accommodation

## Reasoning

**Neighborhood fit:** Ueno/Okachimachi puts the solo traveler within walking distance of Senso-ji (Asakusa, 15 min), direct Ginza Line to Shinjuku/Shibuya, and quick HND access via Keikyu from Shinagawa if needed. Kyoto base at Nijo Castle balances west-side temple access (Kinkakuji bus) with walkable downtown (Nishiki, Gion).

**Hotel type:** Japan's 2–3★ business hotels and apartment-hotels (MIMARU) deliver clean, compact rooms with US-platform booking — better value than Airbnb in regulated Japan. King/queen availability is limited; double bed in compact room is standard at Sakura Cross.

**Booking urgency:** Late July–early August is peak domestic travel — book refundable rates now, convert to non-refundable when flights confirmed.

**Cross-hub logistics:** Aug 4 checkout Tokyo / check-in Kyoto same day — use hotel luggage forwarding (takkyubin) if desired, or carry-on only for Shinkansen ease.
