| Strategy | Application |
|----------|-------------|
| **Depart US Thu/Fri evening** | Land Tokyo Fri/Sat AM; use weekend for jet-lag recovery without burning PTO on travel day |

### Jet Lag Management

**Time shift**: US Pacific (UTC-7) → JST (UTC+9) = **16 hrs forward**; US Eastern = **13 hrs forward**.

**Outbound protocol (US → Tokyo, Day 1)**:
- **In flight**: Sleep if arriving morning; stay awake if arriving evening—target **21:00 JST bedtime**
- **Day 1 (Jul 24)**: No naps >20 min; sunlight walk 15:00–17:00; bed by 21:00 ✓ per [[04-master-itinerary]]
- **Day 2**: Near-normal; slight 15:00 slump OK
- **Day 3 (Jul 26)**: Full energy for Meiji + Kamakura prep
- **Melatonin**: 0.5 mg at local bedtime Days 1–2 only (not high-dose)

**Mid-trip hub changes**: No new time zone—Alps/Kyoto/Osaka all JST; no re-acclimation needed.

**Return (Aug 6, eastbound)**:
- Easier direction; body extends day naturally
- Hydrate on flight; walk airport before boarding
- US recovery: 2–3 days to normal sleep

### Early Access & Crowd Avoidance

Deal-breaker from [[profile-japan]]: **massive crowds**. Timing is the primary weapon in peak summer.

| Site | Opens | **Target arrival** | Why |
|------|-------|-------------------|-----|
| **Tsukiji Outer Market** | ~05:00 vendors | **07:00** Jul 28 | Sushi breakfast before tour groups |
| **Fushimi Inari** | 24 hrs | **05:30–06:00** Aug 1 | Empty torii before 08:00 tour buses |
| **Arashiyama bamboo** | 24 hrs | **07:00** Aug 2 | Grove packed by 09:00 |
| **Kiyomizu-dera** | 06:00 (summer) | **06:30** Aug 3 | Stage view before bus groups |
| **Shirakawa-go** | 09:00 village | **First bus ~08:00** Jul 30 | Viewpoint before 10:00 coaches |
| **Meiji Jingu** | Sunrise | **08:00** Jul 26 | Forest quiet before Sunday crush |
| **Osaka Castle Park** | 24 hrs grounds | **08:00** Aug 5 | Heat + tour groups by 10:00 |
| **Dotonbori** | 24 hrs | **Weekday Tue Aug 4** | Avoid Fri/Sat neon crush |

**Sites that don't need dawn**:
- Nishiki Market (covered; go 14:00 when lunch crowd clears)
- Kuromon Osaka (morning OK but not pre-dawn critical)
- Takayama Sanmachi (evening dusk light + fewer day-trippers)

**Late-access gems**:
- Pontocho alley twilight (not geiko chase—observe only)
- Dotonbori canal reflection after 20:00 on weekday
- Golden Gai: enter one bar **before 21:00** to avoid peak crush
