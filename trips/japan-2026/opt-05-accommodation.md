# Accommodation

### Booking Strategy (Solo)

| Tactic | Why |
|--------|-----|
| **Book 8–12 weeks out** | Pre-Obon domestic travel lifts Jul 27–Aug 3 weekend rates |
| **Platforms** | Booking.com (free cancel), Agoda, Rakuten Travel; Japan Hotel Search for ryokan |
| **Room type** | **Single** or **semi-double** — confirm bed width (140 cm semi-double OK solo) |
| **Cancellation** | Flexible until 7 days out on city hotels; ryokan often stricter |
| **Luggage** | Use hotel **takkyubin** Jul 28 Tokyo→Kyoto, carry day bag on Takayama leg |
| **Check-in** | Many hotels 15:00; drop bags early; ask English front desk for forwarding forms |

**Budget envelope (lodging only)**

| Hub | Nights | Target/night | Subtotal |
|-----|--------|--------------|----------|
| Tokyo | 5 | $85–110 | $425–550 |
| Takayama | 2 | $120–180 (1 ryokan night) | $240–360 |
| Kyoto | 4 | $90–120 | $360–480 |
| Osaka | 2 | $80–100 | $160–200 |
| **Total** | **13** | — | **~$1,185–1,590** |

### TOKYO — 5 Nights (Jul 24–28)

**Recommended neighborhood**: **Ueno** or **Asakusa** (primary); **Shinjuku west** (backup)

| Factor | Ueno/Asakusa | Shinjuku west |
|--------|----------------|---------------|
| Itinerary fit | Day 2 Ueno/Asakusa; easy Keisei/Narita access | Day 3 Meiji/Harajuku; Day 5 Tsukiji reachable |
| Transit | JR Yamanote + Ginza/Keisei | JR + metro hub |
| Crowds | Lower at night than Shibuya | Busier but manageable west of station |
| Solo | Many business hotels with singles | Excellent late food options |

**Rationale**: Matches Days 2–5 without daily cross-city commutes; Shinobazu/Asakusa morning starts on foot.

#### Top Picks (US-bookable, solo-friendly)

| Property | Area | Est./night | Why |
|----------|------|------------|-----|
| **Mitsui Garden Hotel Ueno** | Ueno | $95–130 | Reliable chain; AC; walk to Ueno Park; singles common |
| **Richmond Hotel Premier Asakusa International** | Asakusa | $85–115 | Near Sensō-ji; good arrival-night base |
| **Hotel Gracery Shinjuku** | Shinjuku | $100–140 | Famous Godzilla head; strong transit; use if Ueno sold out |
| **sequence MIYASHITA PARK** | Shibuya-adjacent | $110–150 | Splurge-leaning; only if anime/evening Shibuya priority |

**Recommended**: **Mitsui Garden Hotel Ueno** — best balance for nature-leaning Tokyo days and museum rainy backups.

**Book via**: Booking.com or official site; compare Rakuten for Japan-only deals.

**Arrival note (Day 1)**: Pre-write hotel address in Japanese from confirmation email for taxi fallback.

### TAKAYAMA — 2 Nights (Jul 29–30)

**Recommended neighborhood**: **Within 10 min walk of JR Takayama Station** (Sanmachi Suji, Miyagawa morning market, highway bus terminal)

**Rationale**: Day 6 soft transit + Day 7 Shirakawa-go bus — minimize cobblestone wheeling; onsen recovery after long rail day.

#### Top Picks

| Property | Type | Est./night | Why |
|----------|------|------------|-----|
| **Oyado Koto No Yume** | Ryokan | $140–200 | Central; tatami + kaiseki option; solo rooms sometimes |
| **Takayama Ouan** | Ryokan/onsen | $130–180 | Rooftop bath; walk to old town |
| **Hida Hotel Plaza** | Business | $90–120 | Station-adjacent; Western beds; good rainy backup |
| **Best Western Hotel Takayama** | Business | $85–110 | Predictable AC; less character |

**Recommended**: **Oyado Koto No Yume** or **Takayama Ouan** for **one splurge night** (Jul 29 Hida beef dinner per itinerary — reserve hotel dinner or walk to Ajikura Tengoku).

**Book via**: Rakuten Travel or Japanican for ryokan; confirm **curfew** and **solo dinner** surcharge.

**Critical**: Confirm **Shirakawa-go bus stop** distance; book bus tickets at tourist info near station after check-in.

### KYOTO — 4 Nights (Jul 31 – Aug 3)

**Recommended neighborhood**: **Kawaramachi / Sanjo** (primary); **Kyoto Station** (budget backup only)

| Factor | Kawaramachi | Kyoto Station |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| Itinerary | Nishiki (Day 8), Pontocho, Fushimi line access | Better for Arashiyama JR (Day 10) |
| Heat | Covered arcades, canal walks | Underground mall AC |
| Crowds | Touristy strip but flat evening strolls | Tour bus volume |

**Rationale**: Days 8–11 need early Fushimi (Aug 1) — Hankyu/Kintetsu from Kawaramachi faster than fighting station crowds at 05:30.

#### Top Picks

| Property | Est./night | Why |
|----------|------------|-----|
| **Hotel Gracery Kyoto Sanjo** | $95–125 | Central; singles; walk Nishiki |
| **Mitsui Garden Hotel Kyoto Sanjo** | $110–145 | Quiet chain quality; laundry |
| **Hotel Tavinos Kyoto** | $70–95 | Budget capsule-hotel style but private rooms; solo travelers |
| **Piece Hostel Sanjo** | $55–75 | Social solo option; lockers; not ideal if jet-lagged |

**Recommended**: **Hotel Gracery Kyoto Sanjo** or **Mitsui Garden Kyoto Sanjo**.

**Takkyubin**: Receive forwarded bag from Tokyo by Jul 31 afternoon; send Osaka-bound bag Aug 3 evening.

### OSAKA — 2 Nights (Aug 4–5)

**Recommended neighborhood**: **Namba** (primary); **Shin-Osaka** (only if Namba sold out)

**Rationale**: Day 12 Kuromon + Dotonbori on foot; Day 13 Namba Yasaka; **Nankai Rapi:t** to KIX Aug 6 from Namba.

#### Top Picks

| Property | Est./night | Why |
|----------|------------|-----|
| **Cross Hotel Osaka** | $100–130 | Namba walk; design-forward; solo rooms |
| **Hotel Gracery Osaka Namba** | $90–120 | Same chain consistency as Tokyo/Kyoto |
| **Fraser Residence Nankai Osaka** | $110–150 | Apartment-style; good pre-flight pack night |
| **Dormy Inn Premium Namba** | $85–110 | Onsen on roof; laundry; solo favorite |

**Recommended**: **Dormy Inn Premium Namba** (value + onsen) or **Cross Hotel Osaka** (slightly nicer finish).

**Departure (Day 14)**: Late checkout rare — store bag at Namba station lockers after checkout; allow 2.5–3 hr to KIX.

### Solo Traveler Notes

- **No ryokan multi-night required** — one Alps night delivers culture without blowing budget.
- **Capsule hotels**: OK for 1 night experiment; skip if you need guaranteed sleep (jet lag).
- **Anime optional**: If evening Akihabara wanted, stay Ueno and take Ginza Line — do not move hotel.
- **Noise**: Request **quiet floor / away from elevator** at booking; earplugs for Golden Gai night (Day 3).
