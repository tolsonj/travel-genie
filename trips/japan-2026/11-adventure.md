# Adventure & Nature

## Reasoning

**Profile fit:** Relaxed cultural — not extreme adventure. "Nature" = coastal walk (Enoshima), bamboo grove, Fushimi hillside hike, Shibuya Sky urban panorama. No scuba, no multi-hour mountain summits in August heat.

**Seasonality:** Late July = hot-humid. Schedule land activities dawn; water temps at Enoshima ~26°C — swimmable but profile says beach *walk*, not necessarily swim.

**Recovery:** No high-intensity day before Shinkansen (Aug 4) or departure (Aug 7). Fushimi summit optional — lower loops sufficient.
