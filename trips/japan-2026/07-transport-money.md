# Transport & Money

## Reasoning

**Route legs:** (1) ATL→HND international, (2) Tokyo intra-city, (3) Tokyo→Kyoto Shinkansen, (4) Kyoto intra-city, (5) Kyoto→KIX Haruka, (6) KIX→ATL international. Japan is rail-first; no rental car needed.

**JR Pass math:** 7-day JR Pass (~¥50,000 / ~$335) does **not** pay off for one Shinkansen leg + local rides — buy individual tickets + IC card.

**MCP flights:** Verified 2026-06-29 — recommended total $2,114 (42% of budget). See [[flight-comparison]].

**Money:** Japan is increasingly card-friendly but cash still needed at shrines, small eateries, and some lockers. Withdraw JPY at 7-Eleven ATMs (Seven Bank).

### Scams and mistakes

| Trap | Avoid |
|------|-------|
| Unlicensed taxi at HND | Use official queue or train |
| Nozomi with JR Pass | Pass not valid on Nozomi anyway |
| Last Haruka | Check schedule — gaps after 8 PM |
| IC card insufficient balance | Top up at any station machine |
| Rush-hour Shinkansen standing | Book reserved seat Aug 4 PM (Obon approach) |

### Money management — Japan

| Topic | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Currency | JPY (¥) only |
| Cash vs card | 70% card at chains; cash at shrines, small shops, lockers |
| ATM | 7-Eleven (Seven Bank), post office — fee ~¥220 + bank fee |
| Cards | Visa/MC widely accepted; Amex spotty |
| DCC | **Decline** dynamic currency conversion — charge in JPY |
| Tipping | Not customary |
| Budget app | Trail Wallet or YNAB — set ¥155 = $1 rough (verify rate) |
