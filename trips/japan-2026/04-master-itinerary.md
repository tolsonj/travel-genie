# Master Itinerary

## Reasoning

**Planning philosophy:** "Optimized Experience" structure with 70% scheduled anchors, 30% spontaneous flexibility. Balances nature-first interests with cultural depth, heat mitigation, and recovery days.

**Constraints applied:**
- Late July-August heat → early morning outdoor activities, indoor afternoons
- Crowd aversion → 6-8am starts at major attractions, weekday temple visits
- Nature priority → Alps front-loaded, urban culture back-loaded
- Solo traveler accessibility → clear daily structure, optional social activities
- Budget consciousness → Mix of free/cheap and splurge activities daily

**Seasonal timing assumptions:**
- Travel dates: July 24 - August 6, 2026 (14 days, avoids Obon Aug 13-16)
- Tokyo during Tenjin Matsuri prep (July 24-25)
- Alps at peak summer hiking season
- Kyoto post-Gion Matsuri (festival ends July 24)

**Daily structure template:**
- **Morning (6-11am):** High-energy, outdoor, or crowd-prone activities
- **Afternoon (12-4pm):** Indoor, air-conditioned, or low-intensity activities
- **Evening (5-10pm):** Dining, neighborhoods, nightlife, or relaxation
