# Packing List

## Core Essentials (15 items max)

1. **Passport** (valid through 2028 ✅) + 1 photocopy
2. **Phone** + charger + portable battery (20,000mAh)
3. **Credit/debit cards** (2x) + small amount of USD cash ($100 emergency)
4. **Hiking shoes** (broken-in, ankle support)
5. **Daypack** (20-30L for Kamikochi hike)
6. **Quick-dry clothes** (4 shirts, 2 pants, 7 underwear, 5 socks)
7. **Rain jacket** (lightweight, packable)
8. **Sunglasses** + hat (sun protection)
9. **Toiletries** (travel-size, TSA-compliant for carry-on)
10. **Medications** + doctor's letter (if applicable)
11. **Reusable water bottle** (refill at hotels/konbini)
12. **Universal adapter** (NOT needed, US plugs work in Japan ✅)
13. **First aid kit** (see [[14-health-safety]])
14. **Earplugs** (for capsule hotels shared spaces)
15. **Laundry detergent packets** (hand-wash clothes mid-trip)

## Region-Specific Gear

**Tokyo/Kyoto/Osaka (Urban):**
- Comfortable walking shoes (8-10km daily walking)
- Light layers (August heat = 30-35°C, AC indoors = cold contrast)

**Japanese Alps (Takayama/Kamikochi):**
- Hiking pants (not jeans, chafing on long hikes)
- Extra socks (blister prevention)
- Bear bell (optional, ¥500 at Kamikochi trailhead)

**All Regions:**
- Modest clothes for temples (covered shoulders, no shorts above knee)
- Slip-on shoes (frequent shoe removal at temples, accommodations)

## Tech Setup

- eSIM activated before departure (see [[09-tech-connectivity]])
- Offline maps downloaded (Tokyo, Alps, Kyoto/Osaka)
- Google Translate offline (Japanese ↔ English)
- IC card app (Suica) pre-loaded ¥3,000

## Packing Strategy

**Checked bag (23kg limit):**
- Clothes, shoes, toiletries = ~15kg
- **Shopping space remaining:** 8kg ✅ (see [[06-shopping]])

**Carry-on:**
- Passport, phone, charger, medications, 1 change of clothes (if checked bag lost)

**What NOT to pack:**
- Hair dryer (hotels provide)
- Towel (capsule hotels provide)
- Trekking poles (rent at Kamikochi ¥500)
- Winter gear (late July-August = hot)

**Validation:** Total packed weight ~18kg (checked) + 5kg (carry-on) = under airline limits ✅. Shopping buffer = 8kg available.
