# Packing

## Reasoning

14 days solo, **single country**, late July–early August = **hot humid lowlands** + **cooler Alps evenings**. Activity mix from [[04-master-itinerary]]: urban walking (8–12 km/day), moderate hikes (Fushimi Inari, Kamakura, Arashiyama), Shirakawa-go village paths, temple visits requiring modest dress. **Four hotel hubs** with takkyubin forwarding Jul 28 and Aug 3—pack one medium checked bag + carry-on; daypack for rail/transit days. Tech from [[09-tech-connectivity]]: eSIM (keep US SIM for 2FA), 20,000 mAh power bank, **Type A plugs** (same as US—no voltage adapter for phone/laptop). Budget $5K means no extra checked bag fees if avoidable—compression cubes + one laundry stop mid-trip.

### Core "Do Not Forget" List (15 Items)

1. **Valid US passport** — expires 2028 ✓; one blank page minimum
2. **Visit Japan Web QR codes** — immigration + customs saved offline + printed ([[03-immigration-entry]])
3. **Travel insurance policy # + 24/7 emergency line** — phone contact + printed wallet card ([[14-health-safety]])
4. **JR Pass 7-day exchange voucher** — print + PDF; activate Jul 29 ([[07-transport-money]])
5. **Japan eSIM activated before departure** — Airalo/Ubigi 15-day; QR saved in email ([[09-tech-connectivity]])
6. **Credit/debit cards + bank travel notification** — 7-Eleven ATM compatible (no foreign fee card ideal)
7. **Virtual or physical Suica/PASMO** — loaded ¥3,000–5,000 before leaving airport
8. **Prescription medications** — 17-day supply in carry-on + doctor note in English
9. **20,000 mAh USB-C power bank** — airline-compliant; essential for Fushimi dawn + Shirakawa-go bus day
10. **Trail runners or broken-in walking shoes** — Fushimi stairs + Kamakura cobblestones; not new shoes
11. **Ultralight rain jacket** — afternoon thunderstorms; Alps weather shifts fast
12. **SPF 50 sunscreen + hat** — non-negotiable for Jul/Aug; reapply every 2 hrs
13. **Electrolyte tablets / ORS packets** — heat days in Kyoto/Osaka
14. **Compression packing cubes** — enables takkyubin bag vs carry-on split Jul 28
15. **Hotel confirmation printout** — all addresses in **Japanese + English** for taxi/takkyubin labels

### Summer Japan Wardrobe

**Climate target**: 30–35°C humid (Tokyo/Kyoto/Osaka); 25–30°C Takayama; AC aggressive indoors.

| Category | Pack | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| **Tops** | 5–6 lightweight breathable shirts (linen, merino, Uniqlo Airism) | Quick-dry; one modest shirt for temples |
| **Bottoms** | 2 lightweight trousers + 1 shorts | Temples: knees covered; shorts OK most casual dining |
| **Layer** | 1 packable cardigan or ultralight fleece | Takayama evenings + brutal train AC |
| **Footwear** | Trail runners (primary) + compact sandals | Slip-on sandals for ryokan/onsen |
| **Underwear/socks** | 7 underwear + 3 merino hiking socks | Laundry at hotel Day 5 or Kyoto Day 9 |
| **Sleep** | 1 set sleepwear | Ryokan may provide yukata |
| **Rain** | Packable rain jacket + small folding umbrella | Umbrella also sold at every konbini ¥500 |

**Temple/shrine kit**: Shoulders covered (no tank tops at active shrines); remove hat inside main hall; socks for temple floors where shoes removed.

**Onsen/ryokan (Takayama)**: Ryokan provides yukata + towels; pack minimal; tattoos increasingly accepted but confirm hotel policy.

### Takkyubin & Luggage Strategy

**Hub changes**: Jul 28 (Tokyo→Takayama) and Aug 3 (Kyoto→Osaka)—forward **large checked bag** via **takkyubin** (¥1,500–2,500/bag; 1-day delivery between major cities).

| Bag | Role |
|-----|------|
| **Checked spinner 24–26"** | Main wardrobe; forward between hubs |
| **Carry-on backpack 40 L** | 3-day clothes, all valuables, meds, docs |
| **Packable daypack 18–20 L** | Folds flat in carry-on; deploy for hike/transit days |

**Day 6 (Tokyo→Takayama)**: Carry-on only on train if bag forwarded previous evening from Tokyo hotel front desk.

**Day 14 (KIX departure)**: Carry-on preferred for 14-day solo; checked bag if shopping haul (kitchen knives from Kappabashi must be **checked**—never carry-on).
