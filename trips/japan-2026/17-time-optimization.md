## Jet Lag Management

**Time zone:** Japan = UTC+9, US Eastern = UTC-4  
**Time difference:** 13 hours ahead

**Outbound (US → Japan):**
- Flight crosses International Date Line (lose a day on calendar)
- Strategy:
  - Sleep on plane (eye mask, earplugs, melatonin 5mg)
  - Arrive Tokyo 3pm local = stay awake until 9pm (adjust to local time immediately)
  - Day 1 = light activity (Senso-ji evening walk, no heavy hiking)

**Inbound (Japan → US):**
- Gain a day on calendar (arrive same day as departure)
- Strategy: Sleep on plane, arrive afternoon, go to bed at normal US time (10-11pm)

**Recovery:** 2-3 days to fully adjust (why Day 1 is soft day)
