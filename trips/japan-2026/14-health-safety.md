# Health & Safety

## Reasoning

**Activities:** Moderate walking/hiking (Fushimi, Enoshima, Arashiyama) — no extreme sports. Heat stroke is the primary risk in late July — hydration and dawn scheduling are medical priorities.

**Japan safety:** Among world's safest countries for solo travelers. Low violent crime; watch for bicycle/pedestrian collisions and occasional chikan (groping) on rush-hour trains — avoid rush hour per profile.

**Insurance:** Multi-country policy needed even for single country if departing US — confirm medical evacuation and adventure coverage.
