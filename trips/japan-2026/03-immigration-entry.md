- No COVID-related restrictions as of 2026

**Assumptions:**

### 2. Travel Authorizations (2026-Specific)

#### Visit Japan Web (VJW) — Digital Entry System

**STATUS:** ✅ **STRONGLY RECOMMENDED (not mandatory but saves 30-60 minutes at airport)**

**What it is:** Japan's digital immigration and customs pre-clearance system (similar to USA's ESTA, but free).

**Registration window:** 2 weeks before departure to 6 hours before flight landing

**What you submit:**
1. Passport information (photo upload)
2. Flight details (arrival/departure)
3. Accommodation addresses (first night minimum)
4. Health declaration (any symptoms, medications brought into Japan)
5. Customs declaration (items to declare, duty-free purchases)

**How to access:** [vjw.digital.go.jp](https://vjw.digital.go.jp) (requires email registration)

**QR codes generated:**
- Immigration QR (blue screen)
- Customs QR (red screen)

**At airport:** Show QR codes at immigration and customs — skip paper forms, faster processing.

**Alternative:** Paper disembarkation card and customs form on flight (still accepted, just slower).

**RECOMMENDATION:** Complete Visit Japan Web 24-48 hours before departure. Screenshot QR codes as backup.

#### ETIAS (Europe), UK ETA — NOT APPLICABLE

Japan does not participate in ETIAS (Europe-only system) or UK ETA. No additional travel authorization required beyond passport.

#### Transit Visa Requirements

**IF transiting through another country en route to Japan (e.g., South Korea, China):**
- Most US passport holders receive transit permission without advance visa for <24hr layovers (airside only)
- Check specific country's transit rules if layover >8 hours and leaving airport

**For this trip:** Direct US-Japan flights eliminate transit visa concerns.

### 5. Medication & Prescription Drug Import Rules

#### Strict Control: Japan Bans Many Common Medications

**CRITICAL:** Japan has **very strict** drug import laws. Many over-the-counter medications in the US are **prohibited or restricted** in Japan.

**Commonly banned substances:**
- ❌ Adderall, Vyvanse, other amphetamine-based ADHD meds (stimulants = illegal narcotics in Japan)
- ❌ Pseudoephedrine (found in Sudafed, DayQuil, many cold medicines)
- ❌ Codeine-based painkillers (Tylenol 3, etc.)
- ❌ Medical cannabis/CBD (any form, even with US prescription)

**Allowed with documentation:**
- ✅ Most antibiotics, blood pressure meds, insulin
- ✅ Ibuprofen, acetaminophen (Tylenol), aspirin
- ✅ Allergy meds (Zyrtec, Claritin, Allegra)

#### Required Documentation (If Bringing Prescription Meds)

**For up to 1 month supply of non-controlled medication:**
- No advance approval needed
- Bring **doctor's letter** stating:
  - Medication name (generic + brand)
  - Dosage and frequency
  - Medical necessity
  - Doctor's contact info + signature
- Keep meds in **original pharmacy bottles** with your name on label
- Carry in carry-on luggage (not checked bags)

**For controlled substances (antidepressants, anxiety meds, pain meds):**
- Requires **Yakkan Shoumei** (import certificate) from Japan's Ministry of Health
- Apply **at least 2-3 weeks before departure**
- [Application portal](https://www.ncd.mhlw.go.jp/en/)

**For this trip:** If taking any daily medications, consult [Japan Customs Medication Guide](https://www.customs.go.jp/english/summary/passenger.htm) 1 month before departure.

### 7. Entry Process & Customs Declaration

#### At Tokyo Haneda/Narita Airport

**Step 1: Disembarkation**
- Flight lands
- Fill out paper forms if not using Visit Japan Web
- Proceed to immigration

**Step 2: Immigration (Foreign Passport Line)**
- Show passport + Visit Japan Web QR (if registered)
- Questions asked (typical):
  - "Purpose of visit?" → "Tourism"
  - "How long will you stay?" → "14 days"
  - "Where will you stay?" → Name first hotel
- **Fingerprints + photo taken** (biometric entry for all foreign nationals age 16+)
- Passport stamped with entry date
- Duration: 5-15 minutes (with VJW), 20-45 minutes (without)

**Step 3: Baggage Claim**
- Collect checked luggage
- Proceed to customs

**Step 4: Customs Declaration**
- Show passport + Customs QR (if using VJW) or paper customs form
- Declare:
  - Cash >¥1,000,000 (~$7,000 USD)
  - Alcohol >3 bottles (760ml each)
  - Tobacco >400 cigarettes or 100 cigars
  - Perfume >2 ounces
  - Goods for resale
  - Prohibited items (see below)
- Most tourists select "Nothing to Declare" (green line)
- Random bag checks (5-10% of passengers)

**Duration:** 5-20 minutes depending on line length

**Total entry time:** 30-60 minutes (Haneda, faster) or 60-90 minutes (Narita, larger airport)

#### Prohibited Items at Japan Customs

**Strictly forbidden (confiscation + potential arrest):**
- Illegal drugs (including cannabis in any form)
- Firearms, explosives, ammunition (even spent casings)
- Counterfeit goods, pirated media
- Child pornography (Japan defines this broadly; be cautious with manga/anime content)
- Fruits, vegetables, plants, seeds (agriculture ban)
- Meat products (canned, dried, vacuum-sealed beef jerky, etc.)

**Restricted (requires permits or limits):**
- Prescription medications (see Section 5 above)
- Drones (registration required for flights)
- Ham radio equipment (requires license)

**Allowed:**
- Laptops, cameras, electronics (personal use)
- Duty-free: 3 bottles alcohol, 400 cigarettes, 2oz perfume, ¥200,000 (~$1,400) in goods

### 8. Departure Requirements

#### Leaving Japan (Osaka Kansai Airport)

**Exit immigration:**
- Much faster than entry (5-10 minutes)
- Passport scanned, no questions typically asked
- No exit stamp issued (Japan stopped exit stamps in 2020s)

**Tax refund process (if applicable):**
- If purchased goods with "tax-free" tag (8-10% consumption tax refund)
- Bring receipts + passport to customs counter **before check-in**
- Goods must be in sealed bag, unopened
- Show items if requested

**Departure tax:**
- ¥1,000 (~$7) departure tax typically included in flight ticket price
- No separate payment needed at airport
