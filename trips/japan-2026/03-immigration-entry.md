# Immigration & Entry

## Reasoning

**Citizenship:** US passport holder — visa-free short-term stay for tourism in Japan (up to 90 days). No advance visa application required.

**Single entry, single country:** Fly into Japan at HND Aug 1; domestic travel Tokyo→Kyoto does not involve immigration re-clearance. Depart KIX Aug 7 — single exit.

**2026-specific:** Japan uses **Visit Japan Web** for digital customs/immigration pre-registration (recommended, not always mandatory). No ETIAS/UK ETA (Europe/UK not on route). No Schengen/EES.

**Passport validity:** Japan requires passport valid for duration of stay; best practice: valid **6+ months** beyond Aug 7, 2026 (i.e., valid through Feb 2027+).

**Health:** No yellow fever certificate required from US. No COVID entry restrictions as of 2026 (verify before travel).
