# Immigration & Entry

## Reasoning

Citizenship **confirmed US** ([[01-traveler-profile]]). Route: US → Tokyo (NRT or HND) → domestic travel within Japan → depart **KIX**. Stay: **14 days** (late Jul – early Aug 2026), well under visa-free limits.

Japan entry for US tourists remains **visa-free** for short stays (up to **90 days** for tourism/business visits without paid work). No advance visa application required as of 2026 policy baseline—verify before departure at [travel.state.gov](https://travel.state.gov) and [mofa.go.jp](https://www.mofa.go.jp).

**Visit Japan Web** (VJW) is the standard pre-arrival digital channel for immigration and customs declarations—replaces most paper forms when completed online. Not legally mandatory for all travelers but **strongly recommended** for faster airport processing.

Passport: expires **2028** — exceeds typical requirement (valid for stay duration; airlines often enforce **6 months** beyond travel dates—Montrez clears easily for Aug 6, 2026 departure).

No ETIAS, UK ETA, or EES applies (EU/UK systems). Internal Japan travel requires **no** additional immigration checks between hubs.

2026 note: Japan periodically adjusts digital entry requirements; re-check VJW and airline guidance **30 days before departure**.

### Customs & Prohibited Items (High-Signal)

- **Drugs**: Strict zero tolerance—including some US OTC medications (e.g., pseudoephedrine-containing products). Bring only personal quantities; keep prescriptions in original packaging.
- **Food/agriculture**: Fresh fruit, meat, and some plant products restricted—declare or discard.
- **Cash**: Declare if carrying **> ¥1,000,000** (~$6,700 USD equivalent).
- **Alcohol/tobacco**: Personal allowance limits apply—declare excess.

### Practical Entry Checklist — Japan

#### Before departure
- [ ] Valid US passport (exp. 2028)
- [ ] 1+ blank passport page
- [ ] Open-jaw ticket: into **NRT/HND**, out **KIX**
- [ ] First hotel address in Tokyo (English + Japanese if hotel provides)
- [ ] **Visit Japan Web** completed; immigration + customs QR saved offline
- [ ] Travel insurance purchased (medical + trip delay—summer weather/disruptions)
- [ ] Credit card with no foreign transaction fee; notify bank of Japan dates
- [ ] IC card plan: **Suica/PASMO** (mobile wallet or physical) for Tokyo/Kansai local transit
- [ ] JR Pass voucher (if using) — exchange at airport or major JR office Day 1 or Day 6

#### At arrival (NRT or HND)
- [ ] Immigration: passport + VJW QR (or paper form if not using VJW)
- [ ] Finger photo/biometrics if requested (standard for foreign nationals)
- [ ] Customs: VJW customs QR or declaration form
- [ ] Activate/connect phone (eSIM or pocket WiFi pickup if pre-ordered)
- [ ] Load Suica/PASMO; confirm train route to hotel
- [ ] Keep **landing card stub** if issued (rare with VJW—follow airport staff direction)

#### Domestic travel (no re-immigration)
- [ ] Carry passport or secure photocopy + photo ID for hotel check-in
- [ ] Shinkansen: match JR Pass activation date to inter-hub travel ([[02-route-optimization]])

#### At departure (KIX)
- [ ] Allow **2.5–3 hrs** before international flight
- [ ] Departure immigration + customs (no exit tax separate fee beyond ticket taxes)
- [ ] Tax-free shopping receipts may need stamp at customs if applicable
