# Food & Dining

## Reasoning

Food is **#2 interest**; [[01-traveler-profile]] targets **~$100/day** food (~$1,400 for 14 days) within **$5K all-in** — achievable with **market breakfasts, lunch anchors, one splurge dinner per hub**, konbini hydration. No dietary restrictions stated.

**Late July–August**: appetite drops in heat — lighter lunch, bigger dinner; seek AC (depachika, izakaya, museums). **Crowds**: reserve **Hida beef (Day 6)**, **obanzai (Day 10)**; standing sushi at Tsukiji before 08:30.

Align dishes to [[04-master-itinerary]] days; solo dining is normal at counters and izakaya.

### Solo Dining Customs

- Counter seats = **1 person welcome**; say **ひとりです** (hitori desu).
- No tipping; pay at register or table tablet.
- **Smoking**: Many izakaya have smoking sections — ask **禁煙** (kin-en) if sensitive.
- **Water**: Free oolong/hot water common; iced water less automatic — ask.
- **Meal timing**: Lunch 11:30–13:30; dinner 18:00–21:00; kitchens close early in Alps.

### Food Safety

- Tap water safe in cities; bottled if preferred.
- Summer fish: eat at high-turnover counters (Tsukiji, Kuromon).
- **Heat**: Carry electrolyte drinks (konbini); avoid unrefrigerated mayo salads on street.
