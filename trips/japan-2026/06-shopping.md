# Shopping Strategy

## Reasoning

**Itinerary scope:** Tokyo (Aug 2–3) and Kyoto (Aug 6). Shopping days on non-transit days only. Profile interests include shopping but $5K total budget limits luxury watch/handbag spend — focus on fashion, sneakers, and food gifts.

**Social anchor:** Tokyo dominates creator shopping content — Ginza flagship row, Omotesando architecture boutiques, Shimokitazawa vintage, Daikanyama T-Site. Kyoto: Nishiki food gifts, Kyoto ceramics, teramachi covered arcade.

**Japan strength:** Tax-free shopping for tourists (consumption tax 10% waived at participating stores with passport); exceptional sneaker/streetwear scene in Harajuku/Omotesando; second-hand luxury in Komehyo/Shimokitazawa.

**VAT/tax-free:** No EU-style VAT refund — instead **immediate tax exemption** at register (8% or 10% off) for purchases ¥5,000–¥500,000 per store per day at licensed shops.

### Tax-free summary (Japan)

| Topic | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Rate | 10% consumption tax waived at participating stores |
| Minimum | ¥5,000 per store per day (general goods) |
| Process | Show passport at register; goods may be sealed in bag until departure |
| Consumables | Separate ¥5,000 min for food/cosmetics — cannot mix with general goods in same bag |
| Departure | Present goods + receipt at KIX customs if requested (usually spot-check) |

### Customs/duty cheat sheet (US return)

| Allowance | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Goods for personal use | $800 duty-free |
| Alcohol / tobacco | Standard US limits |
| Declare | If exceeding $800 aggregate gifts/souvenirs |
| Watches/jewelry | Declare if >$800 total |
