Montrez, US citizen (passport valid through 2028), solo traveler on a 14-day **optimized-experience** trip in late July–early August 2026. Interests rank nature > food > history, with anime/gaming as optional evening add-ons. Budget is **$5,000 all-in** with splurges spread evenly across hubs—not concentrated in Tokyo. ## 1. Traveler Profile Summary

→ See full detail: [[01-traveler-profile]]

Structured solo itinerary for one country in 14 days: Tokyo (5 nights) → Takayama/Alps (2) → Kyoto (4) → Osaka (2) → depart KIX Aug 6. Moderate physical pace—several miles daily but no extreme athletics. Per-day envelope ~$357; strategic splurges reserved for Hida beef, one obanzai dinner, and an Alps ryokan night. Heat and pre-Obon domestic travel volume flagged as seasonal constraints.

## 2. Route

→ See full detail: [[02-route-optimization]]

**Open-jaw**: Fly **IN** NRT or HND, fly **OUT** KIX—saves ~2.5 hrs and ¥14,000+ vs backtracking to Tokyo. Geographic sweep: urban acclimation → cooler Alps nature block → Kyoto history → Osaka food finale. Transit ratio **18%** (under 20% threshold). Alternatives documented: Alps-after-Kyoto (Option B), drop Osaka (Option C), add Kanazawa (Option D).

## 3. Visas & Entry

→ See full detail: [[03-immigration-entry]]

**No visa required** for US citizens—visa-free tourism up to 90 days. Complete **Visit Japan Web** within 72 hours of departure for immigration/customs QR codes. No mandatory vaccinations; Hep A and flu recommended. Re-verify entry rules at [travel.state.gov](https://travel.state.gov) within 30 days of Jul 24 departure.

## 7. Transport & Money

→ See full detail: [[07-transport-money]]

**Recommendation: skip 7-day JR Pass**—individual Hikari + Hida reserved tickets (~¥30,800 / ~$205) save ~¥19,200 vs ¥50,000 pass unless adding heavy JR side trips (Nikko, Himeji). IC card (Suica/PASMO) for all local transit; book SmartEX/Eki-net seats 4 weeks out. On-ground transport target ≤10% of budget (~$500). Airport: N'EX or Keisei (NRT), Keikyu (HND); departure via Nankai Rapi:t or Haruka to KIX.

> ⚠️ **Cross-doc note**: Steps 02, 04, and 15 reference JR Pass activation Jul 29; Step 07 math favors individual tickets. Re-run pass math before purchase—see [[07-transport-money]].

## 13. Etiquette

→ See full detail: [[13-etiquette]]

First Japan trip—core norms: no tipping, quiet trains, shoes off on tatami, pocket trash until konbini bin. Essential phrases: *sumimasen*, *arigatou gozaimasu*, *hitori desu* (table for one). Regional tone shifts: Tokyo direct → Alps hospitality → Kyoto formality → Osaka casual. Carry hand towel; mask optional but polite if coughing on crowded trains.

## 14. Health & Safety

→ See full detail: [[14-health-safety]]

Primary risks: **heat/humidity** (30–35°C lowlands), **typhoon season** (KIX Aug 6 window), pre-Obon crowds. Recommended vaccines: Hep A, flu, routine boosters; JE optional for this urban/trail itinerary. Travel insurance mandatory (World Nomads ~$130–160)—medical evacuation + typhoon interruption. Japan healthcare excellent but upfront billing common; carry ORS, SPF 50, electrolytes for heat days.
