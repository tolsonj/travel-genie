## Executive Summary

**Tokyo (Days 1-4):** Urban immersion, anime culture, Mt Takao hiking warmup

**Kyoto (Days 8-11):** Temple circuit, Arashiyama bamboo forest, Nara deer park day trip  
**Osaka (Days 12-14):** Food crawl finale (takoyaki, okonomiyaki, kushikatsu), urban shopping, airport departure

### Transport (6.7% of Budget, $336)

**Long-distance:** Individual train tickets (NO JR Pass — not cost-effective for this route)  
- Tokyo → Takayama: ¥12,000 ($82)
- Takayama → Kyoto: ¥11,000 ($75)
- Kyoto → Osaka: ¥580 ($4)

**Local transit:** IC card (Suica/Pasmo) for subway/bus, load ¥3,000 initially, refill as needed  
**Special:** Kamikochi bus pre-book (¥2,600 = $18 round-trip)

**Strategy:** Skip JR Pass (saves $140), use IC card for local convenience, pre-book long-distance seats 1 month ahead

→ See full detail: [[07-transport-money]]

### Money Management

**Currency:** Japanese Yen (¥), ~¥145 per $1 USD  
**ATM strategy:** 7-Eleven ATMs (24h, English interface, accept foreign cards)  
- Day 1: Withdraw ¥40,000 ($275)
- Day 6: Withdraw ¥30,000 ($200)
- Day 10: Withdraw ¥30,000 ($200)

**Payment split:** 70% cash, 20% IC card, 10% credit card  
**Tipping:** NO TIPPING (insulting in Japan, bill = final price)

**Dynamic Currency Conversion trap:** Always choose "JPY" at ATMs (not USD) to avoid 3-5% markup

→ See full detail: [[07-transport-money]]

### Etiquette & Language

**10 daily rules:** Shoes off indoors, quiet on trains, no eating while walking, bow as greeting, chopstick taboos, slurp noodles, no tipping, queue culture, inside voice, carry your trash  
**10 survival phrases:** Konnichiwa (hello), arigatou gozaimasu (thank you), sumimasen (excuse me/sorry), wakarimasen (I don't understand), ikura desu ka (how much?)

**Cultural immersion:** "Osusume wa nan desu ka?" (what do you recommend?) = conversation starter, shows respect for local expertise
