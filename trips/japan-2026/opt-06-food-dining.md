### Food safety

| Topic | Japan |
|-------|-------|
| Tap water | Safe to drink |
| Street food | High hygiene; busy stalls = turnover |
| Raw food | Sushi/sashimi standard; trust busy counters |
| Summer | Carry electrolytes; avoid unrefrigerated display cases in extreme heat |

### Dining customs

| Custom | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Tipping | **Not done** — can offend |
| Meal timing | Lunch 11:30–2; dinner 6–9 (earlier than Spain, later than US lunch) |
| Chopsticks | Never stick upright in rice; don't pass food chopstick-to-chopstick |
| Solo | Welcomed; say "hitori desu" (one person) |
| Cash | Some small shops cash-only; IC card works at chains |
