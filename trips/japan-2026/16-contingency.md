# Contingency Plans

## 10 Scenarios + Recovery Protocols

### 1. Weather Ruins Outdoor Day
**Scenario:** Typhoon/heavy rain on Day 6 (Kamikochi hike) or Day 3 (Mt Takao)

**Recovery:**
- Indoor alternative: Museums (Tokyo National Museum, Takayama Jinya, Kyoto temples with covered halls)
- Reschedule: Swap Day 6 with Day 7 (Folk Village = indoor-friendly)
- Refund: Kamikochi bus tickets refundable if road closed (force majeure)

**Contacts:** Nohi Bus: +81-577-32-1688

---

### 2. Missed Train Between Regions
**Scenario:** Oversleep, miss Tokyo→Takayama train (Day 5)

**Recovery:**
- Next train: Departures every 2-3 hours (arrive Takayama 2-3h later)
- Hotel: Call to notify late check-in (+81-577-XX-XXXX, saved in phone)
- Itinerary adjustment: Skip Day 5 Takayama Jinya, proceed directly to sake brewery walk

---

### 3. Sick in One Region, Need to Move to Next
**Scenario:** Food poisoning Day 10 (Kyoto), scheduled to move to Osaka Day 12

**Recovery:**
- Stay in Kyoto: Extend hotel 1 day, delay Osaka move to Day 13
- Doctor: Kyoto City Hospital (English line: +81-75-311-5311)
- Transport: Shinkansen refundable if unused (exchange at ticket office)

---

### 4. Visa/Entry Denied at Border (N/A)
**Not applicable:** Single-country trip, no visa required (US passport holder)

---

### 5. Lost Passport in Foreign Country
**Scenario:** Passport stolen/lost in Tokyo (Day 2)

**Recovery:**
1. File police report: Nearest koban (police box), get report copy
2. US Embassy Tokyo: +81-3-3224-5000, schedule emergency passport appointment (1-2 days)
3. Cancel travel: Rebook flights with emergency passport (may require extra fees $200-400)
4. Accommodations: Capsule hotels may require passport at check-in (show police report + embassy appointment confirmation)

**Prevention:** Keep photocopy separate from passport (see [[14-health-safety]])

---

### 6. Political Unrest / Travel Ban
**Likelihood:** Very low (Japan stable)

**Recovery:**
- US Embassy alerts: Sign up for STEP (Smart Traveler Enrollment Program) before departure
- Evacuation: Follow embassy instructions, embassy will coordinate flights if necessary

---

### 7. Group Member Splits from Itinerary (N/A)
**Not applicable:** Solo traveler

---

### 8. Budget Overrun Mid-Trip
**Scenario:** Spent $3,000 by Day 7 (halfway), only $2,000 remaining (should have $2,500)

**Recovery:**
- Cut costs Days 8-14:
  - Skip kaiseki splurge (Day 9): Save $80
  - Convenience store meals: Save $30/day × 7 days = $210
  - Skip Ghibli Museum (Day 4): Save $10
  - Walk instead of subway where possible: Save $20
- **Total savings:** $320 → Back under budget ✅

---

### 9. Luggage Lost on Flight
**Scenario:** Checked bag doesn't arrive Haneda (Day 1)

**Recovery:**
- Airline claim: File lost luggage report at airport (airline provides tracking number)
- Survival kit: Carry-on has 1 change of clothes, toiletries, medications ✅
- Buy essentials: Uniqlo (Tokyo, affordable clothes $20-50), drugstore (toiletries ¥1,000)
- Reimbursement: Travel insurance covers up to $1,000 baggage delay/loss

**Prevention:** Pack essentials in carry-on (see [[15-packing]])

---

### 10. Natural Disaster / Strike Closes Transit
**Scenario:** Earthquake closes shinkansen Day 8 (Takayama→Kyoto)

**Recovery:**
- Alternative route: Bus (Nohi Bus Takayama→Nagoya 4h, then local train Nagoya→Kyoto 1h)
- Delay arrival: Notify Kyoto hotel, extend Takayama hotel 1 night
- Refund: Shinkansen ticket refundable if service disrupted (force majeure)

**Earthquake protocol:**
- Indoors: Drop, cover, hold (under table)
- Outdoors: Move away from buildings
- Tsunami: Japan has excellent warning systems (sirens + phone alerts)
