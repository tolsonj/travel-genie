# Traveler Profile

### Core Identifiers

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Traveler** | Montrez |
| **Countries** | Japan (single-country) |
| **Travel window** | Late July – early August 2026 (~14 days; itinerary anchor **Jul 24 – Aug 6**) |
| **Travel party** | Solo |
| **Decision authority** | Solo (full autonomy) |
| **Citizenship** | **US** — passport valid through **2028** |

### Style & Pace

- **Style**: Adventurous planner — structured days with room to wander; “optimized experience” (efficient moves, not rushed)
- **Pace**: Moderate; one anchor activity per day; avoid intense athletic blocks
- **Authenticity**: Prefer local gems over tourist traps; food tours and cultural experiences welcome
- **Ideal day**: Good weather, great food, beautiful scenery, unhurried exploration

### Budget

| Category | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Total** | **$5,000 USD** (all-in: flights, lodging, food, activities, local transport, buffer) |
| **Allocation intent** | Spread across hubs—not concentrated in one city |
| **Per-day envelope** | ~$357/day average; plan ~$1,000–1,400 for intl flights open-jaw, ~$3,600–4,000 on ground |
| **Splurge levers** | One kaiseki or omakase slot, one Alps ryokan/onsen night, one paid nature experience (Kamikochi day or guided hike) |

### Interests (ranked — planning priority)

1. **Nature** — Alps, river walks, parks, coastal or garden scenery
2. **Food** — markets, izakaya, regional specialties (Hida beef, Kyoto obanzai, Osaka street food)
3. **History** — temples, castles, preserved towns (not museum marathons)
4. Scenic drives *(secondary — use Alps bus/train scenery)*
5. Local culture
6. Anime *(optional evening; not core daylight)*
7. Video games *(optional; Akihabara/Nintendo if budget/time)*

### Physical Profile

- High fitness; comfortable walking several miles daily
- Enjoys hiking but **not** extreme mountaineering
- No stated mobility limitations

### Transit Experience & Preferences

- Limited multi-country experience — favor **clear hub changes**, reserved seats, and app-based transit (Google Maps + Japan Transit Planner; Suica/PASMO)
- Long drives acceptable if scenery rewards (Alps approach qualifies)

### Deal-Breakers

| Deal-breaker | Planning implication |
|---|---|
| **Massive crowds** | Early starts; weekday temples; skip peak-hour Shibuya photo ops; book timed entries; use Takayama/Kamikochi as relief; avoid scheduling Kyoto’s Gion at sunset on a Saturday in peak season |

### Seasonal Constraints (late Jul – early Aug)

- **Heat/humidity** in Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka — outdoor blocks before 10:00 or after 16:00; midday indoor/AC
- **Rain** — afternoon thunderstorms possible; daily rainy backup required (Step 04)
- **Pre-Obon travel** — domestic volume rises late July; full Obon (typically mid-Aug) is mostly **after** this window but still plan for busy Shinkansen Sundays

### Key Planning Constraints

1. Single-country **hub sequence**: Tokyo → Japanese Alps (Takayama base) → Kyoto → Osaka → depart **KIX**
2. **Open-jaw flights**: **NRT or HND in**, **KIX out** — no backtracking to Tokyo for departure
3. Budget requires **strategic splurges**, not 14 days of luxury lodging
4. Nature interest elevates **Alps allocation** to at least 2 nights
5. Crowd aversion elevates **timing discipline** over attraction count
