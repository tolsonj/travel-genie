### Neighborhood strategy — Los Cabos corridor

| Area | Role on trip | Why for this party | Logistics |
|------|--------------|--------------------|-----------|
| **San José del Cabo** | Culture + calm dinners | Art District galleries, Mission, Jazmin's/Mi Cocina — fits history + food without marina crush | 15–25 min to Corridor depending on property |
| **Tourist Corridor** | Beach walks + golf | Palmilla/Chileno Bay courses; resort-beach mornings; profile "perfect day" | Uber or rental car; avoid midday heat |
| **Cabo San Lucas marina** | Arch boats + Aug 10 anchor | El Arco departures; Metate/Los Deseos dinners — go **after 4 PM** | Marina parking tight — Uber drop-off preferred |
| **East Cape / Todos Santos** | Day trips only | Lower crowds than downtown Cabo — see [[12-hidden-gems]] | Full-day rental car |
