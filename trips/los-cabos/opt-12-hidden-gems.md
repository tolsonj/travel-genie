### Only-here experiences

1. **Flora Farms** — 25-acre organic farm in the foothills; meals from on-site gardens (Baja farm-to-table origin story).
2. **Todos Santos** — Pacific-side artist colony where the desert meets surf — different ecosystem from Sea of Cortez east coast.
3. **Chocolate clams on the East Cape** — harvested locally; eat same day at palapa shacks locals use.
