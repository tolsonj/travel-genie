### Route Alternatives

**Option B: Add Todos Santos Day Trip (Aug 9 or Aug 12)**
- Same base; ~1 hr drive each way to Todos Santos (bohemian town, art galleries, beach)
- Pro: History + food + cooler microclimate
- Con: Full day in car; less pool time
- **Vote:** Swap for Sun nature day OR Wed split day

**Option C: Stay Corridor-Only (Max Pool / Min Driving)**
- Skip Todos Santos and Cabo Pulmo; focus on San José + Cabo San Lucas + resort
- Pro: Lowest transit, best heat management
- Con: Miss east-coast snorkeling
- **Vote:** Recommended if Aug 9 golf wins over Cabo Pulmo

**Recommendation:** **Primary route** — corridor base with **Aug 9 vote** between golf and Cabo Pulmo.
