# Master Itinerary

## Reasoning

**Pace:** Relaxed cultural family of four — one anchor per day, late brunch default, **70% scheduled / 30% open**. Vote on forks (golf vs Cabo Pulmo Aug 9; Todos Santos vs pool Aug 12) — majority of four rules.

**Interest mapping:** San José del Cabo = history + Art District + farm-to-table food; Cabo San Lucas = Arch nature + marina; corridor = golf, desert hikes, beach perfect-day moments.

**Heat/crowd protocol:** Outdoor sights **before 10 AM** or **after 5 PM**; pool/resort **11 AM–3 PM**; Arch boat **7 AM** departure to beat cruise-ship crowds.

**Soft day:** Aug 7 arrival — no anchor before 4 PM; pool and unpack only.

**Special days:** Aug 11 Audrey birthday — spa morning, splurge dinner (Flora's Field Kitchen or Edith's), sunset sail, gift ideas embedded. Aug 12 adult activities — tequila tasting or cooking class for parents; daughters parallel beach/resort track.

**Flights BOOKED:** Placeholder times until user provides PNR. Typical ATL→SJD ~4.5 hr; SJD→ATL similar.

**Lodging:** Inspirato per [[profile]] — single base entire stay.
