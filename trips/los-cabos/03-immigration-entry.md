# Immigration & Entry

## Reasoning

All four travelers are **US citizens** entering **Mexico** for tourism via **SJD (Los Cabos International)**. Mexico's entry rules for US passport holders remain straightforward in 2026: **no visa required** for stays up to 180 days; **FMM (Forma Migratoria Múltiple / tourist card)** required but typically issued at port of entry or via airline/in-app pre-registration. Passport must be valid for duration of stay; **6 months beyond travel date** is the safe standard used by airlines and recommended by US State Department.

No ETIAS (Europe), no ESTA (US outbound). Mexico does not require proof of onward travel for US citizens in practice, but holding return ATL tickets satisfies any officer inquiry. Health: **no vaccinations required** for entry from the US; CDC recommends routine vaccines and travel-health awareness for food/water (not mandatory).

Validation focus: confirm FMM process at SJD (electronic vs paper), ensure daughters' passports valid if teen/minor (no special consent letter needed for both parents traveling together), and note Mexico's 2025–2026 digital entry initiatives that may replace paper FMM at major airports.

### Red Flag Warnings

🚨 **Do not lose FMM stub** — exit denied or fine without it. Store in passport wallet separate from checked bags.

🚨 **6-month passport rule** — airlines may deny boarding if expiry within 6 months of return. Check Denver & Kennedy passports especially.

🚨 **Customs declaration** — declare food, plants, >$10,000 USD equivalent. Personal electronics OK.
