## Reasoning

**Profile constraint:** Lodging is **confirmed via Inspirato** — profile explicitly skips hotel MCP search. Property, dates, and amenities come from the [Inspirato itinerary link](https://mytrip.inspirato.com/trip/itinerary/k5SSks4AdZ1qBJLOAIcszgXAwMDEED2t0IDQPFX9vOPz8blppkle), not third-party rate shopping.

**Neighborhood fit:** Los Cabos splits into **San José del Cabo** (walkable historic core, Art District, calmer evenings) and **Cabo San Lucas** (marina, Arch boat departures, nightlife). The **Tourist Corridor** between them fits the profile perfect-day formula — late brunch, beach walks, nature views — with golf at Palmilla/Chileno and low-crowd mornings if you anchor activities before 10 AM.

**Party of four:** Two adults + two daughters — verify bedroom count, sofa bed, and pool access in Inspirato confirmation; no third-adult surcharge scenario.

**Heat/crowd protocol:** AC midday breaks at the residence; schedule San José galleries and golf early; Cabo marina after 4 PM when cruise-ship day-trippers depart.

**Budget:** Inspirato allocation is membership/pre-paid — treat on-trip lodging cash spend as **$0** and reserve the $8K budget for flights (booked), transport, food, golf, and experiences.
