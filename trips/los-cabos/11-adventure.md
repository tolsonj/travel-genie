# Adventure

## Reasoning

**Profile:** Party of four — adults + two daughters — **no physical limitations**; golf and nature are top interests. Moderate adventure OK with heat protocol (early starts).

**Pace:** One adventure anchor per day — golf OR hike OR snorkel, not all three same day.

**Season:** Early August — hot (90°F+) and humid; **dawn–10 AM** for golf/hike; water activities with reef-safe sunscreen.

**Adult suggestions:** Aug 12 spa + dinner; optional ATV/desert for adults while kids pool; Cabo Pulmo is full-day — vote vs relaxed birthday recovery.

**MCP spa:** Sparitual Los Cabos **5.0★** — see [[spa-comparison]].
