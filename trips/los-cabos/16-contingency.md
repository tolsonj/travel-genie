# Contingency Plans

## Reasoning

**Single-hub trip:** All contingencies resolve within the Los Cabos corridor — no cross-border rerouting, no visa-denial complexity. Primary failure modes: **ATL↔SJD flight disruption**, **hurricane/tropical storm**, **heat-related illness**, **lost passport**, and **activity cancellations** (Arch boat, sail, golf).

**Itinerary source:** Day allocation from [[02-route-optimization]] — Aug 7 soft arrival through Aug 13 departure; Audrey birthday Aug 11 is immovable anchor.

**Support stack:** Inspirato concierge for property-level issues; US Consular Agency Cabo San Lucas for passport services; travel insurance hotline for medical/evacuation and trip delay.

## Output

| Trigger | Heavy rain, lightning, high surf, tropical storm watch |

| **Aug 9** (golf/nature vote) | Pivot: resort spa, cooking demo at property, Shoppes at Palmilla, San José museum |
