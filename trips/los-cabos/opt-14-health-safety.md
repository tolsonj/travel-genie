### August heat & hydration protocol

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Outdoor window** | Activities **before 10 AM** or **after 5 PM** — per [[01-traveler-profile]] |
| **Pool/resort block** | **11 AM–3 PM** — AC, shade, zero exertion |
| **Hydration target** | **3–4 L water/person/day** in August heat; start before feeling thirsty |
| **Electrolytes** | 1–2 packets/day after golf, hike, or boat — not soda/alcohol substitute |
| **Alcohol + heat** | Limit midday; tequila tasting (Aug 12) **after** pool block, with food + water |
| **Heat illness signs** | Headache, nausea, confusion, stop sweating → shade, cool water, call **911** if worsening |
| **Clothing** | UPF 50+, light colors, wide-brim hat, sunglasses — see [[15-packing]] |

### Sun protection

| Context | Protection |
|---------|------------|
| Beach / pool | SPF 50+ reef-safe; reapply every 2 hr; lip balm SPF |
| Golf (Aug 9 vote) | Hat with neck flap, sleeves optional UPF; tee time **≤ 7:30 AM** |
| Arch boat (Aug 10) | **7 AM departure** — still high UV on water; long sleeves + hat |
| Desert / Mt Solmar | Closed-toe shoes + hat; no exposed shoulders midday |
| Daughters | Rash guards for snorkel; parental reapply enforcement |

### Water safety

| Activity | Risk | Mitigation |
|----------|------|------------|
| Resort / corridor beaches | Rip currents, strong undertow on Pacific side | Swim only flagged areas; heed red flags |
| Arch boat tour | Sun, seasickness, boarding | Life jackets provided; take dramamine if prone; book operator with safety record |
| Cabo Pulmo snorkel (vote) | Open water, boat transfer | Licensed operator only; buddy system; no solo snorkel |
| Sunset sail (Aug 11) | Evening wind, alcohol | Eat first; life jackets; flexible cancel if storm watch |
| **General** | Do not drink **ocean** water; shower after salt water | — |

**Tap water:** **Not for drinking** in Los Cabos — bottled or filtered only. Ice at reputable restaurants generally safe; street stalls use caution. Brush teeth with bottled water if sensitive stomachs.

### Hurricane & storm season awareness

| Topic | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Season | Eastern Pacific hurricane season **May 15 – Nov 30**; August is active |
| Local risk | Los Cabos can see tropical storms, heavy rain, high surf — direct hits less frequent than mainland but possible |
| Pre-trip | Monitor [NHC Eastern Pacific](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/) T-7d through departure |
| On-trip | Enable weather alerts; Inspirato/concierge for property protocols |
| Activity impact | Arch boats, sails, East Cape drives **first to cancel** — rebook or pivot to spa/indoor |
| Insurance | Confirm **trip interruption** and **cancel for any reason** (if purchased) covers named storms |
| Evacuation | Follow property + local authority instructions; US Consular Agency for citizen services |

### Golf injury prevention (Jack / family round)

| Area | Prevention |
|------|------------|
| Heat | Dawn tee time; cart if walking 18 in August; electrolytes at turn |
| Sun | Hat, sunscreen, reapply at hole 9 |
| Swing / back | 10-min dynamic warm-up; don't hero-drive on first tee cold |
| Feet | Golf shoes with grip; watch for desert critters near rough |
| Hydration | Water every 3 holes; **no alcohol** until round complete |
| Cart safety | Seat belts on hilly courses (Palmilla, Chileno) |
| Insurance | Confirm policy covers golf-related injury / cart accident |

### Health — Mexico (Los Cabos corridor)

| Topic | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Common illnesses | Traveler's diarrhea, heat exhaustion, sunburn, jellyfish sting (rare) |
| Food safety | Cooked food hot; peel fruit; avoid unwashed salad at street stalls |
| Pharmacies | Farmacias del Ahorro, Similares — San José + Cabo; many meds OTC |
| Nearest hospitals | **Hospital H+ Los Cabos** (San José); **BlueNet Hospitals** (Cabo San Lucas) |
| Ambulance | **911** (Mexico unified emergency) |
| Payment | Cash or card; travel insurance direct-bill if policy supports — call hotline first |
