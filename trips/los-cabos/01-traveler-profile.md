# Traveler Profile

## Reasoning

**Single-country corridor fit:** One country (Mexico), one hub (Los Cabos corridor — San José del Cabo + Cabo San Lucas) over **7 nights** is ideal for a relaxed family of four. No border friction, minimal transit, maximum pool/beach recovery time. The corridor delivers golf, desert/nature, colonial history (San José), and Baja food without multi-city logistics.

**August window:** Los Cabos in early August is **hot and dry** (~32–35°C / 90–95°F) with **hurricane-season tail** risk (Pacific storms possible but less frequent than Sep–Oct). Crowds are moderate — summer family travel, but not spring-break peak. Profile deal-breakers (heat, crowds) addressed via **dawn outdoor blocks**, **pool/resort midday**, and **avoiding cruise-ship mornings at the Arch**.

**Travel party:** Four US citizens — John (Jack), wife Audrey, daughters Denver & Kennedy. **Family of four**, Main cabin from **ATL**, **4 passengers**. Rideshare/shuttle preferred over unregulated street taxis. Vote when opinions differ — all four weigh in, majority rules; planner flags forks.

**Budget sanity:** **$8,000** all-in ≈ **$1,143/day** for 4 people. Flights **already booked** (Main cabin ATL↔SJD); lodging **pre-booked via Inspirato** — remaining budget covers ground transport, dining, golf, tours, Audrey birthday splurge, and contingency. Tighter than luxury multi-country trips but workable with prepaid lodging and one splurge day (Aug 11).

**Interest weighting:** Golf 25% · Nature 25% · Food 25% · History 25% — corridor covers all four without over-scheduling.

**Special constraints:** Audrey's **birthday Aug 11 (Tue)** requires dedicated planning in [[04-master-itinerary]]. Adult-oriented activity suggestions welcome (tequila tasting, spa, sunset sail) while daughters have parallel beach/pool options.
