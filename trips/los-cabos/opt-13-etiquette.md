# Etiquette

### Mexico — Los Cabos

#### Daily etiquette (10 norms)

| # | Norm | Context |
|---|------|---------|
| 1 | Greet with **buenos días / tardes** before asking questions | Shopkeepers, golf staff |
| 2 | **15–20% tip** at sit-down restaurants | Cards often have tip line |
| 3 | Beach vendors — polite **no gracias** without engaging | Firm but friendly |
| 4 | Don't drink tap water | Bottled/filtered only |
| 5 | Dress modestly at **Mission** | Shoulders/knees covered |
| 6 | Ask before drone flights | Restricted near airport/Arch |
| 7 | Bargain gently at markets — not fixed-menu restaurants | San José mercado |
| 8 | Pace is **mañana culture** — allow extra time | Tee times excepted |
| 9 | Keep voice down in residential Pedregal/Solmar | Respect homeowners |
| 10 | Sunscreen before beach — locals respect shade breaks | Model for kids |

#### Survival phrases (10) — Spanish

| English | Spanish (phonetic) |
|---------|-------------------|
| Hello | Hola (OH-lah) |
| Thank you | Gracias (GRAH-see-ahs) |
| Please | Por favor (por fah-VOR) |
| Excuse me | Disculpe (dees-KOOL-peh) |
| I don't speak Spanish well | No hablo español bien (no AH-blo es-pahn-YOL bee-EN) |
| How much? | ¿Cuánto cuesta? (KWAN-toh KWES-tah) |
| The check, please | La cuenta, por favor |
| Not spicy | Sin chile, por favor (seen CHEE-leh) |
| Where is…? | ¿Dónde está…? (DON-deh es-TAH) |
| Goodbye | Adiós (ah-DYOHS) |

#### Beach vendor etiquette

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Make eye contact + smile when declining | Touch merchandise then walk away |
| Set boundary once — "No, gracias" | Engage in long haggle if not buying |
| Buy from one vendor if you like craft | Flash large bills on open beach |
| Tip palapa servers 15% | Eat uncooked shellfish from ambulant carts |

#### Safety & respectful tourism

| Topic | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| Night driving | Highway 1 OK; avoid unlit side roads |
| ATMs | Use bank branches · daytime |
| Beach swimming | Heed flag colors · rip currents on Pacific side |
| Wildlife | Don't touch whale sharks if seasonal tour (not Aug peak) |
| Photography | Ask locals before portraits |
| Environment | Reef-safe sunscreen · no coral souvenirs |

#### Supporting locals

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| Eat at San José **fondas** | Cash to family kitchens |
| Buy art from **galleries** not mass souvenir | Aug 8 Art District |
| Tip golf caddies **100–150 MXN**/bag | Expected at Palmilla/Chileno |
| Use licensed operators only | Arch boats · ATV · Pulmo snorkel |
