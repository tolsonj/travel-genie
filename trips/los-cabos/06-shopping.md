# Shopping

## Reasoning

**Profile fit:** Shopping is **secondary** — interests rank golf, nature, food, history ahead of retail. Light souvenir and gallery browsing only; no dedicated shopping days.

**Itinerary slots:** Aug 8 San José Art District naturally covers galleries; Aug 11 birthday may include a small gift purchase; avoid Aug 10 marina midday crush.

**Social anchor:** Puerto Paraíso marina mall IG reels, Luxury Avenue designer row, San José Thursday Art Walk TikTok loops — useful for rainy-day AC, not trip drivers.

**Customs:** US return — $800/person duty-free; declare crafts over allowance; no restricted agricultural products (fresh cheese, plants).
