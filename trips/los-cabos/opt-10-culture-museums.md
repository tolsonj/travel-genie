### Deep history (3 facts — Baja California Sur)

1. **Mission San José del Cabo** (1730) was founded by Jesuits to convert the Pericú people — the current church dates from the 1940s reconstruction after hurricane damage.
2. **El Arco** marks the **Land's End** where the Pacific meets the Sea of Cortez — the granite arch was carved by wind and sea; the area was accessible only by boat until modern marina development.
3. **San José Art District** grew from a 2010s gallery collective — Thursday Art Walk draws locals, not just cruise passengers, when timed off-season.
