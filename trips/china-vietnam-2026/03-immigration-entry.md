# Immigration & Entry

## Reasoning

US citizens visiting Hong Kong (SAR), Mainland China (Shenzhen), and Vietnam. Key facts as of 2026: (1) China extended its unilateral visa-free policy for US citizens to 15 days in 2024–2025; this is now codified but should be verified as policies can revert. (2) Vietnam introduced 90-day visa-free for US citizens in 2023 and this remains in effect for 2026. (3) Hong Kong is separate from Mainland China for immigration purposes — you clear HK immigration at HKG, then clear Mainland China immigration again at Lo Wu (Shenzhen border). This is a critical distinction many travelers miss. No ETIAS (this is Asia), no UK ETA needed.

## Output

### Country-by-Country Entry Checklist

---

#### 🇭🇰 Hong Kong SAR

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Visa | None — US citizens get 90 days visa-free |
| Passport validity | Must be valid for duration of stay; 6 months recommended |
| Entry form | Landing declaration may be required (in-flight form) |
| Health requirements | None as of 2026 |
| Duration | 90 days |

**Notes:** HKG is your international point of entry. Clear HK immigration first. If transiting to Mainland China, you will clear a **separate** Mainland border at Lo Wu or Lok Ma Chau.

---

#### 🇨🇳 Mainland China (Shenzhen)

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Visa | **Visa-free for US citizens — 15 days** (unilateral policy, active as of 2026) |
| Passport validity | 6 months beyond travel date |
| Blank pages | 1 page minimum |
| Entry point | Lo Wu Border Control Point (MTR East Rail) or Shenzhen Bay Port |
| Duration | 15 days per visit |
| Border hours | Lo Wu: 06:30–24:00 daily |

⚠️ **CRITICAL:** China's 15-day visa-free policy was announced in 2024 and extended through 2025; **verify current status at china.embassy.gov or travel.state.gov before departing** as policies can be revoked without advance notice.

⚠️ **Dupe/counterfeit shopping alert:** China does not arrest buyers of counterfeit goods for personal use, but exporting counterfeit goods is illegal. Border guards at Lo Wu occasionally inspect bags departing Shenzhen into Hong Kong. See Step 08 (Customs) for full strategy.

**Application:** No pre-registration needed for visa-free entry — simply present US passport at border control.

---

#### 🇻🇳 Vietnam

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Visa | **Visa-free for US citizens — 90 days** (since Aug 2023, confirmed 2026) |
| Passport validity | 6 months beyond last day of stay |
| Blank pages | 1 page minimum |
| Entry ports | Noi Bai (Hanoi), Tan Son Nhat (HCMC), Da Nang |
| Duration | 90 days (single or multiple entry) |
| E-visa option | Available at evisa.xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn ($25, 90 days) — optional for safety |

**Recommendation:** Despite visa-free status, consider obtaining Vietnam e-visa before travel as a backup (if policy changes or questions arise at border). Processing: 3 business days.

**Health/vaccinations:** No mandatory vaccinations for entry, but hepatitis A, typhoid, and routine vaccines are strongly recommended by CDC.

### Red Flag Warnings

🚨 **China visa-free policy is NOT guaranteed** — it has been extended multiple times and could be paused. Have a contingency (apply for a standard tourist visa at Chinese Embassy/Consulate in Atlanta, 4–6 weeks processing).

🚨 **Two separate immigration checkpoints** between HKG and Shenzhen — both Hong Kong customs AND Mainland China customs. Do not confuse them.

🚨 **US Citizens cannot use Mainland China automated e-gates** — must use staffed counters. Build extra time at Lo Wu.

### Backup Plan — Visa Denial

If China visa-free denied (rare but possible):
- Return to Hong Kong (same day, no issue)
- Apply for China group tour visa or single-entry tourist visa at Consulate
- Restructure: spend extra day in Hong Kong, fly directly from HKG to Hanoi
