# Shopping

## Reasoning

**Itinerary scope:** Hong Kong (Sep 3–5) for all China shopping — dupes/electronics/purses + sneakers; Hanoi food-first — minimal shopping; Hội An / Đà Nẵng (Sep 11) authorized sneakers + lantern souvenirs. **Tailored suits removed. Shenzhen/Guangzhou removed** to avoid a mainland China visa — Hong Kong covers every category. Shopping concentrated on non-transit days; Vietnam exit Sep 12.

**Social anchor:** Hong Kong dominates dupes/electronics/sneaker content — Mong Kok Sin Tat Plaza, Ladies Market, Fa Yuen Sneaker Street, Sham Shui Po, Causeway Bay malls. Vietnam: Đà Nẵng Vincom for brand sneakers.

**Profile fit:** $30K budget with Vietnam splurge — allocate shopping separately from lodging; HK dupes/electronics/purses are discretionary; Vietnam retail sneakers ($100–200/pair) remain a priority spend.

**Customs risk:** Counterfeit watches/bags are **seizable at US border** even for personal use — declare honestly; electronics count toward $800 duty-free allowance; keep receipts and remove packaging to show personal use.

**VAT:** Hong Kong has no VAT/GST refund scheme for tourists. Vietnam 10% VAT — tourist refund at DAD departure for qualifying retail.

### VAT refund summary

| Country | Rate | Min purchase | Refund process |
|---------|-----:|--------------|----------------|
| Hong Kong | 0% (no VAT) | N/A | No tourist VAT refund — price is final |
| Vietnam | 10% | VND 2,000,000 / store (participating retailers) | Tax refund form + passport at shop; claim at **DAD airport** on departure **Sep 12** |

**Note:** Street markets and most electronics stalls in HK do not participate in any refund scheme. Vietnam VAT refund applies to registered retail (e.g., Vincom sneaker purchase).

### Customs/duty cheat sheet (US return via ATL)

| Item | Rule |
|------|------|
| Personal-use goods allowance | **$800** duty-free per person (combine family if traveling together — verify current CBP household rules) |
| Counterfeit watches / handbags / purses | **Prohibited** — CBP may **seize** regardless of personal use; **declare** if bringing; do not misrepresent as authentic |
| Counterfeit risk | Fines up to $10,000+ per item possible; criminal referral for commercial quantities |
| Electronics (phones, tablets, cameras, headphones) | Declare if aggregate value exceeds $800; duty ~3% on excess; keep receipts |
| Personal-use declaration | Used electronics (packaging removed) + worn sneakers support personal-use claim |
| Gifts for others | Count toward $800 combined exemption |
| Commercial quantity | Multiple identical watches/bags/phones may be treated as resale — limit to 1–2 per type |
| Agriculture / food | Declare all — Vietnamese coffee, dried fruit may need inspection |
| CBP form | Complete customs declaration honestly on arrival ATL (Day 13) |

**Practical strategy:** Prioritize electronics as declared personal-use purchases with receipts. Treat HK dupes as high-risk souvenirs — limit quantity, accept seizure risk, never claim as genuine brands.
