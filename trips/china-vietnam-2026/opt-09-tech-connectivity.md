# Tech & Connectivity

### Connectivity strategy — multi-country eSIM

| Option | Provider | Coverage | Data | Cost est. | Notes |
|--------|----------|----------|------|----------:|-------|
| **Recommended** | **Airalo** HK + VN (2 profiles) | Hong Kong 5d + Vietnam 10d | 5 GB HK + 10 GB VN | ~$12 + ~$15 | Install HK before ATL departure; add VN on HAN landing |
| Alt | **Holafly** Asia regional | HK + VN single plan | Unlimited / 15d | ~$35–45 | Hotspot OK · simpler but pricier |
| Alt | **Nomad** APAC multi-country | HK + VN | 10 GB / 30d | ~$25 | One QR · check HK/VN both included |
| Backup HK | 7-Eleven / airport **CSL/Smartone** SIM | Hong Kong only | 8 GB / 7d | ~HKD 118 | Physical SIM if eSIM fails |
| Backup VN | **Viettel** tourist SIM at HAN | Vietnam only | 10 GB | ~VND 300k | Airport kiosk after immigration |

**Swap protocol:** Sep 6 HAN arrival — disable HK eSIM data, enable VN eSIM as default data.

### Power & plugs

| Topic | Hong Kong | Vietnam |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Plug type | **Type G** (3 rectangular pins) | **Type A** / **Type C** (2 round or flat pins) |
| Voltage | 220V | 220V |
| US plug fit | **No** — adapter required | **Often yes** — Type A accepts US two-pin |
| Adapter | ****Type G required** Sep 3–6 + Sep 13 | Optional Sep 6–13; bring universal anyway |
| Portable charger | 10,000 mAh — long transit Sep 1, 13 | Same — beach days + Grab maps |

### Digital security & VPN

| Topic | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| VPN — Hong Kong | **Optional** — Western apps work in HK SAR; use for banking on public WiFi |
| VPN — mainland China | **Not in itinerary** — required if day-trip added |
| VPN — Vietnam | **Not required** — optional for hotel WiFi privacy |
| Public WiFi | Avoid banking without VPN on open networks |
| Document backup | Encrypted cloud + email PDFs to self |
| 2FA | Authenticator app; save backup codes offline |
