# Tech & Connectivity

### Connectivity Strategy

#### Option A: Multi-Country eSIM (Recommended)
- **Provider**: Airalo (airalo.com) or Holafly — both support China + Vietnam in one package or separate regional eSIMs
- **China eSIM**: Mainland China data via local carrier (China Unicom or China Mobile); Airalo "China" package: ~$15–25 for 5–10 GB, 15 days
- **Vietnam eSIM**: Airalo "Vietnam" package or SE Asia regional plan: ~$10–18 for 5–10 GB
- **Install procedure**: Buy and download eSIM QR codes BEFORE traveling; activate upon landing each country
- **Note**: Keep US physical SIM in phone for iMessage/SMS/calls — eSIM provides local data

#### Option B: Physical SIMs Per Country
- **China**: China Unicom SIM available at Beijing Capital Airport arrivals hall (Gate area); ~¥100 for 10 GB, valid 30 days
- **Vietnam**: Viettel or Vietnamobile SIM at Noi Bai Airport arrivals; ~$5 for 5 GB
- **Downside**: Requires SIM swap at each border; potential phone lock issues

**Recommendation**: Airalo eSIM — manage entirely from phone before departure; no SIM swapping needed.

### China VPN — CRITICAL

> ⚠️ **Must install VPN BEFORE entering China**. Once inside, VPN app stores are blocked.

| VPN | Notes |
|-----|-------|
| **ExpressVPN** | Most reliable for China; obfuscation protocol gets through Great Firewall |
| **NordVPN** | Good alternative; Obfuscated servers feature required |
| **Astrill** | Specifically tuned for China; slightly more expensive |

**Setup checklist**:
- [ ] Download VPN app on both phones (iOS/Android) at home
- [ ] Purchase subscription; activate; test connection through Chinese servers
- [ ] Save VPN app login credentials in offline password manager (not Google)
- [ ] Set VPN to auto-connect on WiFi
- [ ] Note: VPN may slow connection by 20–40%; acceptable tradeoff for access to Google Maps, WhatsApp, Gmail

### Offline Survival Kit (Download Before Departure)

#### China
- [ ] **Baidu Maps** (百度地图) — Works offline in China; has English interface; use for navigation instead of Google Maps
- [ ] **DiDi app** — Chinese Uber; works in English; link US credit card before arriving; essential for city transport
- [ ] **WeChat** — Even as a foreigner, needed for payments (link US Visa/Mastercard); also used by many vendors and hotels
- [ ] **Google Translate** — Download Chinese (Simplified) offline pack; camera translation works for menus
- [ ] **Hotel addresses in Chinese script**: The Peninsula Beijing — 北京王府半岛酒店, 8 Jinyu Hutong, Dongcheng; save as phone note
- [ ] **Emergency contacts** (see Step 14)

#### Vietnam
- [ ] **Grab** — SE Asia's Uber; works throughout Vietnam; book in advance; better and cheaper than taxis
- [ ] **Maps.me** — Offline maps for Vietnam; better than Google Maps offline for rural areas
- [ ] **Google Maps** — Works normally in Vietnam; download offline maps for Hanoi, Đà Nẵng, Hội An
- [ ] **Google Translate** — Download Vietnamese offline pack
- [ ] **Hotel addresses in Vietnamese**: Have concierge print Vietnamese address cards for Grab/taxis

### Power & Plugs

| Country | Plug Type | Voltage | Notes |
|---------|-----------|---------|-------|
| **China** | Type A (US flat pins) + Type I (Australian diagonal) — both common | 220V | US devices mostly work (check dual voltage: look for "100–240V" on charger); bring universal adapter |
| **Vietnam** | Type A (US flat pins) + Type C (European round) — both common | 220V | Same as China |
| **Recommendation** | **One universal travel adapter** covers both countries | — | Anker or Belkin universal adapter; QC 3.0 at minimum |

**Portable battery**: Bring a 20,000 mAh USB-C power bank for Great Wall day and long Hội An walking days. Planes: check airline rules — most allow up to 100 Wh (26,800 mAh equivalent) in carry-on only.

### Digital Security

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| China public WiFi surveillance | Use VPN on ALL China WiFi; hotel WiFi is safer but still use VPN |
| Noi Bai Airport WiFi (Vietnam) | Avoid for banking; use eSIM data instead |
| Document backup | Scan all passports, visas, insurance cards → upload to iCloud/Google Drive BEFORE China entry (accessible via VPN); also email to self |
| Social media | In China, access Instagram, WhatsApp, Twitter only via VPN |
| Banking | Notify all banks and credit cards of travel to China AND Vietnam before departure; US banks regularly block international charges without notification |

### Payment Apps

| Country | What Works | What Doesn't |
|---------|------------|-------------|
| **China** | WeChat Pay (link US Visa/MC before arrival — now possible for foreigners), Alipay (similar), Cash (¥ CNY), Visa/Mastercard at 4–5 star hotels | AmEx less accepted; many local vendors are WeChat Pay / Alipay only |
| **Vietnam** | Credit cards at all 4–5 star hotels and tourist restaurants; Grab uses app payment; Cash (₫ VND) for street food and markets | WeChat Pay not used in Vietnam |

**Multi-currency card**: Charles Schwab debit card (zero ATM fees worldwide, ATM fee reimbursed) or Wise card (multi-currency, competitive exchange rates). Carry both.
