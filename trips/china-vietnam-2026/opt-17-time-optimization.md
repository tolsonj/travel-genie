### PTO Hacking

**Current dates**: September 1–14, 2026 (Tuesday to Monday)
- **Departure**: Tuesday Sept 1 — uses 1 PTO (Monday is Labor Day in US — Sept 7, 2026 is Labor Day)
- **Wait**: Labor Day 2026 is September 7 — if trip departs on Tuesday Sept 2, Labor Day weekend extends without using it as PTO
- **Revised suggestion**: Depart Monday Sept 1 (use 0 PTO; Labor Day is a holiday) → or depart Sunday Aug 31 (evening flight, arrive Beijing Tuesday) → land Beijing Sept 1 (Day 1 is essentially "free" because you crossed the date line)

**Optimal dates**:
- **Depart**: Saturday Aug 30 or Sunday Aug 31 (use no PTO for travel days; weekend departure)
- **Arrive Beijing**: Monday Sept 1 (Labor Day back home — not a PTO day)
- **Return**: Saturday Sept 13 or Sunday Sept 14 (weekend return = no PTO used for travel day)
- **Net PTO used**: 9 days (Mon Sept 1 through Fri Sept 12) instead of 10 if flying over the weekend
- **Power play**: Use 1 extra PTO day to add Sept 15 (Monday) → extends to 15 days; best extension would be an extra night in Đà Nẵng at the resort

### Jet Lag Management

**Time zone**: US Eastern (UTC-4) → Beijing (UTC+8) = 12 hours forward
US Pacific (UTC-7) → Beijing (UTC+8) = 15 hours forward

**Strategy — Outbound (US → Beijing)**:
- **Day 0 (flight)**: Stay awake until Beijing local time ~9–10 PM; force alignment to local timezone from Day 1
- **Day 1 (Beijing)**: Do NOT nap; exposure to natural light (afternoon walk to Wangfujing) resets circadian rhythm; go to bed at 10 PM local time; set alarm for 8 AM
- **Day 2**: Near-normal; slight fatigue afternoon is normal
- **Day 3 (Great Wall)**: Full energy; jet lag typically resolved by Day 3 with this protocol

**Melatonin**: 0.5–1 mg at local bedtime on Days 1 and 2 (not 5–10 mg — the marketing dose; 0.5 mg is optimal for circadian reset)

**Arrival at each new city**:
- Beijing arrival Day 1: Light, no demanding activities — CORRECT ✓
- Hanoi arrival Day 6 evening: Arrive, walk lake, sleep — CORRECT ✓
- Đà Nẵng arrival Day 8 midday: Resort check-in, beach walk, room service — CORRECT ✓

**Return jet lag**: Eastbound is easier; your body naturally extends the day; stay up one extra hour each day; resolved within 2–3 days.

### The "Power Play" — If Adding 2–3 PTO Days

**Best 2-day extension**: Add Sept 15–16 → extra 2 nights at Đà Nẵng resort
- Maximum return: Two more beach/resort days at the splurge property; Cham Islands snorkeling with better weather window (one more day of flexibility); extra Hội An day for leisurely shopping
- Cost: ~$700–1,400 extra accommodation; minimal flight change fees

**Alternative 2-day extension**: Add Sept 0 night (depart Friday Aug 28, land Beijing Sunday Aug 30)
- Extra 2 days in Beijing: Possible day trip to Xi'an (Terra Cotta Warriors — 1 hr flight; historically extraordinary)
- Return: Xi'an is worth 2 days if itinerary extends; single greatest addition to China experience
- Cost: ~$400–600 domestic China flight; ¥200 extra accommodation

**Recommendation**: Resort extension beats Xi'an addition for this profile (relaxed-cultural, beach ideal day, splurge in Vietnam).
