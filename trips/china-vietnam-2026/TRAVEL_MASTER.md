## 2. Route

→ See full detail: [[02-route-optimization]]

**Open-jaw**: Fly IN to Beijing PEK, fly OUT of Đà Nẵng DAD. Route: Beijing (5 nights) → Hanoi (2 nights) → Đà Nẵng/Hội An (5 nights). Transit ratio: **14%** (under 20% threshold). Two internal flights (Beijing→Hanoi ~4 hrs; Hanoi→Đà Nẵng ~1.25 hrs). No overland crossings.

**Alternative routes**: Option B (Shanghai instead of Beijing — more shopping, less history); Option C (Beijing + Halong Bay cruise — adds nature, reduces city time).

**Book first**: Flights (open-jaw, US↔Beijing and Đà Nẵng↔US), then Đà Nẵng resort (3–4 months out), then Great Wall tour.

## 6. Shopping Strategy

→ See full detail: [[06-shopping]]

Shopping is the #1 profile interest. Budget: ~$4,000–6,800 across the trip.

**Priority #1 — Hội An tailoring (Days 10–12)**: Best value custom tailoring in the world. Recommended shops: Yaly Couture (+84 90 580 4286 — WhatsApp ahead), A Dong Silk, Bebe Tailor. Order linen shirts ($40–80), silk dresses ($80–150), lightweight suit ($200–350), ao dai ($80–200). Insist on 2 fittings minimum. Day 10 consult → Day 11 fitting → Day 12 pickup.

**Priority #2 — Silk & crafts**: Beijing Day 4 (Wangfujing + Sanlitun); Hội An Days 10–13 (lanterns, lacquerware, pottery, handmade leather sandals).

**Priority #3 — Beijing sneakers**: Sanlitun flagships for Li-Ning (China-exclusive), Nike China, Adidas. Buy exclusives, not savings — prices are 5–15% higher than US.

**Skip**: Luxury watches, designer handbags — no price advantage in China or Vietnam vs US. Counterfeit risk is very high at Silk Street and markets; shop only at brand flagships and vetted tailor shops.

**US Customs**: $1,600 duty-free per couple. Estimated duty on excess purchases: $72–260. Keep all receipts. File CBP Form 4457 for high-value items taken FROM the US before departure.

## 7. Transport & Money

→ See full detail: [[07-transport-money]]

**Within cities**: Beijing metro + DiDi app; Hanoi walk + Grab; Đà Nẵng Grab + bicycle (Hội An).

**Never**: Street taxis (consistent with profile — past bad experiences). Always DiDi (China) or Grab (Vietnam).

**Money strategy**:

- Charles Schwab debit card (zero ATM fees worldwide)
- WeChat Pay linked to US Visa/Mastercard for China
- Carry ¥1,000–1,500 CNY cash (China) and ₫3–5M VND cash (Vietnam)
- Decline dynamic currency conversion; always pay in local currency

## 8. Customs & Borders

→ See full detail: [[08-customs-borders]]

**US → China**: Customs declaration at PEK; personal goods under ¥5,000 duty-free; no fresh food from US.

**China → Vietnam**: Declare cash >$5,000; no food products cross; VAT refund at PEK for eligible China purchases (11–13% at Tax Free stores; minimum ¥500/receipt).

**Vietnam → US**: $800/person duty-free ($1,600/couple); register high-value items leaving US on CBP Form 4457 before departure; keep all receipts; Vietnam VAT refund available at DAD airport (10%; get VAT invoice at purchase).

## 14. Health & Safety

→ See full detail: [[14-health-safety]]

**Vaccinations** (visit travel clinic 6–8 weeks before): Hepatitis A, Typhoid, Japanese Encephalitis (Vietnam), Influenza.

**Travel insurance**: World Nomads Explorer or Allianz OneTrip Prime. Cover: medical evacuation + typhoon cancellation + adventure activities.

**China emergency hospitals**: Beijing United Family Hospital (+86-10-5927-7000); English-speaking.
**Vietnam emergency**: Vinmec International Hanoi (+84-24-3974-3556); Family Medical Practice Da Nang (+84-236-3582-699).

**Emergency numbers**: China — police 110, ambulance 120. Vietnam — police 113, ambulance 115.

**Tap water**: NEVER drink tap water in either country.
