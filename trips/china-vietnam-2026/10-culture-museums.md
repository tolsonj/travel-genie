### Deep history (3 facts per country)

#### China (Hong Kong SAR)

1. **Peak Tram** (1888) predates most HK skyscrapers — the funicular was built for British governors escaping summer heat, not tourists.
2. **Chi Lin Nunnery** is the world's largest hand-built wooden Buddhist complex using Tang-dynasty joinery — no nails — rebuilt 1990s on a former cinema site.
3. Hong Kong's **Star Ferry** (1888) carried an estimated 70M passengers/year pre-pandemic — the harbor crossing is the same commute locals used for 130+ years.

#### Vietnam

1. **Temple of Literature** (1070) was Vietnam's first national university — stone stelae record names of doctor laureates for 700 years; passing exams here was the pinnacle of Confucian meritocracy.
2. **Hoa Lo Prison** held Vietnamese revolutionaries under French rule, then American POWs (including John McCain) — the name means "fiery furnace" (from street charcoal trades).
3. **Hội An** was a **Cham** port before Vietnamese settlement; Japanese Covered Bridge (1590s) marks when Japanese traders controlled the town before the Tokugawa isolation edict forced their departure.
