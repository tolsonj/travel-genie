# Packing

---

### Core "Do Not Forget" List (15 Items)

1. **Valid passports** — valid until March 2027 minimum; both travelers
2. **China visa** (in passport or printed approval letter)
3. **Vietnam e-visa** printed copies (2 each; one in carry-on, one in checked)
4. **Travel insurance policy number + 24/7 emergency line** — saved in phone AND on paper
5. **VPN activated on both phones** (ExpressVPN/NordVPN — must be installed BEFORE China entry)
6. **All eSIMs activated** (Airalo China + Vietnam eSIMs downloaded and QR codes saved)
7. **Multi-currency debit card** (Charles Schwab) + backup credit card; both banks notified
8. **Prescription medications** (14-day supply + 3 days buffer; original prescriptions in carry-on)
9. **Universal travel adapter** (both China and Vietnam use A and C type plugs; one universal covers both)
20,000 mAh USB-C power bank (checked for airline Wh compliance)
10. **DEET 30% insect repellent** — mosquito risk in Vietnam
11. **SPF 50 sunscreen** (2 bottles — expensive at Vietnam resorts)
12. **Compression packing cubes** — enables expansion for return shopping haul
13. **Lightweight packable daypack** — for Great Wall hike; beach days; daily city use
14. **CBP Form 4457** (US Customs pre-registration of valuable items taken FROM US: watches, jewelry)
15. **Copies of all hotel bookings, flight confirmations** — in both cloud (via VPN) and printed

### Country-Specific Gear

**China (Beijing)**
- Comfortable walking shoes with grip (required for Great Wall uneven steps)
- Light layer / cardigan (Beijing AC is aggressive indoors; temples can be cool even in September)
- Small umbrella (foldable) — afternoon rain showers possible
- Temple-appropriate clothing (shoulders + knees covered): one pair trousers + short-sleeve shirt
- Camera (real camera, not just phone) for Great Wall + Forbidden City + Jingshan sunset

**Vietnam (Hanoi + Đà Nẵng/Hội An)**
- Swimwear (2 sets minimum — daily beach use)
- Light linen/cotton shirts (humidity is intense; synthetic fabrics are miserable)
- Sandals (Birkenstocks or equivalent) + flip-flops (resort pool)
- Rain poncho/jacket (typhoon season; more useful than umbrella in heavy rain)
- Long sleeve light layer for mosquito protection at evening outdoor dining
- Modest temple wear (same as China or buy a ₫100,000 linen shirt in Hội An — perfect solution)
- Underwater camera / GoPro for Cham Islands potential snorkeling and beach

### Duty-Free / Shopping Bag Strategy

- **Outbound**: Pack one lightweight foldable duffel bag (Osprey Ultralight or similar) compressed in main luggage; will become second checked bag on return
- **In Hội An**: Tailor will pack garments in garment bags; ask for compression option for suits
- **Heavy/bulky marble**: Non Nước Stone Village ships internationally; get shipping quote; not worth carrying
- **Valuables in carry-on**: All watches, jewelry, and items on CBP Form 4457 go in carry-on for return
- **One rule**: If it doesn't fit without straining the zipper, ship it (DHL Vietnam: reliable, door-to-door, 7–10 days to US)

### Border-Crossing Outfit

One outfit optimized for airports, security lines, and all-day wear:

| Item | Why |
|------|-----|
| Slip-on shoes (no laces) | Fast security clearance |
| No belt | No metal = faster screening |
| Light linen trousers or tailored joggers | Comfortable + presentable for lounge access |
| Polo or button-down (not T-shirt) | Presentable for business class check-in |
| Cardigan or light jacket | AC on planes; temperature fluctuations |
| Compression socks | 4+ hour flights → circulation |
