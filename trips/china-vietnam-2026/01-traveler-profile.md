# Traveler Profile

## Reasoning

**Country count vs timeframe:** Two countries over 14 calendar days with **11 hub nights** is realistic for a relaxed couple's trip. Three hubs — **Hong Kong** (shopping, history, harbor nature), **Hanoi** (food, history, Old Quarter), **Đà Nẵng / Hội An** (beach resort splurge, tailoring, nature) — match all four interests without excessive border friction.

**Entry/exit fit:** Profile specifies **fly into Hong Kong**. **Open-jaw DAD out** (Đà Nẵng → ATL) avoids backtracking through Hanoi or Hong Kong and puts the resort finale where the "perfect day" (late brunch, beach walk, dramatic views) lands naturally in central Vietnam.

**Early September window:** Hong Kong still warm-humid (~30°C) but outside peak Golden Week. Northern Vietnam (Hanoi) transitions to pleasant shoulder season. Central Vietnam (Đà Nẵng) may see occasional showers — acceptable with indoor/resort backups. Heat managed via dawn starts, AC hotels, pool midday breaks — profile deal-breakers addressed.

**Travel party:** Couple (wife in great shape), **1 king or two queens**, economy from **ATL**, **2 passengers**. Mass transit + ride-hail preferred; avoid unregulated taxis (profile: taxis were ripoffs).

**Decision rule:** Vote when traveling with others — both travelers decide jointly; planner flags forks for discussion.

**Budget sanity:** **$30,000** all-in ≈ $2,143/day. Unverified long-haul flight estimate ~$3,500–5,000 (~12–17%) leaves ample room for 4–5★ lodging, Vietnam splurge resort, tailoring, and shopping. Vietnam gets elevated hotel and experience spend.

**Interest weighting:** Shopping 25% · Nature 25% · Food 25% · History 25% — Hong Kong (shopping/history/food), Hanoi (history/food), Đà Nẵng/Hội An (nature/beach/shopping tailoring).

**Shopping specifics:** China leg — realistic imitation watches, bags, electronics (headphones, cameras, phones, tablets) plus US customs guidance. Vietnam leg — bespoke suits in Hội An, authentic athletic sneakers available in US/EU.
