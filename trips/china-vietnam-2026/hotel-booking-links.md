## Booking checklist

1. Open each **Hotels.com** (or Booking/Expedia) link with the trip dates already filled.
2. Filter **free cancellation** where possible (especially Đà Nẵng).
3. Compare **official site** vs OTA for Accor / IHG / Peninsula — loyalty + packages sometimes win.
4. Confirm **1 room · 2 adults · king or 2 queens** before pay.
5. After booking, store confirmation #s next to the hub dates above.

*Prices move; re-check before locking non-refundable rates.*
