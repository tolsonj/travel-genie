# Adventure

---

### CHINA — BEIJING

#### Land Adventures

**Great Wall Hike (Mutianyu) — Day 3**
- **Activity**: 4–6 km ridge walk between restored towers; elevation change ~200 m over walk
- **Difficulty**: Moderate — steps are uneven; some steep sections between towers 14–20
- **Duration**: 2–3 hours of active walking
- **Highlights**: Dramatic ridge views; option to continue to unrestored "wild wall" section beyond Tower 20 (bring good shoes for this)
- **Toboggan descent**: Metal luge track from Tower 6 to base — 580 m descent, ~3 min; exhilarating and crowd-appropriate (it's fast but not extreme)
- **Fitness required**: Moderate; their fitness level is more than sufficient
- **Gear**: Comfortable walking shoes (NOT sandals); sunscreen; water; the wall vendors at the top charge ¥25 for water
- **Border crossing rule**: Day 3 is not a border day — CLEAR ✓

**Jingshan Hill Climb — Day 2**
- **Activity**: 88-meter artificial hill; 300 steps to the Wanchun Pavilion at summit
- **Duration**: 30 minutes up and down
- **Reward**: Best panoramic view of Forbidden City's full symmetry — worth every step

**Summer Palace Rowboat — Day 5**
- **Activity**: Rowboat rental on Kunming Lake (⅔ the size of New York's Central Park)
- **Duration**: 1–2 hours; ~¥100/hour for rental
- **Character**: Leisurely — paddles among lotus flowers; pagodas visible from water; peaceful rather than strenuous

#### Water Sports — China
None viable in Beijing (landlocked; no beach); skip.

### VIETNAM — ĐÀ NẴNG / HỘI AN

#### Water Sports

**Swimming / Beach — Mỹ Khê Beach (Days 9, 13)**
- **September water**: ~28°C; generally calm on the Đà Nẵng side (protected by the peninsula)
- **Waves**: Mild surf; safe swimming year-round on this stretch
- **Equipment**: Resort provides loungers and umbrellas; no equipment needed

**Stand-Up Paddleboarding (SUP)**
- **Availability**: Most 4–5 star beach resorts in Đà Nẵng offer SUP rental (Hyatt Regency, Fusion Maia)
- **September viability**: Good — morning conditions are typically flat; avoid afternoons if wind picks up
- **Duration**: 30 min–1 hr; no instruction needed for beginners
- **Cost**: Often included at Fusion Maia (all-inclusive) or ~$15–20/hr elsewhere

**Cham Islands Snorkeling**
- **Location**: 18 km offshore from Hội An; accessible by speedboat (~45 min)
- **September viability**: Marginal — visibility varies (50–60% chance of clear conditions); water clarity peaks March–August; September is end of good season
- **Typhoon risk**: Check weather 48 hrs before booking; operators cancel if sea conditions deteriorate
- **Operator**: Cham Island Diving Center (reputable; PADI certified); book 1 day ahead, not in advance
- **Duration**: Full-day trip including boat + snorkeling + lunch on island
- **Cost**: ~$35–50/person
- **Recommendation**: Plan for Day 11 afternoon (after fitting); treat as weather-dependent bonus, not scheduled anchor

#### Land Adventures — Vietnam

**Son Trà Peninsula Nature Walk (Day 11)**
- **Activity**: Scenic drive/walk through protected rainforest; Lady Buddha lookout; douc langur spotting
- **Difficulty**: Easy to moderate depending on path chosen; road access by car/motorbike taxi
- **Wildlife**: Red-shanked douc langurs — one of the most colorful primates in the world; visible most mornings in the trees near the road
- **Duration**: 2–3 hours including Lady Buddha visit
- **Best time**: Early morning (7:30–10 AM) for langur sighting and pre-heat

**Marble Mountains (Day 9)**
- **Activity**: Stairs and cavern exploration inside marble hills
- **Difficulty**: Easy — stairs are well-maintained; cable car available for ascent
- **Caves**: Huyen Khong Cave (natural light shafts illuminate Buddhist shrines) and Van Thong Cave (natural chimney; views from top)
- **Duration**: 1.5–2 hours
- **Best time**: 9–11 AM before midday heat peaks inside the stone

**My Son Sanctuary Walk (Day 12)**
- **Activity**: Archaeological site walking tour; uneven jungle terrain between temple groups
- **Difficulty**: Easy — flat mostly; muddy after rain
- **Duration**: 1.5–2 hours
- **Best time**: 7:30 AM opening

- No adventure gear restrictions for this route (no knives, no dive equipment that would flag)
- GoPro and cameras are fine
- Selfie sticks are fine

### Operator Vetting Guide

| Activity | Questions to Ask |
|----------|-----------------|
| Cham Islands snorkeling | "Are your guides PADI certified?" "What is your boat capacity?" "Do you have life jackets?" |
| Any water activity | "Is the equipment inspected daily?" "What is the cancellation/refund policy if weather deteriorates?" |
| Son Trà guide | "Does your vehicle have insurance?" "How many in the group?" (Private guide preferred) |

**Safety standards**: Vietnam has improved significantly; Đà Nẵng resort-area operators catering to foreign tourists maintain reasonable safety standards. Avoid very cheap operators recommended by street touts; book through resort concierge or TripAdvisor-rated operators.
