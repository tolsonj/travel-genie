# Food & Dining

### Signature dishes per country

| Country | Must-try (5) | Where to find |
|---------|--------------|---------------|
| **China (Hong Kong)** | Har gow (shrimp dumpling), siu mai, char siu bao, roast goose, egg tart | Dim sum houses (TST, Mong Kok); roast-meat shops; cha chaan teng |
| **Vietnam** | Phở bò, bún chả, bánh mì, gỏi cuốn (fresh spring rolls), cao lầu (Hội An) | Hanoi Old Quarter; Hội An Ancient Town; Đà Nẵng seafood strip |

### Food safety

| Topic | Hong Kong | Vietnam |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Tap water | **No** — bottled only | **No** — bottled only |
| Street food | High hygiene standards; choose busy stalls | Busy stalls; cooked-to-order; peel fruit |
| Ice | OK at established venues | OK at reputable cafés/restaurants |
| Shellfish | Harbor seafood — reputable venues only | Đà Nẵng seafood — same-day catch at rated spots |
| Hand sanitizer | Carry — market browsing | Carry — Old Quarter walks |

### Dining customs

| Topic | Hong Kong | Vietnam |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Tea / drinks | Tap table to thank for tea refill; don't stick chopsticks upright | Plastic-stool culture; iced coffee is dessert + caffeine |
| Sharing | Dim sum shared family-style; request split bill ("go Dutch") common | Communal plates; pass with serving spoon |
| Tipping | 10% optional at upscale; not expected at cha chaan teng | Not expected; round up or 5–10% at fine dining |
| Reservations | Splurge (Peninsula, Zuma): book T-7d | Essence, Duong's, Hong Hoai's: book T-14d |
| Pace | Fast turnover at dim sum — flag staff when done | Relaxed; second round of beer/snacks normal |
| Dietary notes | English menus common in TST/Central | Point-and-smile OK; "không cay" = not spicy |
