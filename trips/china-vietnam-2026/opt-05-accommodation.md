# Accommodation

### Hong Kong — 3 Nights (Sept 1–3)

**Neighborhood**: Tsim Sha Tsui or Central  
Rationale: Victoria Peak, Star Ferry, IFC/Landmark shopping, Airport Express access.

| Hotel | Stars | Rate Est. | Why |
|-------|-------|-----------|-----|
| **The Peninsula Hong Kong** | 5★ | $500–750/night | Iconic harbour; Star Ferry steps away |
| **Mandarin Oriental Hong Kong** | 5★ | $450–650/night | Central; Peak Tram nearby |
| **Hyatt Centric Victoria Harbour** | 4★ | $250–380/night | TST; Marriott/Hyatt bookable on US OTAs |

**Recommended**: **Peninsula HK** or **Mandarin Oriental** — matches 4–5★ profile; harbour walks on itinerary.

**Budget (3 nights)**: ~$1,200–1,800 total.

### Hanoi — 2 Nights (Sept 6–8)

**Recommended neighborhood**: Old Quarter / Hoan Kiem Lake district  
Rationale: All Hanoi activities (lake, temple, hutong-equivalent streets, night market) are walking distance; immersive cultural location; safe, well-lit area at night.

#### Top Hotel Options (US Bookable)

| Hotel | Stars | Rate Est. | Why |
|-------|-------|-----------|-----|
| **Sofitel Legend Metropole Hanoi** | 5★ | $280–400/night | The iconic Hanoi hotel (1901); French colonial grandeur; within 5 min walk of Hoan Kiem; bunker tour on-site (Cold War history); book on Accor or Booking.com |
| **La Siesta Classic Hang Be** | 4★ boutique | $90–130/night | Best value luxury in Old Quarter; rooftop bar; all activities walking distance |
| **JW Marriott Hanoi** | 5★ | $200–300/night | Business district (Ba Dinh); 20 min from Old Quarter; larger rooms, better pool; Marriott Bonvoy bookable |

**Recommended**: **Sofitel Legend Metropole** — historic, central, unforgettable; worth the premium for 2 nights given it's an iconic property.

**Budget for Hanoi (2 nights)**: ~$600–800 (splurge-appropriate for a legendary hotel).

### Đà Nẵng / Hội An Base — 5 Nights (Sept 8–13)

**Recommended neighborhood**: Mỹ Khê Beach / Non Nước Beach, Đà Nẵng (NOT Hội An town)  
Rationale: Beachfront resort base perfectly positioned — 20 min to Hội An by Grab, 15 min to Marble Mountains, 5 min to Son Trà Peninsula trailhead; beach and nature access from the property.

#### Top Resort Options (US Bookable)

| Hotel | Stars | Rate Est. | Why |
|-------|-------|-----------|-----|
| **InterContinental Đà Nẵng Sun Peninsula** | 5★ luxury resort | $450–700/night | Cliff-top resort on Son Trà Peninsula; one of Asia's most spectacular resort settings; private beach; IHG bookable |
| **Fusion Maia Đà Nẵng** | 5★ | $320–500/night | All-spa-included resort (unlimited spa treatments included in rate); beachfront; on Booking.com/Expedia |
| **Pullman Đà Nẵng Beach Resort** | 5★ | $250–380/night | Mỹ Khê Beach; strong US platform presence; Accor bookable; reliable quality |
| **Vinpearl Luxury Đà Nẵng** | 5★ | $200–350/night | Good value for the star rating; beachfront; on Hotels.com |
| **Hyatt Regency Đà Nẵng** | 5★ | $280–400/night | World of Hyatt bookable; multiple pools; excellent food; non-motorized water sports included |

**Recommended (Vietnam splurge)**: **InterContinental Đà Nẵng Sun Peninsula** — this is the splurge property; the design (by Bill Bensley), setting on a forested peninsula cliff above a private beach, and service level are extraordinary. Book direct or through IHG for best rate + benefits.

**Recommended (value splurge)**: **Fusion Maia** — all-spa-included means daily massages/treatments are built in; exceptional value for couples; the "perfect day" template is built for this resort.

**Budget for Đà Nẵng (5 nights)**: ~$2,000–3,500 depending on resort choice (InterCon at top end). This is the designated splurge segment.

### Booking Strategy

| Segment | Book how far in advance | Platform |
|---------|------------------------|----------|
| Đà Nẵng resort (Sept is shoulder but resorts fill) | **NOW** — 3–4 months out | IHG.com, Fusion direct, or Hyatt.com |
| Beijing (Sept is not peak season) | 6–8 weeks out | peninsula.com direct or Hotels.com |
| Hanoi (Metropole fills fast year-round) | 6–8 weeks out | Accor.com or Booking.com |

**Cancellation policy**: Book with free cancellation where possible (most 5-star hotels offer it up to 72 hrs before). Typhoon risk in Đà Nẵng in September justifies flexible booking.

### Cross-Border Logistics

| Scenario | Action |
|----------|--------|
| Beijing early arrival (Day 1) | Request early check-in when booking; Peninsula is good at accommodating; arrive mid-morning and bags can be held if room not ready |
| Hanoi late arrival (Day 6 evening) | Room pre-blocked; Metropole concierge meets at airport if arranged |
| Đà Nẵng arrival midday (Day 8) | Request early check-in; resort can arrange lunch before room ready |
| Đà Nẵng late checkout (Day 14) | Request late checkout (2 PM typical for 5-star); luggage storage available if earlier |
