### 2. Tailored & Bespoke Clothing — Hội An Deep Dive

Hội An is one of the top three cities in the world for custom tailoring (alongside Hong Kong and Ho Chi Minh City). Skilled tailors, fast turnaround (24–72 hrs), fabric quality improving dramatically at mid-price tiers, and prices that are 60–80% below equivalent Western custom tailoring.

#### Recommended Tailors

| Shop | Specialty | Price Range | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------------|-------|
| **Yaly Couture** | Full wardrobe: suits, dresses, shirts | $80–400/garment | Longest-established premium shop; English-fluent staff; US clients regularly; WhatsApp: +84 90 580 4286 |
| **A Dong Silk** | Silk dresses, ao dai, shirts | $60–250/garment | Excellent women's wear; quality silk sourced; central Ancient Town location |
| **Bebe Tailor** | Casual and formal; fast turnaround | $50–200/garment | Good for shirts and trousers; 24–48 hr on simple items |
| **Mango Mango** | Dresses, women's resort wear | $40–150/garment | Boutique feel; high-quality linen and cotton |
| **Lantern Town Tailor** | Suits and formal wear | $150–500/garment | Best for men's suits; wool and linen options |

#### Turnaround Times

| Garment | Minimum Time | Recommended Lead |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Simple shirt / blouse | 24 hours | 48 hours |
| Dress (mid-complexity) | 36–48 hours | 3 days |
| Suit (jacket + trousers) | 48–72 hours | 3–4 days |
| Ao dai (Vietnamese formal dress) | 24–48 hours | 2 days |
| Full wardrobe (5+ pieces) | 3–5 days | 5 days |

**With 3 days in Hội An (Days 10–12)**: Day 10 consultation and measurements → Day 11 first fitting → Day 12 pickup and final alterations. This works perfectly for garments of moderate complexity. Do NOT order suits on Day 12 expecting same-day pickup.

#### Fabric Quality Tiers

| Tier | Price signal | Best choice |
|------|-------------|------------|
| **Economy** | <$30 fabric cost | Polyester blends; avoid — will not last or drape well |
| **Mid** | $30–80 fabric cost | Good cotton, linen, basic silk; excellent for shirts and casual dresses |
| **Premium** | $80–200 fabric cost | Pure silk, wool blends, Italian-origin fabric; suits and formal dresses |
| **Ultra** | $200+ fabric cost | Imported wool or Japanese cotton; worth it for a full suit |

**What to order in Hội An**:
- ✅ Linen shirts (2–3 each; excellent fabric, great value ~$40–80)
- ✅ Silk blouse or dress (Vietnamese silk; ~$80–150; stunning quality)
- ✅ Lightweight suit (jacket + trousers; wool-linen blend; ~$200–350; best value in the world)
- ✅ Ao dai (Vietnamese traditional dress; silk; ~$80–200; one-of-a-kind souvenir)
- ❌ Avoid: Heavy wool suits (Vietnam can't source premium wool reliably; winter suits are a risk)
- ❌ Avoid: Replicas of designer brands (quality is inconsistent; legal risk at US customs)

#### Red Flags — Tailor Scams to Avoid

| Red flag | What it means |
|---------|--------------|
| Pushes you to decide same day without measuring | Not a real tailor — reseller of off-the-rack |
| No fitting offered (just take measurements and deliver) | No quality control; cannot fix issues |
| Price drops dramatically when you push back | First price was inflated by 300%+; inconsistent quality signal |
| "My cousin owns the fabric store — let's go now" | Commission scam — you pay inflated fabric prices |
| Shop is 100m from the tourist bus drop-off | High-turnover tourist trap; prioritizes volume over quality |
| No photos of past work or portfolio | Unverifiable quality |

**Fitting protocol**: For any garment over $100, insist on 2 fittings minimum. The first fitting reveals fit issues (shoulder width, trouser break, sleeve length). The second fitting confirms corrections. Skipping fittings is how you end up with an unwearable garment. Reputable shops expect this — it's how they maintain reviews.

#### Beijing Tailoring

Available but not the priority:
- **Silk Street (Xiushui Market)** upper floors have tailors — tourist-facing, inconsistent quality, aggressive sales
- **Wangfujing area tailors** — better but still require multi-day lead; not worth it when Hội An follows
- **Recommendation**: Skip Beijing tailoring entirely; save all custom clothing budget for Hội An

### 3. Designer Watches

**China and Vietnam are NOT recommended for authorized luxury watch purchases.** Prices at authorized dealers (present in Beijing luxury malls) match or exceed US retail, with no meaningful VAT refund benefit.

| Scenario | Recommendation |
|----------|---------------|
| Authorized dealer watch (Rolex, Patek, AP) | Buy at home or in Hong Kong (10–15% cheaper at gray market authorized) |
| Gray market watches (Beijing) | Moderate counterfeit risk; not recommended without expert guidance |
| Pre-owned watches | Avoid in both countries unless you bring your own authentication expert |
| Watch souvenirs (<$200) | Chinese brands (Seagull, Fiyta) are legitimate, interesting, and affordable — genuine Chinese watchmaking |

**If buying a watch in Beijing**: Only visit authorized boutiques in malls (Shin Kong Place, SKP Mall). Do NOT engage with any street-level or market watch sellers — counterfeits are pervasive and sophisticated. Keep original receipt + box for US customs.

**Import duty on returning to US**: Watches are dutiable above the $800/person exemption. A $5,000 watch incurs ~$150–250 duty (3–5%). Register any watch you're taking FROM the US (even your current watch) on CBP Form 4457 before departure to avoid being taxed on re-entry.

### 4. Designer Handbags

**China and Vietnam offer no price advantage** for authorized luxury handbags (LV, Chanel, Hermès, Gucci) vs US retail. Prices are set globally by luxury houses and often slightly higher in Asia due to import duties.

| Scenario | Recommendation |
|----------|---------------|
| Authorized luxury boutiques (Beijing) | Present in SKP Mall; no savings vs US; skip unless seeking specific item |
| Waitlist strategy | No advantage in Beijing for Hermès/Chanel waitlists vs US |
| Counterfeit handbags | **Illegal to import to US** — US Customs confiscates and can fine up to $10,000; DO NOT BUY |
| Vietnamese silk clutches / handmade bags | ✅ Authentic local craft; ~$20–80 in Hội An market; beautiful and unique |
| Lacquerware clutch bags | ✅ Traditional Vietnamese craft; excellent quality; ~$30–100 |

**Best handbag purchase on this trip**: A hand-embroidered silk clutch or lacquered wood purse from Hội An — genuinely made locally, beautiful quality, unique to Vietnam, and under $100.

### 6. Athletic & Sneaker Shopping

**Beijing** is the only city on this itinerary with meaningful sneaker shopping:

#### Nike
- **Nike Beijing Flagship** — Sanlitun Village (Taikoo Li North); largest Nike store in North Asia; China-exclusive colorways of Air Max, Dunk, and Jordan lines; Asia-only release dates often differ from US
- **Air Force 1 China Pack** and regional colorways typically available in Beijing that are not sold in US
- Price: Generally 5–15% more expensive than US retail (China import duties on footwear); shop for exclusives, not savings

#### Adidas
- **Adidas Flagship** — Sanlitun area; China-exclusive Ultraboost and NMD colorways
- **Adidas Originals** (separate store in Sanlitun) — better selection for lifestyle/retro styles

#### New Balance
- **NB China exclusives**: New Balance China has collabs with Chinese designers/brands not available in US; Sanlitun area has multi-brand stores carrying these

#### Other brands
- **ASICS**: Strong in Chinese market; Tiger series and Gel-Kayano colorways sometimes China-exclusive
- **Li-Ning**: Chinese domestic brand with extraordinary cultural cachet in China; NBA collabs, "China Li-Ning" fashion line; Sanlitun has flagship; prices ¥300–1,500 ($42–210); unique item to bring home
- **ANTA**: Another quality Chinese brand; functional athletic wear at excellent prices

**Best sneaker district**: Sanlitun Taikoo Li (North and South buildings) — all major flagships concentrated in 2 blocks; walkable

**Sneaker market/resale**:
- **Dewu app** (得物): China's StockX equivalent; verified resale; can browse but buying requires Chinese payment methods; useful for price reference
- Physical resale: Bailian Outlets in outer Beijing has clearance footwear; 45 min from city — not worth the trip for this itinerary

**Vietnam / Hanoi**:
- Standard athletic stock; no exclusives; prices similar to US
- Skip sneaker shopping in Vietnam

### 7. Logistics & Customs Strategy

#### Country Shopping Order (Luggage Logic)

| Day | Location | Buy | Rationale |
|-----|----------|-----|-----------|
| Days 2–5 | Beijing | Crafts, silk scarves, tea, sneakers | Buy early; travel to Hanoi with this packed |
| Day 7 | Hanoi | Coffee beans, lacquerware, small items | Buy light; 2 more countries of carrying ahead |
| Days 10–13 | Hội An / Đà Nẵng | **Tailored clothing, silk, lanterns, pottery, final haul** | Last stop before home — buy heavy here |

**Rule**: Buy bulky/fragile items in Vietnam (last stop). Tailored clothing, marble pieces, and large lacquerware should not be transported through multiple countries.

#### Shipping From Vietnam

| Service | Transit | Cost | Notes |
|---------|---------|------|-------|
| **DHL** | 5–7 business days to US | ~$40–80 for 5kg box | Most reliable; DHL Vietnam locations in Đà Nẵng and Hội An; track online |
| **Vietnam Post EMS** | 7–14 business days | ~$20–40 | Cheaper; reasonable reliability for non-fragile items |
| **Store shipping** (tailors) | Varies | $20–60 | Many Hội An tailors ship internationally; confirm insurance coverage |
| **Checked baggage overage** | Immediate | ~$100–200/extra bag | Foldable duffel bag + overage fee vs. shipping cost; often cheaper to just check |

**Recommendation for tailored clothing**: Carry on the plane if volume permits; hard luggage protects garments; ask tailor to vacuum-pack. Only ship if volume is unmanageable.

**Marble and stone**: Non Nước Stone Village (Đà Nẵng) can ship large pieces internationally via freight; small pieces (<3 kg) carry in checked luggage.

#### VAT Refund Summary

| Country | Rate | Minimum Purchase | Claim Location | Process |
|---------|------|-----------------|---------------|---------|
| **China** | 11–13% | ¥500 (~$70) per receipt, same store | PEK Airport departure terminal | Get Tax Free form at purchase; Customs stamp at PEK; refund counter before security |
| **Vietnam** | 10% | ₫2,000,000 (~$80) per item | DAD Airport (Đà Nẵng) | Get VAT invoice (hóa đơn VAT) at purchase; refund counter at airport |
| **Global Blue / Planet** | Varies | — | PEK and major Vietnam airports | Available at participating retailers; pre-register at globalblue.com |

**Practical tip**: Chinese VAT refunds at PEK require a Customs stamp on departure — allow 30 extra minutes at the airport. Many small shops don't participate; luxury malls (SKP, Shin Kong) are the most reliable.

#### US Customs Duty Cheat Sheet (On Return)

| Category | Duty-Free Per Person | Above Exemption |
|----------|---------------------|----------------|
| Total goods | $800 | 3–5% duty on next $1,000 |
| Alcohol | 1 liter | Duty applies |
| Tobacco | 200 cigarettes | Duty applies |
| **Per couple total** | **$1,600** | — |

**High-value item strategy**:
- Register any valuables leaving the US (watches, jewelry) on **CBP Form 4457** at your departure airport — prevents being taxed on re-entry
- Declare honestly on CBP Form 6059B; duty on $3,000 of goods above exemption is ~$90–150 — not worth the legal risk of undeclaring
- Keep ALL receipts separated by country for Customs inspection

### 8. Authenticity & Scam Protection

#### Counterfeit Risk by Country & Category

| Category | Beijing Risk | Vietnam Risk | Action |
|----------|-------------|-------------|--------|
| Luxury watches | **VERY HIGH** (markets) | High | Never buy from markets or street vendors |
| Luxury handbags | **VERY HIGH** (Silk Street market) | High | Never buy "designer" bags in Asia markets |
| Athletic shoes | Moderate (fakes are common at non-flagship stores) | Moderate | Buy ONLY at brand flagships or Nike.cn |
| Silk | Moderate (synthetic silk sold as real) | Moderate | Test: burn a thread — real silk smells like hair; synthetic smells like plastic |
| Tailored clothing | Low (quality risk, not counterfeit risk) | Low | Use vetted tailors only; see Section 2 |
| Artisan crafts | Low | Low | Machine-made vs handmade is the main distinction |

#### How to Identify Fake Silk

1. **Burn test**: Pull a thread; hold flame to it — real silk burns slowly like hair and leaves a crushable ash; synthetic melts, smells like plastic, and beads
2. **Ring test**: Real silk pulls smoothly through a finger ring; rough/synthetic catches
3. **Price test**: Real silk at proper thread count cannot be sold for <$5/meter; if it's ¥30/meter (~$4), it's synthetic
4. **Ask**: Reputable shops will show you the silk certification or let you do the burn test

#### Safe Shopping Zones vs Counterfeit Districts

**Beijing**:
- ✅ **Safe**: SKP Mall, Shin Kong Place, Sanlitun Taikoo Li, Parkview Green, brand flagships
- ❌ **Avoid for luxury**: Silk Street (Xiushui) — counterfeit capital of Beijing; fine for novelty items but NEVER for anything claimed to be luxury
- ❌ **Avoid**: Yaxiu Market (similar to Silk Street)

**Hanoi**:
- ✅ **Safe**: Established silk shops on Hang Gai (Silk Street of Hanoi); Dong Xuan Market for local goods
- ❌ **Avoid**: Any vendor offering luxury brand goods in Old Quarter markets — all counterfeit

**Hội An**:
- ✅ **Safe**: Yaly, A Dong, any shop with a street presence and reviews
- ❌ **Avoid**: Tailors in unmarked side streets who approach you unprompted

#### Receipt & Authentication Standards

| Item | Demand at purchase |
|------|--------------------|
| Any purchase >$100 | Dated, itemized receipt with shop name and address |
| Tailored clothing | Receipt showing fabric type + labor cost separately |
| Silk goods | Receipt + certification or burn-test opportunity |
| Watches | Original box + papers + warranty card + authorized dealer receipt |
| Artisan crafts >$50 | Receipt with maker's name if handmade |

### Recommended Shopping Days Per City

**Beijing — Day 4 (primary shopping day)**
- AM: Wangfujing + Qianmen (traditional crafts, tea, silk scarves, Chinese cloth shoes)
- PM: Sanlitun Taikoo Li (Li-Ning flagship, Nike flagship, Adidas flagship, Parkview Green for luxury browsing)
- Budget: ¥2,000–6,000 (~$280–840)

**Beijing — Day 2 (secondary)**
- PM only: Nanluoguxiang Hutong shops (artisan, indie brands, quality crafts)
- Budget: ¥500–1,500 (~$70–210)

**Hanoi — Day 7 (limited window)**
- Morning: Hang Gai (Silk Street) — silk scarves, ao dai fabric, embroidery
- Afternoon: Dong Xuan Market (coffee beans, spices, woven baskets, local products)
- Budget: ₫500,000–2,000,000 (~$20–80)

**Hội An — Day 10 (consultation + shopping)**
- AM: Tailor consultation + measurements (2 hrs)
- AM–PM: Ancient Town browsing: lanterns, silk, lacquerware, handmade bags
- Budget: $500–1,500 (excluding tailoring deposit ~50%)

**Hội An — Day 12 (tailor pickup + final shopping)**
- AM: My Son; PM: Tailor final pickup + final Ancient Town pass
- Budget: Tailor balance payment + $100–300 final purchases

**Đà Nẵng — Day 13 (final shopping)**
- PM: Vincom Plaza or Indochina Riverside Mall (last-minute items; marble carvings at Non Nước)
- Budget: $100–300
