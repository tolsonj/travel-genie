### Style & Pace

- **Style**: Relaxed, culturally immersive — not rushed, not spontaneous
- **Pace**: Moderate-relaxed; late mornings preferred; long transit days should be avoided
- **Ideal day**: Late brunch → beach or waterfront walk → scenic nature views of the city at dusk

### Accommodation

- **Tier**: 4–5 star hotels; resorts acceptable and preferred in Vietnam
- **Booking requirement**: Must be bookable on US platforms (Marriott, Hilton, Expedia, Hotels.com, Booking.com)
- **No**: hostels, guesthouses, Airbnb with uncertain quality

### Budget

| Category | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| **Total** | $20,000 USD for both travelers |
| **Allocation intent** | Splurge in Vietnam; be value-conscious in China |
| **Per-day estimate** | ~$1,428/day total (both) across 14 days |

### Interests (ranked by profile signals)

1. **Shopping** — tailored clothing, luxury/designer goods implied by budget
2. **Food** — local dining a priority
3. **Nature** — scenic views, beach walks, landscape experiences
4. **History** — cultural sites, not museum marathons

### Physical Profile

- Both travelers in excellent physical condition
- No mobility or accessibility limitations
- Can handle moderate walking, stairs, uneven terrain

### Transit Experience & Preferences

- Local mass transit (metro/subway): positive past experience — **recommended**
- Taxis: past negative experience (rip-offs) — **avoid unmetered taxis**; use apps (DiDi in China, Grab in Vietnam)
- Preference: efficient, app-based, predictable transport

### Deal-Breakers

| Deal-breaker | Planning implication |
|---|---|
| **Large crowds** | Avoid peak tourist hours; book early-entry tickets; skip hyper-popular queues |
| **Heat** | September is shoulder/late-monsoon season — heat is a real concern in both countries. Schedule outdoor activities for early morning or late afternoon. Midday rests essential. |

### Key Planning Constraints

1. September in both China and Vietnam means heat + humidity + possible typhoon/rain — itinerary must have strong rainy-day fallbacks
2. Vietnam resort stay should be clustered for relaxation (not fragmented across cities)
3. China portion should prioritize efficiency: maximize cultural/shopping value without over-scheduling
4. **HKG entry** → high-speed rail to Guangzhou for mainland China segment; then flight to Vietnam (no overland China–Vietnam border)
5. Hong Kong and mainland China are **separate immigration regimes** — plan visas and apps for both
