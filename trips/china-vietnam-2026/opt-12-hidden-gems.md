### Only-here experiences

1. **Chi Lin Nunnery** — largest nail-free Tang-style wooden Buddhist complex in the world (HK only).
2. **Cao lầu** — Hội An noodle dish using well water and ash-kneaded dough; unavailable outside town.
3. **Egg coffee (cà phê trứng)** — Hanoi invention (1920s Giang Café); order at origin not airport copy.
