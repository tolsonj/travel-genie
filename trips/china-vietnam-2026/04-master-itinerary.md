# Master Itinerary

## Reasoning

14 days, couple, relaxed-cultural. **HKG entry** per profile: Hong Kong 3 nights → Guangzhou 2 nights → Hanoi 2 → Đà Nẵng resort 5. September heat/crowd deal-breakers: morning anchors, midday AC, Grab not street taxis. Vietnam splurge on 5★ beach resort. Tailor arc in Hội An Days 10–12. Transit days Sept 6 and 8 are soft.

## Output

---

### HONG KONG — Sept 1–3 (3 nights)

#### Day 1 — Tuesday, Sept 1: ARRIVAL (Soft)
- **Arrive HKG** — afternoon/evening US departure
- **Transfer**: Airport Express to Central/TST (~24 min)
- **Evening**: Victoria Harbour promenade (TST) — Symphony of Lights 8 PM (optional)
- **Dinner**: Tim Ho Wan or Lin Heung Tea House (dim sum)
- **Wow**: First harbour skyline at dusk
- **Low energy**: Hotel lounge only
- **Rainy**: Harbour City mall + indoor tea
- **Book NOW**: HK hotel (Peninsula, Mandarin Oriental, Hyatt Centric TST)

#### Day 2 — Wednesday, Sept 2: Peak + Central
- **Morning (8 AM)**: **Victoria Peak** via Peak Tram — before tour buses; Sky Terrace views
- **Midday**: Lunch Central (Yung Kee roast goose or Capo Italian if Western break)
- **Afternoon**: Central shopping — IFC Mall, Landmark, PMQ design shops
- **Evening**: Star Ferry Central ↔ TST at sunset; rooftop drink (Aqua or Sevva)
- **Wow**: Peak panorama over harbour + islands
- **Low energy**: Skip Peak Tram hike; bus 15 to Peak
- **Rainy**: Hong Kong Museum of History or M+ (West Kowloon)

#### Day 3 — Thursday, Sept 3: Stanley + Food
- **Morning**: Bus/taxi to **Stanley Market** — waterfront, relaxed shopping
- **Midday**: Late brunch Stanley — beach walk (Stanley Main Beach)
- **Afternoon**: Causeway Bay (Times Square, local boutiques) OR Sheung Wan antiques
- **Evening**: Temple Street Night Market (after 7 PM) — food stalls, atmosphere
- **Wow**: Stanley coastal walk — “perfect day” template preview
- **Low energy**: Causeway Bay only; skip Temple Street
- **Rainy**: Pacific Place + hotel spa

---

### GUANGZHOU — Sept 4–5 (2 nights)

#### Day 4 — Friday, Sept 4: HK → Guangzhou (Transit-light)
- **Morning**: Check out HK; **West Kowloon → Guangzhou South** high-speed rail (~47 min)
- **Afternoon**: Shamian Island — colonial architecture, quiet tree-lined streets
- **Evening**: Pearl River night cruise or Riverside dining (Binjiang Road)
- **Wow**: Shamian facades at golden hour
- **Low energy**: Hotel rest; single neighbourhood stroll
- **Rainy**: Guangdong Museum (new campus, Zhujiang New Town)
- **Book NOW**: Rail tickets; Guangzhou hotel (Four Seasons, Ritz-Carlton, or White Swan)

#### Day 5 — Saturday, Sept 5: Culture + Shopping
- **Morning**: **Chen Clan Ancestral Hall** — Lingnan architecture, folk art
- **Midday**: **Taikoo Hui** / Tee Mall — luxury shopping, air-conditioned
- **Afternoon**: Beijing Road pedestrian zone — snacks, local brands
- **Evening**: Cantonese dinner (Bingsheng or Panxi Restaurant — book ahead)
- **Wow**: Chen Clan carved screens and courtyards
- **Low energy**: Mall-only afternoon
- **Rainy**: Full day Taikoo Hui + spa at hotel
- **Pack**: Prepare for CAN → HAN flight Sept 6

---

### TRANSIT — Sept 6: Guangzhou → Hanoi (SOFT)

#### Day 6 — Sunday, Sept 6
- **Flight CAN → HAN** (~2.5 hrs) — morning flight preferred
- **Arrive Hanoi**: e-visa immigration; **Grab** to Old Quarter hotel (no airport taxis)
- **Evening**: Hoan Kiem Lake loop; pho at Pho Gia Truyen (Bat Dan)
- **Wow**: Lake lit at night
- **Low energy**: Hotel dinner only
- **Book NOW**: Sofitel Metropole or La Siesta Classic; Grab SIM/eSIM active

---

### HANOI — Sept 6–7 (2 nights)

#### Day 7 — Monday, Sept 7: Old Quarter + Temple of Literature
- **Morning (8:30)**: Hoan Kiem Lake + Ngoc Son Temple
- **Mid-morning**: Old Quarter guild streets; Dong Xuan Market (brief)
- **Afternoon**: **Temple of Literature** — gardens, quiet
- **Evening**: Ta Hien beer street OR weekend night market
- **Wow**: Temple courtyard at golden hour
- **Low energy**: Lake + café afternoon
- **Rainy**: Vietnam Museum of Ethnology

#### Day 8 — Tuesday, Sept 8: Hanoi → Đà Nẵng (SOFT)
- **Morning**: Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum complex (early; closed Mon/Fri)
- **Flight HAN → DAD** (~1.25 hrs)
- **Afternoon**: Check-in **Mỹ Khê beach resort** — beach sunset walk
- **Wow**: First Vietnam beach at dusk
- **Low energy**: Resort only
- **Book NOW**: Domestic flight with international batch

---

### ĐÀ NẴNG / HỘI AN — Sept 8–13 (5 nights)

#### Day 9 — Wednesday, Sept 9: Marble Mountains + Beach
- **Morning**: Marble Mountains (caves, cool interiors)
- **Midday**: Resort late brunch + pool
- **Afternoon**: Mỹ Khê Beach walk
- **Evening**: Seafood at Bê Mặn (My An)
- **Wow**: Buddhist cave shrines in limestone
- **Low energy**: Full resort day
- **Rainy**: Cham Sculpture Museum (indoor)

#### Day 10 — Thursday, Sept 10: Hội An Ancient Town
- **Morning (8 AM)**: UNESCO Ancient Town before crowds
- **Mid-morning**: **Tailor consultation** — Yaly / Bebe / A Dong (2 fittings min)
- **Lunch**: Cao Lầu (Hội An only)
- **Evening**: Lantern-lit old town + river candle boats
- **Wow**: Lanterns at dusk
- **Rainy**: Tailors + covered Assembly Halls

#### Day 11 — Friday, Sept 11: Fitting + Sơn Trà
- **Morning**: Tailor fitting
- **Late morning**: Sơn Trà Peninsula — Lady Buddha, langurs
- **Afternoon**: Resort spa/pool
- **Evening**: Sky 36 rooftop (Novotel) — city/coast views
- **Wow**: Red-shanked douc langurs
- **Low energy**: Fitting + resort only

#### Day 12 — Saturday, Sept 12: My Son + Pickup
- **Early**: **My Son Sanctuary** (7:30 departure)
- **Late morning**: Hội An tailor **final pickup**
- **Lunch**: White Rose dumplings (authorized shop)
- **Afternoon**: Beach
- **Wow**: Jungle temples in morning mist
- **Rainy**: Swap My Son to Day 13

#### Day 13 — Sunday, Sept 13: Final Beach + Shopping
- **Morning**: Sunrise Mỹ Khê walk
- **Midday**: Farewell resort brunch
- **Afternoon**: Vincom / Indochina Riverside — coffee, lacquerware
- **Evening**: NON La or Madame Lân — farewell dinner
- **Wow**: Last sunset on beach

#### Day 14 — Monday, Sept 14: DEPARTURE
- Checkout; DAD airport **2.5 hrs** early for international
- Connection via ICN, TPE, or SIN
- Carry-on: visas, tailor garments, valuables
