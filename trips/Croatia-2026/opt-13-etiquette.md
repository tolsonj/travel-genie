# Etiquette

Croatia is relaxed, but locals notice small courtesy signals: greet politely, keep voices calm in old towns, and respect churches/monuments with straightforward clothing.

### Dining & gestures

- Greeting: “Dobar dan” (good day) or a simple smile + nod is perfect before ordering.
- Seating: Wait to be shown your table; don’t grab prime spots while staff are still seating.
- Payment: Ask for the check when ready; many places bring the terminal to your table.
