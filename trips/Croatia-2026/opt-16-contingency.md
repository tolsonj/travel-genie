# Contingency

Small plan changes beat big stress: one emergency checklist, then two “if heat/rain hits” alternatives.

### Heat / rain fallback

- If you hit peak heat: swap outdoor sights for a café lunch + short, shaded streets walk.
- If rain slows you down: switch to indoor historic exhibits (palaces, museums) and resume waterfront later.
- If transport shifts: keep the “arrival hour” flexible and protect your evening dinner slot.
