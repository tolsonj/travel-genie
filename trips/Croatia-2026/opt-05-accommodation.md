### Booking strategy

| Tactic | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Prioritize flexible cancellation | Heat + crowds can force itinerary micro-adjustments |
| Choose “walkable old-town” or “short-transfer” locations | Less uphill walking, more relaxed pace |
| Breakfast-first neighborhoods | Late brunch is still easier when breakfast is nearby |

### Notes

- Prefer air-conditioned rooms or strong ventilation (coastal humidity still feels heavy).
