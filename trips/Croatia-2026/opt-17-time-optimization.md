# Time Optimization

A short 7-day circuit rewards “date flexibility” and crowd-timing more than complex routing.

### PTO checklist

- Aim for arrival/morning buffer so Day 1 starts gently.
- If you can shift flights by ±1 day, choose the option with the smallest “crowd penalty” window.

### Date-flex savings (if flights vary)

| Leg | Shift day | Possible savings | Notes |
|-----|-----------:|-------------------:|-------|
| ATL → DBV | -1 day | $40–$120 (unverified) | Pick earlier departure to protect Day 2 energy |
| ZAD → ATL | +1 day | $30–$90 (unverified) | Trade “last-day pace” for a better fare |
