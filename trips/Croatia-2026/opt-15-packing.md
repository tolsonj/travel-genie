# Packing

Pack for sea-breeze evenings and August sun: breathable layers first, then “only if needed” extras.

1. **Passport / ID** — keep physical + a scan copy in your day bag.
2. **Power + charging** — universal adapter, short cable, and a small power bank.
3. **Sun protection** — sunscreen + hat (coastal sun is intense).
4. **Comfort shoes** — good walking shoes for old streets and viewpoints.
5. **First aid mini kit** — blister care + pain relief.

**Documents**
- [ ] Passport (physical)
- [ ] Passport scan (offline)
- [ ] Travel insurance proof

**Electronics**
- [ ] Phone + charger
- [ ] Offline maps downloaded
- [ ] Power bank
