# Master Itinerary

### Trip overview

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Duration | Aug 8–Aug 15, 2026 · 7 Croatia days (relaxed pace) |

### Day 1 — Arrival + first Old Town stroll

**Location:** Dubrovnik

- **Morning**: Arrive, hydrate, and do an easy “orientation loop” near your first coffee stop.
- **Afternoon**: Stradun walk + short viewpoint detour; plan shade breaks every 60–90 minutes.
- **Evening**: Sea-front dinner and a slow sunset promenade.

**Wow moment**: First time you see the Adriatic opening up from the walls.

### Day 2 — Old Town history + sea-salt seafood

**Location:** Dubrovnik

- **Morning**: Historical core walk (keep it short but meaningful); timed-entry style if needed.
- **Afternoon**: Late brunch + waterfront downtime; shop for small local crafts.
- **Evening**: Sunset viewpoint (short climb) with a “late dessert, no rush” mindset.

**Low energy**: No long museum marathons — prioritize views and local food.

### Day 3 — Dubrovnik → Split (coast transfer day)

**Location:** Split

- **Morning**: Early goodbye coffee; one last quick viewpoint before the hop.
- **Afternoon**: Arrival into Split + Diocletian-area wandering at a relaxed pace.
- **Evening**: Marina dinner and a first photo round in warm evening light.

**Transit**: Keep transfers under ~5 hours and protect energy for sunset.

### Day 4 — Split culture + coastal rhythm

**Location:** Split

- **Morning**: Palace/old-stones highlights (go early to beat the biggest crowds).
- **Afternoon**: AC lunch break; plan a casual harbor walk and souvenir browsing.
- **Evening**: Dinner with something “Adriatic” (grilled seafood or slow-cooked local dish).

**Wow moment**: Diocletian’s Palace feeling like a living maze, not just a site.

### Day 5 — Island or nature day option

**Location:** Split (with optional day trip)

- **Morning**: Nature-focused outing — choose either a nearby island beach or a park-like viewpoint hike (your “heat tolerance” decides).
- **Afternoon**: Picnic-style lunch + sea breeze reset.
- **Evening**: Return early enough for a calm, late-brunch tomorrow recovery.

**Low energy**: If heat peaks, switch to indoor cafés + scenic short stops only.

### Day 6 — Split → Zadar (sea organ sunset)

**Location:** Zadar

- **Morning**: Coach/ferry hop; arrive, store bags, and do an easy landing walk.
- **Afternoon**: Roman-era streets + slow lunch; keep errands clustered to reduce walking.
- **Evening**: Sea Organ + sunset “linger” (short, not exhausting).

**Wow moment**: The moment you realize the waterfront is the day’s highlight.

### Day 7 — Zadar finale + departure buffer

**Location:** Zadar / departure buffer

- **Morning**: Last local bites + a small shopping loop (postcards, small crafts, easy gifts).
- **Afternoon**: Pack + transit buffer to your departure plan (stay flexible for timing).
- **Evening**: Depart home.

**Transit**: Protect buffer time — August scheduling can shift with ferries/coach stops.
