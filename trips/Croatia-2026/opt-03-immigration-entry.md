# Immigration Entry

Plan your entry docs early and keep a single “passport + entry” folder on your phone and in print.

### Key checklist

- ✅ Confirm passport validity (typically 3+ months beyond entry date).
- ✅ Save your accommodation confirmation / address for the entry form.

### Travel authorization

| Item | Status / expectation | Notes |
|------|------------------------|-------|
| Visa | Often not required for short tourist stays (confirm) | United States passport holder — verify at the time of booking |
| Passport | Valid for required buffer window | Take a photo + export scans to cloud |
| Entry form | Keep confirmation screenshot | Useful if airline asks at check-in |

### Application timeline

| Step | Target date | Notes |
|------|--------------|-------|
| Verify requirements | 8–10 weeks before departure | Croatia entry rules can change seasonally |
| Collect documents | 6–8 weeks before | Passport + reservations + travel insurance proof |
| Final backups | 1 week before | Print + offline phone folder |

### Arrival / transit-only reminders

| Topic | What to prepare | Why it matters |
|-------|------------------|-----------------|
| Transit days | Keep a “carry-on docs” pouch | Fewer surprises during connections |
| Customs forms | Fill clearly | Speeds up entry desk processing |
