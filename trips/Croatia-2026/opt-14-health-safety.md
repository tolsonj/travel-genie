# Health Safety

Heat-aware planning keeps “nature + culture” fun instead of draining your energy.

### Pre-trip health kit

- Bring basic meds: antihistamine, pain relief, stomach comfort, and blister care.
- Pack sunscreen + a lightweight cap (summer sun is strong in coastal streets).
- Save clinic / pharmacy names from your hotel confirmation pages offline.

### Heat + hydration plan

| When | What to do | Why |
|------|--------------|------|
| Morning | Water first, then outdoor walk | Start cool and avoid heat spikes |
| Midday | Café / AC break | Recover between outdoor segments |
| Evening | Re-check foot comfort + hydration | Keep steps safe for night walks |

### Quick “when to slow down” rule

- If you feel dizzy, stop, shade, water, and cool down before continuing.
