# Shopping

**Spend priority:** Focus on small, locally made crafts (avoid midday “impulse” shopping when crowds peak).

### Recommended Shopping Days Per City

**Dubrovnik — Days 1–2 (Old Town heat + crowd plan)**

- **Morning**: Old Town artisan streets — ceramics, local textiles, small crafts
- **Afternoon**: Shade market loop — postcards + easy souvenirs (short stops only)
- **Budget**: $60–$120 (two souvenir rounds + gifts)

**Split — Days 3–4 (Palace-morning timing)**

- **Morning**: Diocletian-area lanes — leather goods + classic Adriatic essentials
- **Afternoon**: Marina shopping promenade — lighter items, olive-oil style gifts
- **Budget**: $80–$160 (spend where variety is highest)

**Zadar — Days 5–6 (Sunset souvenir finale)**

- **Morning**: Old town quick-hit stalls — local snacks + artisan kits
- **Afternoon**: Waterfront browse — minimal purchases, keep it relaxed
- **Budget**: $50–$110 (final gifts + postcards)

| Category | Dubrovnik | Split | Zadar | Winner |

| Leather / daily wear | Med | High | Med | Split |
