# Customs Borders

Keep the border day friction low: one document set, one “what can I bring back” checklist, and a calm customs mindset.

### Before you fly

- ✅ Keep receipts for anything “above normal gift amounts”.
- ✅ Take photos of: passport bio page + entry/exit stamps (or confirmations).
- ✅ Keep a small offline note listing your last hotel address.

### Trip customs overview (arrival + return)

| Topic | Quick rule | What to do |
|-------|------------|--------------|
| Duty-free | Limits depend on your travel/return details | Check the latest US guidance for returning travelers |
| Tax-free shopping | Some refunds require counters / stamps | Ask for the right documents at purchase |
| Carry-on | Liquids and electronics follow airline rules | Pack electronics charged + easy to reach |

### Duty-free & tax-free reminders

| Category | Typical expectation | Practical tip |
|----------|----------------------|----------------|
| Food items | Often allowed but declared if needed | Declare if unsure; save receipts |
| Alcohol/perfume | Refund eligibility varies | Keep packaging + travel documents together |
| Gifts | Bring “gift-ready” items | Add a gift note so they’re easy to explain at customs |
