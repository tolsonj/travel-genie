# Adventure

Adventure that fits a relaxed pace: one “nature lift” per hub, then an immediate cooling/reset afterward.

### Fitness match (no heroics)

- Comfortable walking + gentle climbs only (old streets, viewpoints, coastal paths).
- Bring water + sun protection; treat August as heat-first planning.

### Day activity fit

- Morning: outdoors + views.
- Afternoon: switch to indoor cultural moments or shaded stops.

### Adventure options

| Option | Hub | Best time | Safety note |
|--------|-----|------------|-------------|
| Coastal viewpoint loop | Dubrovnik | Morning | Wear breathable shoes; keep it short in peak sun |
| Island beach reset | Split | Late morning / early afternoon | Ferry timing changes; keep a backup café |
| Krka / park-style nature day | Zadar region | Early morning | Arrive early for shade + less crowding |
