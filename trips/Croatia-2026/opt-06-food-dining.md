# Food & Dining

Late brunch first, then a sea/city walk, then a reservation-free dinner plan that stays flexible in August heat.

### Meal map

| Day | Breakfast | Dinner |
|-----|------------|---------|
| Day 1 (Dubrovnik) | Espresso + local pastry | Grilled fish + sea-salt sides |
| Day 2 (Dubrovnik) | Brunch-style café breakfast | Slow-cooked dalmatian specialty |
| Day 3 (Split) | Bakery + fresh fruit | Waterfront seafood dinner |
| Day 4 (Split) | Late breakfast in the old streets | Classic “Adriatic grill” night |
| Day 5 (Split) | Easy snack breakfast | Island-style lunch + early dinner |
| Day 6 (Zadar) | Light morning bites | Roman-era tavern dinner (comfort pace) |
| Day 7 (Zadar) | Final pastries + coffee | Sunset-adjacent farewell dinner |

### Food budget

| Category | Daily range | Notes |
|-----------|-------------:|-------|
| Breakfast + coffee | $35–$55 | Late starts; keep it simple |
| Lunch / snacks | $40–$70 | Waterfront spots when weather peaks |
| Dinner (anchor) | $90–$140 | Reserve if a restaurant is truly “must-do” |
