# Hidden Gems

Small detours that keep the trip feeling personal — and avoid the “everybody went here” queues.

### Quick picks (crowd-avoid swaps)

- Go 20 minutes earlier than “top attraction” opening windows.
- If a square is packed, switch to a parallel sea-side street for 45 minutes.

### Neighborhood deep dive

| City | Hidden gem | Why it works | Best time |
|------|-------------|---------------|------------|
| Dubrovnik | Upper-walls short loop | Sea views without the longest crowds | Early morning |
| Split | Marina back-street lunch spot | Feels local; easy late-brunch pacing | Late morning |
| Zadar | Quiet waterfront park bend | Sunset vibes with less foot traffic | Golden hour |

### Nighttime secrets

- Keep evenings “low-effort”: one curated dinner + a 20–30 minute stroll max.
- Treat night walks as photo/light breaks, not full sightseeing days.
