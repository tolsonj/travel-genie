# Transport & Money

### Ground transport by city

#### Bangkok

| Use case | Mode | App / ticket | Est. cost | Notes |
|----------|------|--------------|----------:|-------|
| Airport ↔ Sukhumvit | **Airport Rail Link** + BTS | ARL ticket | ~THB 45 + BTS 17–47 | Phaya Thai connect |
| Sukhumvit / Siam / Chatuchak | **BTS + MRT** | Rabbit card or single | THB 17–47/trip | Avoid rush 7–9 AM |
| Short hops / late night | **Grab** | Grab app | THB 60–150 | Link Visa/MC |
| River (Wat Arun) | **Chao Phraya ferry** | Pier ticket | THB 16–32 | Scenic |

**Rabbit card:** Buy at BTS station THB 100 deposit + load THB 200.
