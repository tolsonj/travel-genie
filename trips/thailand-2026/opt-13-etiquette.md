# Etiquette

### Daily etiquette (10 norms)

| # | Norm | Context |
|---|------|---------|
| 1 | Remove shoes entering homes/temples | Always |
| 2 | Never touch anyone's head | Sacred in Thai culture |
| 3 | Don't point feet at Buddha/images | Sit knees tucked in temples |
| 4 | Dress modestly at temples | Shoulders + knees covered |
| 5 | Wai greeting — hands together, slight bow | Return wai to elders |
| 6 | Keep voice low in temples | Respect quiet |
| 7 | Stand for national anthem | Played in cinemas, public |
| 8 | Don't disrespect royal family | Legal offense |
| 9 | Smile — "sanuk" (fun) culture | Patience in queues |
| 10 | Haggle gently at markets | Not in malls |

### Survival phrases (10)

| English | Thai (phonetic) |
|---------|-----------------|

| Thank you | Khop khun (krap/ka) |
| Not spicy | Mai pet (krap/ka) |

| Too expensive | Paeng pai |
| Where is…? | … yoo tee nai? |
| Bathroom | Hong nam |
| Help! | Chooay duay! |

### Conversation starters

1. "What food should I try here?" — always welcome
2. "Is this temple very old?" — shows interest
3. "Where do locals eat?" — better than "best restaurant"
4. "Is it rainy season now?" — seasonal chat
5. "Chiang Mai or Bangkok — which do you prefer?" — friendly debate

### Gestures & taboos

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Use right hand for giving/receiving | Touch monks (women especially) |
| Lower head slightly passing Buddha | Step on threshold of temple |
| Public affection minimal | Raise voice in anger |

### Supporting locals

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| Eat at busy local stalls | Money to families |
| Buy crafts at night bazaar | Direct to artisans |
| Tip massage 50–100 THB | Not mandatory but appreciated |
| Skip orphanage tourism | Exploitative |
