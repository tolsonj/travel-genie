# Shopping

## Reasoning

**Itinerary scope:** Bangkok (Aug 2–3), Chiang Mai (Aug 5 night bazaar), Krabi (minimal — focus nature). Shopping days on non-transit days only.

**Social anchor:** Bangkok dominates TikTok/IG shopping content — ICONSIAM, Chatuchak, MBK, platinum mall. Chiang Mai: night bazaar, Nimman crafts. Krabi: skip major shopping except beachwear.

**Profile fit:** Interests include shopping; budget is modest ($5K total) — focus value + experience over luxury watch/handbag hunting (Step 6 categories scaled down for budget).

**VAT:** Thailand 7% VAT — tourist VAT refund at airports for qualifying purchases >2,000 THB/single shop, 5,000 THB total — BKK departure on outbound KBV→ATL may require BKK airport refund counter if purchased in Bangkok.
