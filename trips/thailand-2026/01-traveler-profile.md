# Traveler Profile

## Reasoning

**Country count vs timeframe:** One country (Thailand) over 7 hub nights is ideal for a relaxed solo trip. Three hubs — Bangkok (history, food, shopping), Chiang Mai (temples, mountains, cooler air), Krabi (beach + limestone karsts) — deliver all four interests without multi-country border friction.

**Entry/exit fit:** Profile leaves start city to the planner. **Open-jaw BKK in / KBV out** avoids backtracking to Bangkok and puts the beach finale where the "perfect day" (late brunch, beach walk, dramatic views) lands naturally.

**Late July / early August window:** Southwest monsoon peaks on the Andaman (Krabi) — expect afternoon showers and choppy seas; Gulf islands would be drier but Krabi's Railay/Ao Nang still works with rain backups and lower crowds than peak Dec–Feb. Bangkok and Chiang Mai are hot-humid (32–35°C) — profile deal-breakers managed via dawn starts, AC hotels, and indoor midday blocks. Shoulder pricing helps the **$5,000** budget.

**Travel party:** Solo, one king/queen room, economy from **ATL**, 1 passenger — straightforward booking and Grab-first ground transport (profile: taxis were ripoffs; mass transit worked).

**Decision rule:** Vote when traveling with others — solo trip, so planner optimizes without forks unless noted as optional swaps.

**Budget sanity:** $5,000 all-in ≈ $714/day. Unverified flight total ~$2,350 (~47%) leaves ~$2,650 for 7 hotel nights (~$95/night avg at 3–4★), food, domestic flights, activities — tight but feasible with 3–4★ (not 5★) and street-food-heavy dining.

**Interest weighting:** Food 25% · Nature 25% · Shopping 25% · History 25% — balanced across Bangkok (history/shopping/food), Chiang Mai (nature/history/food), Krabi (nature/beach).
