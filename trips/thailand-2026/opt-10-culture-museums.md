### Deep history (3 facts)

1. **Bangkok:** Founded 1782 after Ayutthaya fall — Grand Palace is 18th-c. Rattanakosin capital shift.
2. **Chiang Mai:** Lanna Kingdom ("million rice fields") — distinct culture from Bangkok central Thai.
3. **Krabi:** Limestone karsts are 260M-year-old coral reef remnants — visible geology lesson.
