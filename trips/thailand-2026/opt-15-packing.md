# Packing

### Do not forget (15)

1. Passport (6+ mo validity)
2. Travel insurance card
3. eSIM QR / confirmation
4. Universal power adapter
5. Lightweight rain jacket
6. Modest temple cover (scarf + long pants)
7. Hiking sandals/shoes
8. Reef-safe sunscreen SPF 50
9. Insect repellent
10. Hand sanitizer
11. Reusable water bottle
12. Grab-ready phone + Visa
13. Printed hotel confirmations
14. TDAC QR screenshot
15. 10,000 mAh power bank
