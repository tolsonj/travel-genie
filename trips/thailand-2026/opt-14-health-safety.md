# Health & Safety

### Pre-trip health

| Item | Action |
|------|--------|
| Insurance | Multi-trip or single — cover medical + evacuation |
| Vaccines | Hep A, typhoid (CDC); routine boosters current |
| Rx meds | Full supply + doctor letter |
| First aid | Bandages, ORS, antidiarrheal, pain reliever |

### Health per hub

| Hub | Hospital | Tap water | Food safety |
|-----|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Bangkok | Bumrungrad (Intl) | No | Busy stalls OK |
| Chiang Mai | Chiang Mai Ram | No | Same |
| Krabi | Ao Nang Hospital | No | Seafood freshness check |

### Safety per hub

| Hub | Scams | Avoid |
|-----|-------|-------|
| Bangkok | Gem scam, tuk-tuk temple loop | Unmarked taxis |
| Chiang Mai | Elephant abuse camps | Unlicensed trek guides |
| Krabi | Jet-ski damage claim | Rough sea boats |

### Heat protocol (deal-breaker)

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 11 AM–3 PM | Indoor/AC when possible |
| Outdoor | Hat, SPF 50, 2L water/day |
| Signs of heat illness | Stop, shade, ORS, clinic if vomiting |
