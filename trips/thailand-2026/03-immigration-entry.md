# Immigration and Entry

## Reasoning

**Citizenship:** US passport holder (assumed from ATL home airport and profile context). Thailand offers **visa exemption** for US tourists — no pre-application for stays under 60 days (2026 policy; verify before travel).

**Single country:** No inter-country visas; domestic flights BKK–CNX–KBV are internal — carry passport for ID at airports only.

**Passport validity:** Thailand requires **6 months** beyond entry date (Aug 1, 2026 → valid through Feb 2027 minimum).

**Health:** No yellow fever certificate from US. CDC recommends Hep A, typhoid for extended stays; trip is 7 nights — routine vaccines sufficient for most travelers.

**2026-specific:** Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) may replace paper TM6 — check [immigration.go.th](https://www.immigration.go.th) T-30d.
