| Slot | Plan |

| **Afternoon** | Depart **ATL** (~1:00–5:00 PM typical for Asia connections) |

### Day 2 — Saturday, Aug 1 · **SOFT DAY** · Arrive Bangkok

| Slot | Plan |
|------|------|
| **Morning** | Land **BKK** (~8:00–11:00 AM); **Airport Rail Link** to Phaya Thai + Grab to hotel; drop bags |
| **Afternoon** | **Late brunch** at hotel or **Roast** (Thonglor) — slow settle |
| **Evening** | Gentle **Lumphini Park** walk or **Asiatique** riverfront (if energy) |

**Wow moment:** First glimpse of Bangkok skyline at dusk.  
**Low energy:** Hotel pool/nap 2–4 PM.  
**Rainy-day backup:** **ICONSIAM** indoor mall + river views.  
**Skip the trap:** Tuk-tuk "20 baht temple tour" scams near Khao San.  
**Free:** Lumphini walk · **Splurge:** Riverside brunch.  
**Transit:** Rail Link + Grab <60 min.  
**Documents:** Passport; TDAC if required; hotel confirmation.

---

### Day 3 — Sunday, Aug 2 · Bangkok · History + river

| Slot | Plan |
|------|------|
| **Morning** | **Grand Palace + Wat Phra Kaew** — depart hotel by **7:30 AM** (crowd avoidance) |
| **Afternoon** | **Wat Arun** (cross-river ferry); AC lunch **Supanniga Home** or street pad thai |
| **Evening** | **Chao Phraya dinner cruise** (optional) OR **Chinatown** (Yaowarat) street food |

**Wow moment:** Emerald Buddha hall morning light.  
**Low energy:** Ferry ride only; seated dinner.  
**Rainy-day backup:** **National Museum** Bangkok.  
**Skip the trap:** Grand Palace "closed" touts outside gate.  
**Free:** Wat Arun entry modest fee · **Splurge:** Dinner cruise.  
**Transit:** Grab + ferry ~45 min total.

### Day 4 — Monday, Aug 3 · Bangkok · Shopping + food

| Slot | Plan |
|------|------|
| **Morning** | **Chatuchak Weekend Market** (if Sun Aug 2 missed, use **JJ Green** Mon) OR **Or Tor Kor** market |
| **Afternoon** | **Siam Paragon / CentralWorld** shopping (AC); lunch **Thipsamai** pad thai |
| **Evening** | **Rooftop bar** — **Octave** or **Vertigo** for city views (profile nature views) |

**Wow moment:** 360° rooftop sunset over Bangkok sprawl.  
**Low energy:** Mall browsing seated.  
**Rainy-day backup:** Full mall day Siam district.  
**Skip the trap:** Fake designer alleys near Nana.  
**Free:** Window shopping · **Splurge:** Rooftop drink + mall finds.  
**Transit:** BTS Mo Chit / Siam ~30 min.

### Day 5 — Tuesday, Aug 4 · **SOFT HUB DAY** · BKK → Chiang Mai

| Slot | Plan |
|------|------|
| **Morning** | Checkout; Grab to **DMK or BKK**; fly **BKK→CNX** (~10:00 AM) |
| **Afternoon** | Land CNX; Grab to Old City hotel; rest; **late lunch** khao soi **Khao Soi Khun Yai** |
| **Evening** | Short **Old City wall** stroll; early night |

**Wow moment:** First view of Doi Suthep from Old City at dusk.  
**Low energy:** Hotel rest; wall walk only.  
**Rainy-day backup:** **MAIIAM** contemporary art museum.  
**Skip the trap:** Red taxi meters "broken" at airport.  
**Free:** Wall walk · **Splurge:** Khao soi dinner.  
**Transit:** Flight 1h15 + Grab 20 min.

### Day 6 — Wednesday, Aug 5 · Chiang Mai · Temples + nature

| Slot | Plan |
|------|------|
| **Morning** | **Doi Suthep** — depart by 8:00 AM (cooler, fewer buses) |
| **Afternoon** | **Wat Chedi Luang** + **Wat Phra Singh** Old City (AC cafés between) |
| **Evening** | **Chiang Mai Night Bazaar** — crafts + street food |

**Wow moment:** Golden chedi view from Doi Suthep terrace.  
**Low energy:** Old City temples on foot (flat).  
**Rainy-day backup:** **Lanna Folklife Museum**.  
**Skip the trap:** Elephant "sanctuary" without research.  
**Free:** Night bazaar browsing · **Splurge:** Doi Suthep tram.  
**Transit:** Grab to Doi Suthep ~30 min each way.

### Day 7 — Thursday, Aug 6 · Transit + arrive · CNX → Krabi

| Slot | Plan |
|------|------|
| **Morning** | Checkout; fly **CNX→KBV** (via BKK, ~9:00 AM depart) |
| **Afternoon** | Land **KBV**; Grab to **Ao Nang** hotel; beach rest |
| **Evening** | **Ao Nang Beach** sunset walk — profile perfect-day element |

**Wow moment:** Limestone karsts silhouetted at sunset from Ao Nang.  
**Low energy:** Beach lounging only.  
**Rainy-day backup:** Hotel spa or **Ao Nang massage**.  
**Skip the trap:** Patong-style party push from touts (you're in Ao Nang).  
**Free:** Beach walk · **Splurge:** Seafood dinner **The Hilltop**.  
**Transit:** ~4h door-to-door including connection.

### Day 8 — Friday, Aug 7 · Krabi · Nature + beach

| Slot | Plan |
|------|------|
| **Morning** | **Railay East viewpoint hike** (if dry) OR **Tiger Cave Temple** (366 steps, views) |
| **Afternoon** | **Late brunch** beachside; long-tail boat **Phra Nang Cave Beach** if seas calm |
| **Evening** | Final **beach walk** + pack for early departure |

**Wow moment:** Railay viewpoint panorama over Andaman.  
**Low energy:** Beach only; skip hike.  
**Rainy-day backup:** **Krabi Town** indoor market + café.  
**Skip the trap:** Overpriced island speedboat in rough seas.  
**Free:** Viewpoint hike · **Splurge:** Long-tail private boat.  
**Transit:** Long-tail 15 min from Ao Nang if boats running.
