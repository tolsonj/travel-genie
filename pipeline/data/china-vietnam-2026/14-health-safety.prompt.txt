# Health & Safety

## Reasoning

Both travelers are in excellent physical condition with no health limitations. September: mild heat risk in Vietnam (average 28–32°C in Đà Nẵng), monsoon food hygiene risks, and mosquito-borne illness (dengue, Japanese encephalitis) are the main medical concerns. Neither country is high-risk for political safety. Adventure activities in [[11-adventure]] are moderate-intensity — no extreme sport medical clearance needed. Travel insurance with medical evacuation is non-negotiable given travel to China where medical care for foreigners is complex.

#### Travel Insurance

**Must-have coverage**:
- Medical evacuation (China: medical care for foreigners is expensive and evacuation to Hong Kong or home may be necessary in serious cases)
- Trip cancellation for typhoon (Vietnam leg)
- Pre-existing conditions waiver
- Activity coverage: hiking (Great Wall), snorkeling (Cham Islands), water activities
- Baggage loss

**Recommended providers**:
- World Nomads (worldnomads.com): Standard Explorer plan; covers adventure activities; medical evacuation included
- Allianz Travel: OneTrip Prime; good medical limits; widely recognized in Asia
- **Purchase**: Same day as first flight booking to activate pre-departure cancellation coverage

#### Prescription Medications
- Bring sufficient supply for entire 14 days + 3 extra days buffer
- Original prescription from doctor + note in English (for customs)
- Controlled substances: Check SAMHSA list against China and Vietnam regulations — common anxiety medications and some sleep aids face import restrictions
- Anti-diarrheal (Imodium) + oral rehydration salts: Pack in carry-on
- Altitude sickness: Not relevant for this route (all cities are at sea level or low elevation)

#### China — Beijing

**Hospitals near accommodation (The Peninsula Beijing)**:

| Hospital | Distance | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|
| **Beijing United Family Hospital** (和睦家医院) | 5 km; ~15 min by DiDi | International standard; English-speaking staff; works with foreign insurance |

**Tap water**: Do NOT drink; bottled water everywhere; hotel water is fine for brushing teeth with caution
**Food safety**: See [[06-food-dining]]; cooked food at high-turnover restaurants is safe; avoid raw salads at market stalls
**Air quality**: September Beijing air quality is typically Moderate/Good (monsoon season clears pollution); check AQI app daily; carry N95 masks if AQI exceeds 150 on any day

**Common health issues in Beijing for tourists**:
- GI issues (food/water adjustment) — most common; resolved with Imodium + hydration
- Jet lag — 12–13 hr time difference from US East; allow Day 1 for adjustment
- Sunburn — September sun in Beijing is still strong; apply SPF 50 daily

### Safety Per Country

#### Beijing Safety

| Risk | Level | Mitigation |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Petty theft | Low | Wallet in front pocket; camera strap around wrist; standard vigilance |
| Scam | Moderate | Tea ceremony scam (young woman invites you for tea → ¥2,000+ bill); art gallery scam; English student scam — decline all impromptu invitations |
| Traffic | Moderate | Look right THEN left at crossings; electric scooters run silently on sidewalks |
| Political | Very Low | Don't engage in political conversations; don't photograph military installations |

**Tea ceremony scam**: In Beijing, particularly near Tiananmen and Wangfujing, well-dressed Chinese "students" will approach speaking good English and invite you to a nearby tea ceremony. This is a coordinated scam — you'll receive a large bill. Decline politely.

#### Vietnam Safety (Hanoi + Đà Nẵng/Hội An)

| Risk | Level | Mitigation |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Bag snatching | Moderate in Hanoi | Don't walk with phone/purse dangling from shoulder near streets; motorbikes can snatch |
| Taxi scams | High (at airports) | Use Grab exclusively; see [[07-transport-money]] |
| Overcharging | Moderate at markets | Negotiate; know approximate prices; have small bills ready |
| Flooding/typhoon | Moderate in Sept | Monitor weather; resort is your shelter; see [[16-contingency]] |
| Traffic crossing | High (perceived) | Walk at steady pace; motorbikes flow around you |

**Hội An bag snatch risk**: Low in the Ancient Town itself (pedestrian area); moderate on the riverside road. Keep camera strap across body; phone in zipped pocket at night.
