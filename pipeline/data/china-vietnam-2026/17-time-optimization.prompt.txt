### Jet Lag Management

**Time zone**: US Eastern (UTC-4) → Beijing (UTC+8) = 12 hours forward
US Pacific (UTC-7) → Beijing (UTC+8) = 15 hours forward

**Strategy — Outbound (US → Beijing)**:
- **Day 0 (flight)**: Stay awake until Beijing local time ~9–10 PM; force alignment to local timezone from Day 1
- **Day 1 (Beijing)**: Do NOT nap; exposure to natural light (afternoon walk to Wangfujing) resets circadian rhythm; go to bed at 10 PM local time; set alarm for 8 AM
- **Day 2**: Near-normal; slight fatigue afternoon is normal
- **Day 3 (Great Wall)**: Full energy; jet lag typically resolved by Day 3 with this protocol

**Melatonin**: 0.5–1 mg at local bedtime on Days 1 and 2 (not 5–10 mg — the marketing dose; 0.5 mg is optimal for circadian reset)

**Arrival at each new city**:
- Beijing arrival Day 1: Light, no demanding activities — CORRECT ✓
- Hanoi arrival Day 6 evening: Arrive, walk lake, sleep — CORRECT ✓
- Đà Nẵng arrival Day 8 midday: Resort check-in, beach walk, room service — CORRECT ✓

**Return jet lag**: Eastbound is easier; your body naturally extends the day; stay up one extra hour each day; resolved within 2–3 days.
