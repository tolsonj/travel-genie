### Scenario 1: Weather Ruins Outdoor Day

**Heat advisory (>35°C real-feel)**
- **Action**: Execute rainy/heat backups already in [[04-master-itinerary]]—shift outdoor block to 06:00 start OR swap to indoor pivot
- **Tokyo**: teamLab Planets, Teien Art Museum, depachika food floors
- **Takayama**: Sake breweries, Hida Folk Village partial cover, hotel onsen
- **Kyoto**: Railway Museum, Manga Museum, covered Nishiki Market
- **Osaka**: Kaiyukan aquarium, TeamLab Osaka, Umeda Sky if evening clears
- **No phone needed** for most pivots—self-directed

**Heavy rain / thunderstorm**
- Use per-day **Rainy backup** lines in [[04-master-itinerary]]
- Fushimi Inari: light rain OK with umbrella; **cancel if lightning**
- Arashiyama: swap to Sagano Romantic Train (if booked) or indoor museum day

**Typhoon watch (multi-day)**
- Monitor JMA + NHK World; see Scenario 10 below

### Scenario 2: Typhoon Threatens KIX Departure (Aug 4–6)

| Level | Response |
|-------|----------|
| **Watch (5+ days out)** | Confirm Aug 6 flight on airline app; screenshot confirmation; charge all devices |
| **Approach (48 hrs)** | Contact airline re: free change policy; identify backup flights Aug 7–8; notify employer if PTO tight |
| **Landfall (24 hrs)** | Shelter in Osaka hotel; **do not attempt KIX**; eat konbini supplies; stay away from Dotonbori riverfront |
| **Airport closure** | Airline rebooks automatically or via app; call insurer **trip interruption** line immediately; document JMA advisory screenshots |
| **Post-storm** | Allow 24 hrs for train/flight normalization; JR West status: [westjr.co.jp](https://www.westjr.co.jp) |

**Osaka hotel**: Ask front desk typhoon protocol (most business hotels have backup generator + English notices).

### Scenario 3: Missed Shinkansen or Reserved Train

**Tokyo → Takayama (Day 6)**
- **Next train**: Tokaido Shinkansen runs every 10–20 min Tokyo→Nagoya; Hida Limited Express ~hourly Nagoya→Takayama
- **Action**: Go to JR ticket office (Midori-no-madoguchi); exchange next available Hida seat with JR Pass
- **Delay cost**: Arrive Takayama afternoon vs morning—execute **Low energy option** (hotel rest; shorten Sanmachi walk)
- **Hotel**: Call/email Takayama lodging; Japanese hotels hold until ~18:00 if notified
- **Insurance**: Claim if total delay >6 hrs and documented (rare on JR)

**Takayama → Kyoto (Day 8)**
- Same protocol via Nagoya connection
- **Luggage**: If takkyubin sent Jul 28, bag should already be at Kyoto hotel—proceed without checked bag

**Kyoto → Osaka (Day 12)**
- Trains every 10 min; minimal disruption; next Special Rapid in 15 min

**JR Pass seat reservation lost**: Re-reserve at any JR office with passport + pass; no penalty.

### Scenario 4: Illness — Food Poisoning, Heat Exhaustion, or Flu

**Mild (upset stomach, headache, low fever <100°F)**
- Rest half-day at hotel; konbini rice balls + electrolytes
- Skip optional afternoon block; keep evening meal light
- **Recoverable**: 1 lost half-day fits within 70% rule ([[17-time-optimization]])

**Moderate (fever, vomiting, unable to walk)**
- **Tokyo**: Tokyo Medical and Surgical Clinic +81-3-3436-3028 (appointment/walk-in)
- **Kyoto**: Japan Baptist Hospital (English)
- **Osaka**: Primary Care Osaka or hotel concierge doctor call
- **Call insurance 24/7 line first** for clinic referral + payment guidance
- Cancel next day's early Fushimi/Arashiyama start—sleep in; swap anchor to following day if energy returns

**Serious (chest pain, high fever >102°F, dehydration, injury on trail)**
- Call **119** (ambulance) or hotel front desk to call for you
- **Do not hike** Fushimi/Arashiyama with fever—heat + stairs = dangerous
- **Flight home**: Airline may deny boarding with active fever—get clinic clearance note
- **Insurance**: Medical evacuation clause if hospitalization required

### Scenario 5: Shirakawa-go Bus Cancelled or Sold Out

**Prevention**: Book round-trip bus 4 weeks ahead (Jul 30 morning slot)—see [[04-master-itinerary]] Booking Queue.

**If cancelled (weather/road closure)**
- **Pivot**: Execute Day 7 **Low energy option**—Takayama morning market + onsen + Hida Folk Village
- **Alternative**: Hida Folk Village open-air museum (gassho-style buildings; partial rain cover)
- **Refund**: Bus operator refunds automatically for weather cancellations—keep confirmation email

**If missed bus (overslept)**
- Next bus may be sold out in peak season—**no same-day recovery**
- Swap to Takayama-only day; Shirakawa-go is the trip's main regret risk—protect 08:00 departure

### Scenario 6: Lost Passport

**Step 1**: Retrace steps; check hotel lost-and-found (Japan returns lost items at very high rate)

**Step 2**: File police report at koban (交番) or police station—required for embassy
- Police: **110**

**Step 3**: US Embassy Tokyo +81-3-3224-5000 (24/7) OR Osaka Consulate +81-6-6315-5900 if in Kansai
- Emergency passport (limited validity) issued in **1–3 business days**
- Bring: police report, passport photo (2×2), proof of citizenship (digital copy), travel itinerary, $170 fee

**Step 4**: Delay KIX departure if needed; contact airline with embassy letter; activate insurance

**Prevention**: Digital + paper passport copies per [[14-health-safety]]; never leave passport in takkyubin bag.

### Scenario 7: JR Pass Problem (Wrong Activation, Lost Voucher, Hida Supplement)

**Wrong activation date**
- Pass activates on first JR gate tap—if activated early, you lose days
- **Fix**: Cannot undo; buy individual tickets for uncovered legs OR accept lost pass days; calculate at JR office

**Lost voucher before exchange**
- Contact vendor (Klook/official JR Pass site) for reissue email—allow 24 hrs

**Hida Limited Express seat fee**
- JR Pass covers base fare; **seat reservation free** at JR office; Green Car requires upgrade fee
- If told to pay large supplement: ask for English supervisor; show JR Pass coverage chart

**Pass math fails (individual tickets cheaper)**
- Rare for this itinerary; if so, buy individual Shinkansen tickets Tokyo→Nagoya segments only; keep pass for Hida + Kansai

### Scenario 8: Takkyubin / Luggage Lost or Delayed

**Bag not at Kyoto hotel on Day 8**
- Call Yamato Transport (Kuroneko) **English line**: +81-57-0-003-010; tracking number from Tokyo hotel receipt
- **Carry-on has**: 2 days clothes, meds, docs, electronics—continue trip
- **Emergency buy**: Uniqlo full outfit ~¥8,000 ($55); Kyoto Kawaramachi 10 min from most hotels
- **Insurance**: Baggage delay >6 hrs—keep receipts for essentials (toothbrush, shirt, underwear cap ~$200)

**Sent bag to wrong hotel**
- Show tracking slip to current hotel front desk—they will call Yamato for redirect (usually +1 day)

### Scenario 9: Budget Overrun

**Early overspend triggers**: Extra omakase, taxi overuse, impulse electronics in Akihabara.

| Cut | Savings |
|-----|---------|
| Skip Hida beef splurge → soba/izakaya | ~¥8,000 ($55) |
| Convenience-store breakfast all week | ~¥500/day |
| Skip paid teamLab/Kaiyukan (use free rainy backups) | ~¥3,000–4,000 |
| Walk vs taxi in Kyoto | ~¥1,000/trip |
| One less souvenir shopping session (Kappabashi/Kyoto crafts) | Variable |
| Downgrade final Osaka dinner to kuromon grazing | ~¥5,000 |

**Do not cut**: Travel insurance, Shirakawa-go bus, JR Pass seat reservations, KIX airport timing buffer.

**Emergency fund**: Keep ¥30,000 (~$200) cash reserve + unused credit headroom.

---

### Scenario 10: Earthquake During Trip

**During earthquake**
- **Drop, cover, hold on**; stay away from windows/signs
- After shaking stops: check J-Alert app; follow hotel evacuation instructions
- Trains stop automatically—expect 30 min–several hr suspension

**Minor quake (common)**
- No itinerary change; verify Shinkansen status before Day 6/8 travel

**Major quake (M7+ affecting travel)**
- Contact US Embassy; defer rail travel until JR all-clear
- **Insurance**: Trip interruption if outbound flight cancelled
- **KIX departure**: Check airport operational status; airlines waive change fees during declared disasters (verify real-time)
