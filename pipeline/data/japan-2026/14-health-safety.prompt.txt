**Travel Medicine Clinic**: Schedule May–June 2026; bring printed [[04-master-itinerary]]; verify CDC Japan notices at [travel.cdc.gov](https://travel.cdc.gov).

#### Travel Insurance

**Must-have coverage**:
- Emergency medical + **medical evacuation** (helicopter rescue from Alps trails = $10,000+ without coverage)
- Trip interruption / cancellation for **typhoon** or flight disruption (KIX departure Aug 6)
- Activity coverage: **hiking on marked trails**, cycling (Arashiyama), walking tours
- Baggage delay (takkyubin misdelivery rare but possible)
- Pre-existing condition waiver if applicable

**Recommended providers**:
- **World Nomads Explorer** (~$130–160 for 14 days solo): Covers hiking Category 1–3; evacuation included
- **Allianz OneTrip Premier** (~$110–140): Strong medical limits; widely accepted at international clinics
- **Purchase**: Same day as first flight booking to activate pre-departure cancellation

**Policy card**: Save 24/7 emergency line in phone contacts as **INSURANCE EMERGENCY**; print one copy for wallet.

#### Prescription Medications
- 14-day supply + **3 extra days** buffer in carry-on (never checked)
- Original prescription + doctor note in English (Japan customs may inspect controlled substances)
- **Japan import restrictions**: Codeine, amphetamine-class ADHD meds, and some sleep aids face strict limits—verify via [mhlw.go.jp](https://www.mhlw.go.jp) "Guide for Bringing Medicines" or embassy guidance; apply for **Yunyu Kakunin-sho** (import certificate) if required
- **OTC carry-on**: Imodium, oral rehydration salts, ibuprofen, antihistamine

### Heat & Typhoon Protocol (Late July–Early August)

#### Heat Management

| Risk zone | Typical conditions | Mitigation |
|-----------|-------------------|------------|
| **Tokyo** (Days 1–5) | 30–33°C, humid | Outdoor blocks before 10:00 or after 16:00 per [[04-master-itinerary]] |
| **Takayama / Alps** (Days 6–7) | 25–30°C (cooler) | Still hydrate; Shirakawa-go has limited shade mid-day |
| **Kyoto** (Days 8–11) | 32–35°C, brutal humidity | Fushimi **05:30**; Kiyomizu **06:30**; midday AC/indoor |
| **Osaka** (Days 12–13) | 32–35°C | Castle exterior morning only; Kaiyukan/indoor backup |

**Heat exhaustion signs**: Dizziness, nausea, stopped sweating, confusion → move to AC immediately, oral rehydration, cool wet cloth on neck. **Heat stroke** (altered mental state) → call **119** immediately.

**Daily hydration target**: 2.5–3 L water on walking days; convenience stores everywhere for cold bottles + electrolyte drinks (Pocari Sweat, Aquarius).

#### Typhoon Protocol

**Monitor**: [Japan Meteorological Agency (JMA)](https://www.jma.go.jp/en/), NHK World app, hotel front desk notices.

| Level | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **Tropical storm watch** (3–5 days out) | Confirm KIX departure Aug 6; download offline maps; charge power bank |
| **Typhoon approach** (24–48 hrs) | Cancel outdoor castle/temple blocks; shift to indoor pivots (Kaiyukan, museums, depachika); avoid river/waterfront walks |
| **Typhoon landfall** | Shelter in hotel; **do not travel**; Shinkansen/JR may suspend—see [[16-contingency]] |
| **Post-storm** | Trains resume gradually; allow +1 day buffer before international departure if possible |

**Highest-risk days for disruption**: Aug 4–6 (Osaka/KIX window). If typhoon threatens Aug 6 departure, contact airline immediately; travel insurance trip-interruption clause applies.

### Safety Per Region

| Risk | Level | Mitigation |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Petty theft | Very low | Japan is among safest globally; still watch bags on crowded trains |
| Scams | Low | Overpriced bars in Golden Gai/Shinsekai—check menu prices before ordering |
| Traffic | Low–moderate | Look **both ways** at crossings; cyclists on sidewalks in Kyoto |
| Earthquake | Moderate (constant low probability) | Know hotel evacuation route; J-Alert app; "drop, cover, hold on" |
| Heat illness | **High** (seasonal) | Midday indoor pivot; electrolytes; know 119 protocol |
| Typhoon | Moderate (seasonal) | Monitor JMA; indoor backup days built into [[04-master-itinerary]] |
| Bear (Alps) | Very low | Stay on marked paths; optional bear bell at trail shops ¥500 |
| Crowd crush | Moderate | Deal-breaker from [[profile-japan]]—early starts per [[17-time-optimization]] |

**Night safety**: Solo walking in Tokyo/Kyoto/Osaka tourist zones is generally safe; standard urban awareness after midnight in entertainment districts.
