# Shopping Strategy

### Japan Shopping Strategy (Solo)

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Buy late, carry light** | Major souvenirs in **Kyoto/Osaka** last week; Tokyo for specialty only |
| **Tax-free** | Passport + same-day purchase ≥ ¥5,000 at registered stores; unopened until customs |
| **Payment** | IC card + contactless Visa; some small shops cash-only |
| **Packing** | Soft foldables in carry-on; ship heavy via **takkyubin** from department store desks |

---

### Tax-Free (免税)

1. Look for **Tax-Free** sign; show passport at counter (not vending machines).
2. Categories: consumables (food/cosmetics) vs general (electronics, clothing) — **separate bags**, sealed until departure.
3. **Customs**: You are a **US visitor** — Japan export OK; US import duty-free exemption ~$800 **aggregate** merchandise; declare over limit.
4. **Receipt**: Keep until KIX; staff may staple passport copy — remove carefully at immigration.

**Best tax-free stops**: Don Quijote (Osaka Namba), Bic Camera (Kyoto Station), department store duty-free desks (Isetan/Shiseido counters).

### Traps to Avoid

1. **Multi-floor tax-free maze** — queue at one consolidated counter per store.
2. **Ceramics in carry-on** — bubble wrap at store; else **takkyubin to home** (international shipping rare — carry carefully).
3. **Gachapon binge** — fun but adds ¥500×30 fast.
4. **Fake "outlet" villages** — time sink; skip unless rainy.
5. **Shopping on transit days** — Day 6, 8, 12 are for logistics + food only.
