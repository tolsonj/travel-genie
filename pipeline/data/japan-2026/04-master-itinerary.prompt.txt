# Master Itinerary

**Approximate dates: Friday July 24 – Thursday August 6, 2026**

---

### TOKYO — Jul 24–28 (5 nights)

#### Day 1 — Friday, Jul 24: ARRIVAL (Soft)
- **Morning**: Land **NRT or HND**; VJW QR immigration/customs ([[03-immigration-entry]]); Narita Express / Keisei / Haneda monorail to hotel
- **Afternoon**: Check-in; buy/load **Suica**; convenience-store lunch; short walk in neighborhood (Ueno Park edge or Sumida river if near Asakusa)
- **Evening**: Early ramen or gyudon near hotel; bed by 21:00 (jet lag)
- **Wow moment**: First night skyline—Sumida River or Tokyo Station Marunouchi lit facades
- **Low energy option**: Hotel convenience store dinner only; no walking tour
- **Rainy backup**: Underground passage walk Tokyo Station → Marunouchi + depachika food hall browsing
- **Book NOW**: First 5 Tokyo nights; airport transfer route pinned in Maps

---

#### Day 2 — Saturday, Jul 25: Ueno Nature + Asakusa History
- **Morning anchor (07:30)**: **Ueno Park** — Shinobazu Pond lotus, wooded paths, fewer crowds than Shibuya; optional **Tokyo National Museum** (AC, history without heat)
- **Afternoon**: **Asakusa** — Sensō-ji outer grounds and Nakamise early; shift to indoor **Kappabashi** kitchen street (food culture)
- **Evening**: Dinner **Ueno / Okachimachi** izakaya alley (Hoppy Street) or **Ameyoko** market grazing
- **Wow moment**: Lotus pond reflection at Shinobazu before crowds arrive
- **Low energy option**: Museum-only morning; skip Nakamise shopping
- **Rainy backup**: Full museum day + covered Nakamise arcade
- **Book NOW**: Museum timed ticket if weekend busy

---

#### Day 3 — Sunday, Jul 26: Meiji Forest + Harajuku Light Touch
- **Morning anchor (08:00)**: **Meiji Jingu** forest approach — shaded, nature-first; quiet before tour buses
- **Afternoon**: **Yoyogi Park** edges; coffee in **Omotesando** backstreets (skip Takeshita crush unless brief)
- **Evening**: **Shinjuku** Omoide Yokocho or **Golden Gai** (one small bar, early); avoid peak 22:00 crush
- **Wow moment**: Torii tunnel at Meiji in morning mist after rain
- **Low energy option**: Meiji only; afternoon café and hotel pool/onsen if hotel has bath
- **Rainy backup**: Meiji shrine covered areas + **Tokyo Metropolitan Teien Art Museum** or teamLab Planets (book ahead)

---

#### Day 4 — Monday, Jul 27: Day Trip — Kamakura Coast (Nature > History)
- **Morning anchor (07:30 train)**: JR to **Kamakura** — **Daibutsu (Great Buddha)** walk through wooded approach
- **Afternoon**: **Enoshima** or **Hase** coastal walk (Enoden line scenery); seafood lunch
- **Evening**: Return Tokyo; simple dinner near hotel
- **Wow moment**: Enoden coastal tram view with Fuji on clear days
- **Low energy option**: Kamakura town + Buddha only; skip Enoshima climb
- **Rainy backup**: Swap to **Nikko** Toshogu (more history, forest canopy) OR indoor **Edo-Tokyo Museum**
- **Book NOW**: None mandatory; check Enoden weekend crowds

---

#### Day 5 — Tuesday, Jul 28: Tsukiji Outer + Imperial East Gardens
- **Morning anchor (07:00)**: **Tsukiji Outer Market** — sushi breakfast, tamagoyaki, grilled seafood
- **Afternoon**: **Imperial Palace East Gardens** (free, shaded history walk); **Marunouchi** architecture stroll
- **Evening**: Pack light bag for Takayama; prep **takkyubin** if sending large luggage to Kyoto hotel (ask hotel front desk)
- **Wow moment**: First tuna/sashimi breakfast standing at Tsukiji counter
- **Low energy option**: Market only; skip palace gardens
- **Rainy backup**: **Depachika** food floors Tokyo Station + **Japan Food Town** indoor

---

### TAKAYAMA / JAPANESE ALPS — Jul 29–30 (2 nights)

#### Day 6 — Wednesday, Jul 29: TRANSIT + Takayama Old Town (Soft)
- **Morning**: Shinkansen Tokyo → Nagoya + **Hida Limited Express** to Takayama; **activate JR Pass** if using
- **Afternoon**: Check-in; **Sanmachi Suji** preserved merchant streets (Hida architecture, sake breweries)
- **Evening**: **Hida beef** dinner (reserve)—budget splurge night
- **Wow moment**: First glimpse of cedar-scented old town at dusk
- **Low energy option**: Hotel onsen/rest only after rail day
- **Rainy backup**: Sake brewery tastings + Hida Folk Village (open-air but partial cover)
- **Book NOW**: Takayama lodging with dinner; reserved Hida Express seats

---

#### Day 7 — Thursday, Jul 30: Shirakawa-go + Morning Market
- **Morning anchor (08:00 bus)**: **Shirakawa-go** gassho-zukuri village—first bus = fewest tour groups
- **Afternoon**: Return Takayama; **Miyagawa Morning Market** (if Fri/Sat/Sun overlap—else riverside walk); pack for Kyoto
- **Evening**: Light **soba** or hotel kaiseki-lite; early sleep
- **Wow moment**: Ogimachi viewpoint over thatched roofs before 10:00 crowds
- **Low energy option**: Takayama market + onsen only; skip Shirakawa-go
- **Rainy backup**: **Hida Takayama Museum of History and Art** + covered arcade shopping
- **Book NOW**: Shirakawa-go highway bus round-trip (essential summer)

---

### KYOTO — Jul 31 – Aug 3 (4 nights)

#### Day 8 — Friday, Jul 31: TRANSIT + Nishiki (Soft)
- **Morning**: Hida Express → Nagoya → Shinkansen **Kyoto**; drop bags or receive forwarded luggage
- **Afternoon**: **Nishiki Market** covered food arcade; **Pontocho** alley stroll (reserve no dinner yet)
- **Evening**: Casual **izakaya** near Kawaramachi; early night
- **Wow moment**: First Kyoto canal glimpse at twilight near Pontocho
- **Low energy option**: Market grazing only; hotel bath
- **Rainy backup**: Nishiki fully covered; **Kyoto Railway Museum** indoor

---

#### Day 9 — Saturday, Aug 1: Fushimi Inari + Sake (Early Beat Crowds)
- **Morning anchor (05:30–06:00)**: **Fushimi Inari** — torii tunnel to first overlook before tour buses
- **Afternoon**: **Fushimi sake district** — Gekkeikan Okura Sake Museum or brewery tour (indoor/cool)
- **Evening**: **Gion backstreets** at distance—observe, do not chase geiko; dinner **Pontocho** or **Nishijin** soba
- **Wow moment**: Empty torii corridor in early light
- **Low energy option**: Fushimi base only; return hotel by 11:00
- **Rainy backup**: Inari is walkable in light rain (umbrella); afternoon 100% indoor sake museum
- **Book NOW**: Optional sake tour slot

---

#### Day 10 — Sunday, Aug 2: Arashiyama Bamboo + River (Nature Day)
- **Morning anchor (07:00)**: **Arashiyama** bamboo grove then **Tenryu-ji** garden (enter temple for shade)
- **Afternoon**: **Hozu River** scenery walk or rental cycle on Katsura paths; **monkeys park** optional (steep climb—skip if low energy)
- **Evening**: **Obanzai** home-style Kyoto dinner (Warajiya or similar—reserve)
- **Wow moment**: Bamboo grove when still quiet; river mountain backdrop
- **Low energy option**: Tenryu-ji garden only; tram back to central Kyoto
- **Rainy backup**: **Sagano Romantic Train** (book ahead) or **Kyoto International Manga Museum** + café

---

#### Day 11 — Monday, Aug 3: Higashiyama Dawn + Philosopher’s Path
- **Morning anchor (06:30)**: **Kiyomizu-dera** early entry; descend **Ninenzaka/Sannenzaka** before shops fully open
- **Afternoon**: **Philosopher’s Path** shaded canal walk → **Ginkaku-ji** (Silver Pavilion gardens)
- **Evening**: Pack for Osaka; **Namba-bound** snack prep; try **yudofu** or tofu kaiseki (budget pick one)
- **Wow moment**: Kiyomizu stage view over Kyoto basin in morning air
- **Low energy option**: Philosopher’s Path flat walk only; skip Kiyomizu climb
- **Rainy backup**: **Nanzen-ji** sub-temples with covered corridors + **Kyoto National Museum**

---

### OSAKA — Aug 4–5 (2 nights)

#### Day 12 — Tuesday, Aug 4: TRANSIT + Dotonbori Food (Soft)
- **Morning**: JR **Kyoto → Osaka** (~30 min); hotel drop bags
- **Afternoon**: **Kuromon Ichiba Market** — takoyaki, kushikatsu samples, fruit
- **Evening**: **Dotonbori** neon food crawl—okonomiyaki (**Mizuno** or **Chibo**), gyoza alley; stay weekday to reduce crush vs weekend
- **Wow moment**: Glico sign reflection on canal after dark
- **Low energy option**: Kuromon only; convenience-store dinner
- **Rainy backup**: **Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan** (indoor, world-class) or **Umeda Sky Building** if rain clears at sunset

---

#### Day 13 — Wednesday, Aug 5: Castle Exterior + Namba Gems
- **Morning anchor (08:00)**: **Osaka Castle Park** grounds and moat walk—skip interior museum if crowds/heat; free exterior history fix
- **Afternoon**: **Namba Yasaka Shrine** (lion head stage) + **Amerikamura** café break; optional **Shinsekai** kushikatsu (late lunch)
- **Evening**: Final splurge **kushikatsu** or **yakiniku**; confirm **Haruka** or Nankai KIX timing
- **Wow moment**: Castle ramparts view from Nishinomaru Garden
- **Low energy option**: Namba neighborhood food only; skip castle
- **Rainy backup**: **TeamLab Osaka** (if open) or **Kaiyukan** full afternoon

---

#### Day 14 — Thursday, Aug 6: DEPARTURE
- **Morning**: Checkout; **takkyubin** should have preceded you or carry-on only
- **Midday**: **Nankai Rapi:t** or **Haruka** to **KIX** — allow **2.5–3 hrs** before international departure
- **Afternoon/Evening**: Flight **KIX → US** (open-jaw completion)
- **Wow moment**: Last convenience-store onigiri at gate—trip bookend
- **Low energy option**: Airport lounge if access; minimal morning activity
- **Rainy backup**: N/A—indoor airport path
- **Book NOW**: Confirm KIX transfer; online check-in 24 hrs prior
