### Optional Swap Adventures

#### Kamikochi Alpine Valley — Swap for Day 7 OR extra Takayama day

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Why** | Premier **#1 nature** hit — Azusa River, Hotaka peaks views |
| **Access** | Bus from Matsumoto or Takayama area (~2.5–3 hr each way from Takayama — **long day**) |
| **Better access** | Stay Takayama; tour bus **Jul 30** early — replaces Shirakawa-go if already seen OR skip market |
| **Trails** | **Kappa Bridge loop** 1.5 hr easy; **Myojin Pond** 2–3 hr moderate flat |
| **Elevation** | ~1,500 m base — **cooler** than cities |
| **Season** | Open late Apr–Nov; **Jul 30 OK** |
| **Cost** | Bus + ¥700 conservation fee |
| **Fitness** | Moderate; no climbing required for valley floor |
| **Book** | Alpico bus seats summer peak |

**Recommendation**: If Shirakawa-go feels too touristy, swap to **Kamikochi** — stronger nature payoff for your interest stack.

#### Mt Takao — Swap for Day 4 (Tokyo) instead of Kamakura

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Why** | Forested mountain **45 min from Shinjuku**; cooler elevation |
| **Access** | Keio line to Takaosanguchi; cablecar or trail 1 |
| **Trails** | Trail 1 (paved, 90 min up); **Trail 6** watercourse — more nature |
| **Elevation** | 599 m summit |
| **Duration** | Half to full day |
| **Difficulty** | Moderate — your fitness handles easily |
| **Crowds** | Weekday better; Jul 27 Mon fits itinerary if Kamakura done elsewhere |
| **Gear** | Trail shoes; insect repellent |

**Tradeoff**: Kamakura = history + coast; Takao = pure forest hike — pick based on weather (rain → Takao forest still OK; Kamakura rain → Edo-Tokyo Museum).

### Safety & Season

- **Heat exhaustion**: Rest 11:00–15:00 indoor on Aug 1–3 Kyoto hikes.
- **Thunderstorms**: Alps afternoon — descend Kamikochi by 14:00 if cells build.
- **Bears**: Kamikochi — rare on main trails; bell not required on crowded paths.
- **Solo hiking**: Japan trail culture safe; tell hotel front desk if solo alpine bus day.
