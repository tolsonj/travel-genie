### Solo Dining Customs

- Counter seats = **1 person welcome**; say **ひとりです** (hitori desu).
- No tipping; pay at register or table tablet.
- **Smoking**: Many izakaya have smoking sections — ask **禁煙** (kin-en) if sensitive.
- **Water**: Free oolong/hot water common; iced water less automatic — ask.
- **Meal timing**: Lunch 11:30–13:30; dinner 18:00–21:00; kitchens close early in Alps.

### Food Safety

- Tap water safe in cities; bottled if preferred.
- Summer fish: eat at high-turnover counters (Tsukiji, Kuromon).
- **Heat**: Carry electrolyte drinks (konbini); avoid unrefrigerated mayo salads on street.
