# Etiquette

### Daily Norms (Everywhere)

| Situation | Do | Don't |
|-----------|-----|-------|
| **Greeting** | Small bow or nod; **arigatou gozaimasu** | Loud handshake default |
| **Shoes** | Remove when tatami / some ryokan/inns | Walk on tatami with outdoor shoes |
| **Trash** | Pocket trash; konbini bins | Eat walking + litter |
| **Volume** | Quiet on trains, temples | Phone calls on Shinkansen/local train |
| **Tipping** | Never | Leave coins — staff may chase you |
| **Queues** | Single file; wait marked lines | Cut |
| **Photos** | Ask at shrines/shops if unsure | Flash in dark halls; drone without permit |
| **Smoking** | Use designated zones | Walk-smoke (illegal many wards) |
| **Cash** | Carry ¥5,000–10,000 | Assume every stall takes card |
| **Hot weather** | Sleeve in temples (light scarf) | Shirtless in public |

### Regional Notes

#### Tokyo

- Fast-paced; stand **left on escalators** (Tokyo) — **right in Osaka/Kyoto** (verify signs — follow locals).
- **Trash**: Few bins — carry out.
- **Akihabara**: Ask before photographing maids/cosplayers.

#### Takayama / Japanese Alps

- **Slower pace**; greet shopkeepers entering/leaving stores.
- **Morning market**: Don't block stalls with large bag.
- **Shirakawa-go**: Stay on paths; private homes are not museums.

#### Kyoto

- **Most formal** — softer voice in residential lanes (Ninenzaka, Gion).
- **Geiko/Maiko**: No touching, no blocking; photography often prohibited in alleys.
- **Rickshaw**: Negotiate route/time; not required for short hills.

#### Osaka

- **Casual, jokey culture** — OK to chat with counter chefs.
- **Comedy loudness** in Dotonbori — still no litter.
- **Kuidaore** ("eat till you drop") — sample sizes, not waste.

### What Will Surprise You (Good)

- Staff bow when you leave convenience store.
- Exact change trays at registers — don't hand cash directly if tray present.
- Vending machines everywhere — reliable.
- Police helpful for lost items — report at **koban** if needed.

### What Will Surprise You (Avoid)

- Assuming English everywhere — patience + translate app.
- Talking on elevator — quiet box culture.
- Opening taxi doors — driver auto-opens rear left.
