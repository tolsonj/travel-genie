# Hidden Gems

### How to Use This List

- Pick **one gem per city** max — do not stack off-itinerary detours.
- Best inserted when **low energy** or **rainy backup** slots open.
- Solo-friendly = counter food, public baths, neighborhood walks.

---

### TOKYO — Local Favorites

| Gem | Area | Why | Swap for trap |
|-----|------|-----|---------------|
| **Yanaka Ginza** | Yanaka | Showa-era shopping street, cats, old Tokyo calm | Nakamise overbuying |
| **Nezu Shrine azalea paths** | Nezu | Small shrine, tunnel torii — quieter than Inari preview | — |
| **Kagurazaka** | Shinjuku edge | French-Japanese alley, stone steps, evening solo dining | Golden Gai peak 22:00 |
| **Todoroki Valley** | Setagaya | 1 km gorge **20 min from Shibuya** — nature hidden | Overhyped Shibuya crossing linger |
| **Sumida Hokusai Museum** | Ryogoku | Small focused museum + river walk | Skytree mall |
| **Jimbocho book district** | Kanda | Used books, curry shops | Akihabara maid cafes |

**Itinerary hook**: Day 2 afternoon if Sensō-ji crowded → metro to **Nezu Shrine** (15 min). Day 5 post-Tsukiji → **Hokusai Museum** + river stroll.

**Trap avoid**: Robot Restaurant closed/long dead; themed cafés with 2 hr queues.

### TAKAYAMA / ALPS

| Gem | Why |
|-----|-----|
| **Shiroyama Park viewpoint** | Sunset over old town; 10 min climb; locals jog here |
| **Miyagawa morning market** (local side) | Buy pickles from farmers, not gift stalls |
| **Funamachi canal lane** | Evening walk; fireflies rare Jul |
| **Hida no Sato** (if Shirakawa-go sold out) | Open-air museum closer; fewer tour buses than Ogimachi peak |

**Trap avoid**: Overpriced "Hida beef" snack sticks everywhere — save budget for sit-down Jul 29.

---

### KYOTO — Beyond the Postcard

| Gem | Why | Best time |
|-----|-----|-----------|
| **Daimonji-yama (Nigatsudo)** | Free city view near Todai-ji equivalent hillside — **Kiyomizu area backup** | Day 11 if energy |
| **Honen-in Temple** | Moss entrance, no crowd; near Philosopher's Path start | Day 11 add-on |
| **Sennyu-ji** | Imperial burial temple; forest silence | Early Aug morning |
| **Nishijin textile district** | Working weavers, not rental kimono shops | Day 8–9 spare hour |
| **Ippodo Tea Kyoto** | Tasting counter; AC | Heat midday |
| **Kawai Kanjiro's House** | Craftsman home museum; intimate | Rainy 1 hr |

**Trap avoid**: Bamboo grove midday; **Arashiyama** north of river — **Adashino Nenbutsu-ji** moss garden instead.

**Gion trap**: Main Hanamikoji — use **Shirakawa canal** north Gion at dusk (observe quietly).

---

### OSAKA — Namba Depth

| Gem | Why |
|-----|-----|
| **Horie / Orange Street** | Design boutiques, cafes; local creative scene |
| **Kuromon back lanes** | Eat where staff eat — side streets off main arcade |
| **Nakazaki-cho** | Retro cafes near Umeda; morning coffee |
| **Hozenji Yokocho** | Moss-covered Buddha alley; lantern vibe near Dotonbori but calmer |
| **Tenma** | Izakaya district where salarymen drink — solo counter OK |

**Trap avoid**: Dotonbori chain-only crab billboards — eat at **Mizuno** or back-street kushikatsu instead.
