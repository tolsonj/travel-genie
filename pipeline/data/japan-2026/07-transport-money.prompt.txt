### Money Management

#### Currency

| Item | Value (planning) |
|------|------------------|
| Rate | **¥145–150 = $1 USD** (verify at departure) |
| Quick math | ¥1,000 ≈ $7; ¥10,000 ≈ $69 |

#### Cash vs card

| Use | % | Examples |
|-----|---|----------|
| **Cash** | ~60% | Markets, temples, small izakaya, buses, takkyubin |
| **IC card** | ~25% | Trains/subway (preloaded) |
| **Credit card** | ~15% | Hotels (prepaid), department stores, some chains |

**Daily cash envelope**: ¥8,000–12,000 ($55–80) covers food + incidentals; adjust down on konbini-heavy days.

#### ATMs (foreign cards)

| ✅ Use | ❌ Avoid |
|--------|----------|
| **7-Eleven** ATMs (English, 24h) | Random bank ATMs (often reject foreign cards) |
| **Japan Post Bank** (inside post offices) | DCC—always withdraw in **JPY**, not USD |

**Fees**: Japan ATM ¥110–220; use a **no-FTF debit** (Charles Schwab, Capital One 360, Fidelity CMA).

**Withdrawal schedule**:

| When | Where | Amount |
|------|-------|--------|
| Day 1 | Airport 7-Eleven | ¥40,000 |
| Day 7 | Takayama or Kyoto Station | ¥30,000 |
| Day 11 | Kyoto if low | ¥20,000 |

#### Credit cards

- **0% foreign transaction fee**: Chase Sapphire, Capital One Venture, etc.
- **Acceptance**: Hotels/chains yes; many dinner-only spots **cash only**.
- Set **PIN** before departure (chip+PIN common).
- Pay in **JPY** at terminal—decline dynamic currency conversion.

#### Tipping

**No tipping** in Japan (restaurants, taxis, hotels). See [[13-etiquette]] when written.

#### Budget tracking

- App: Trail Wallet or Notes—log daily ¥ spend.
- **Transport target**: **~$400–450** on-ground (excl. intl flights) ≈ **8–9%** of $5,000 ✅
